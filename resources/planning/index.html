Furnishing Budgets and Room Planning

Build an itemized furnishing plan, measure the property, and separate purchase costs from delivery, installation, and reserves.

Updated October 6, 2026

Start with the decisions that determine every purchase: what the property needs, who will use each room, how items will get inside, and how much cash remains after installation. These worksheets help turn a collection of attractive products into a coherent scope.

Work through the decisions in order

  1. Walk the property and record what can remain before setting shopping allowances.
  2. Measure rooms and delivery routes; write down the uses each room must support.
  3. Price essential items with delivery and installation, then reserve funds before choosing optional upgrades.

Start with the earliest unresolved decision. Use the examples as worksheets, then replace their assumptions with measurements, written product specifications, and local operating information.
