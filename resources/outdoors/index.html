Weather, Outdoor Spaces and Seasonal Storage

Plan exposure-aware outdoor furniture, drying and storage routines, shade, salt care, and seasonal furnishing handoffs.

Updated October 6, 2026

Outdoor furnishings interact with sun, rain, wind, salt, dirt, and the storage space available between stays. Begin with the site conditions and product instructions. These guides turn those observations into a practical furnishing and reset plan without treating any material as weatherproof.

Work through the decisions in order

  1. Observe exposure at the times guests are likely to use the space.
  2. Check product suitability and where loose items can be stored or dried.
  3. Write a local reset and seasonal handoff before installing furnishings.

Start with the earliest unresolved decision. Use the examples as worksheets, then replace their assumptions with measurements, written product specifications, and local operating information.
