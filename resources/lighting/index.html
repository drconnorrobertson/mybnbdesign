Lighting Plans and Window Treatments

Specify consistent bulbs, useful controls, glare management, nighttime routes, and window coverage for a rental.

Updated October 6, 2026

Lighting succeeds when a new guest can immediately see where to go and how to make a room comfortable. Start with activities and controls, then select fixtures. The collection addresses practical specification and testing rather than promising a universal brightness formula.

Work through the decisions in order

  1. Visit during daylight and after dark to record the actual light conditions.
  2. Separate arrival, task, relaxation, and cleaning needs on the room plan.
  3. Test fixtures, controls, window coverage, and replacement bulbs before photography.

Start with the earliest unresolved decision. Use the examples as worksheets, then replace their assumptions with measurements, written product specifications, and local operating information.
