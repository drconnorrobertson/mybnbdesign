Use the inventory as a purchasing and reset record

Complete a separate sheet for each room. The lists below are prompts rather than a requirement to buy every item. Record the planned guest use, the quantity actually needed, the retained quantity, the quantity to order, and the place each item belongs. A useful inventory also identifies the exact size or model, its care instructions, its delivery status, and who checks it at handover.

Start with the furnished capacity and the real operating plan. Count dining places with chairs in use, beds with their actual linen sizes, and storage with luggage present. Do not infer permitted occupancy from the number of chairs or beds. Check relevant property requirements separately. Where a supplied appliance or activity requires technical review, keep that approval outside the shopping checklist and record who is responsible.

Before ordering, mark each line as retain, repair, buy, optional, or not applicable. Split sets into their replaceable components: a lost pillowcase does not necessarily require a new sheet set, and a broken mug does not require another full dinner service. Include freight, assembly, receiving, disposal, and spare quantities in the purchasing sheet rather than treating the product price as the whole cost.

Living room and dining inventory

Trial the full guest group before approving a layout. Include seating with feet on the floor, chairs pulled out, and a sofa bed open if supplied. Leave controls, cleaning access, doors, and usable travel routes in view. The inventory is complete when the items fit and can be reset, not just when the purchase list has been ticked.

Category: Seating
Items to check: Sofa; armchairs; any supplied sofa bed; protective covers where appropriate
Specification or reset question: Usable seats, open-bed footprint, upholstery care, replaceable components

Category: Tables
Items to check: Coffee or side tables; dining table; dining chairs
Specification or reset question: Stable condition, chair pullout, drink placement, cleaning access

Category: Lighting
Items to check: Fixed lights; reading/task lamps; compatible bulbs and recorded spares
Specification or reset question: Controls reachable from actual seats; glare and nighttime route tested

Category: Entertainment
Items to check: Television where supplied; remote; operating instructions; documented cables
Specification or reset question: Guest setup accurately described; no owner accounts or private paperwork exposed

Category: Storage
Items to check: Luggage landing; coat/shoe storage; book or game storage if supplied
Specification or reset question: Storage does not block movement; contents can be counted and reset

Category: Finishing items
Items to check: Rug if appropriate; wall art; selected washable throws or cushions
Specification or reset question: Exact care method, secure installation review, manageable decoration quantity

Category: Dining reset
Items to check: Placemats or table protection where supplied; serving pieces; spare chair parts if appropriate
Specification or reset question: Table protection follows finish instructions and is easy to reproduce

Kitchen inventory by the meals you actually support

Write a simple meal trial before choosing quantities: prepare breakfast, assemble a basic cooked meal, and clear up afterward. Check the equipment provided with that trial and the property’s guest capacity. Choose compatible cookware from the exact appliance instructions. Do not add a device just to lengthen the amenity list; each supplied device needs storage, operating instructions, cleaning, and a reset check.

Inventory prompts: Frying pan; saucepan; larger pot; lids; baking tray; oven dish where relevant
Count or compatibility check: Compatible with supplied cooktop/oven; capacity matches the intended meal

Category: Preparation
Inventory prompts: Cutting boards; suitable knives; measuring tools; mixing bowl; colander
Count or compatibility check: Stable, cleanable storage; components counted separately

Category: Utensils
Inventory prompts: Spatula; stirring spoon; ladle; tongs; whisk; peeler; opener
Count or compatibility check: Tools fit the actual cookware and supported cooking tasks

Category: Dishware
Inventory prompts: Dinner plates; side plates; bowls; mugs
Count or compatibility check: Usable quantity for the guest allocation and laundry/dishwashing process

Category: Glassware and cutlery
Inventory prompts: Drinking glasses; forks; knives; spoons; teaspoons
Count or compatibility check: Replaceable model or size recorded; no chipped or unsuitable pieces counted

Category: Serving and storage
Inventory prompts: Serving bowl; platter where supplied; food-storage containers and lids
Count or compatibility check: Pair lids with containers; confirm safe intended use from product instructions

Category: Appliances
Inventory prompts: Refrigerator; cooktop/oven; coffee setup; kettle; toaster; other actual supplied appliances
Count or compatibility check: Installed and commissioned; manual and guest instructions available

Bedroom and guest-bedroom inventory

Use one record per sleeping position and bed size, including a separate record for a convertible bed. Check the mattress, frame, protector, sheet depth, and cover together before buying multiples. Optional pillow choices need clean storage and care instructions. Furniture marketed for children, bunks, or infant use needs its own exact product and qualified suitability review; a room list is not a safety approval.

Category: Bed system
Inventory prompts: Bed/frame; mattress; exact fitted protector; fitted/flat sheets as used; pillowcases; cover or blanket
Functional check: Dimensions and making method tested as one system

Category: Linen stock
Inventory prompts: Installed sets; sets in laundry; working stock; separately justified spare components
Functional check: Quantity follows the actual changeover and laundry-return cycle

Category: Pillows
Inventory prompts: Installed pillows; optional alternatives if supplied; appropriate protectors/cases
Functional check: Care labels, identifying labels, and clean dry storage documented

Category: Bedside use
Inventory prompts: Suitable bedside surface; reading light; reachable power; personal-item landing
Functional check: Test from the actual bed position; avoid obstructing doors or curtains

Category: Storage
Inventory prompts: Luggage support; clothes storage; usable hangers; any supplied laundry bag
Functional check: Capacity and placement match the room rather than unused floor area

Category: Window and climate
Inventory prompts: Window treatment; operating instructions; approved control labels
Functional check: Light gaps and control access checked in actual nighttime conditions

Category: Guest bedroom
Inventory prompts: A separately counted complete bed/linen system for every supplied bed
Functional check: Do not assume a spare room can share stock or instructions with the main bedroom

Bathroom, outdoor, utility and connectivity inventory

Inspect bathroom contents after a realistic guest-use trial, including towel drying and placement of personal toiletries. Outdoors, distinguish permanently installed items from loose pieces that require a weather handoff. For supplies stored in an owner cupboard, separate guest access from the cleaner’s stock and record the approved storage arrangement. Keep property-specific emergency equipment and inspection requirements in a qualified checklist rather than inventing a universal shopping quantity.

Area: Bathroom linens
Inventory prompts: Bath towels; hand towels; washcloths where supplied; bath mat; working linen stock
Handover check: Actual allocation, laundry cycle, dry storage, and towel-drying locations

Area: Bathroom accessories
Inventory prompts: Hooks/rails; mirror; waste bin; toilet-paper holder; cleaner-approved dispensers; personal-item surface
Handover check: Reach, mounting review, cleanability, and replenishment owner

Area: Bathroom extras
Inventory prompts: Hair dryer or other listed amenity if supplied; its manual and storage
Handover check: Exact instructions available; damaged equipment excluded from reset

Area: Outdoor activities
Inventory prompts: Suitable table/chairs or loungers; approved shade; cushion covers; storage
Handover check: Exposure suitability, dry storage, weather limits, and loose-item count

Area: Utility and laundry
Inventory prompts: Washer/dryer if supplied; manuals; iron/board if supplied; baskets; permitted care supplies
Handover check: Installed process, machine capacity, drying, and access instructions

Area: Cleaning support
Inventory prompts: Approved cleaning tools; vacuum if supplied; spare bags/parts; waste supplies
Handover check: Owner and guest responsibilities distinguished; product labels retained

Area: Connectivity
Inventory prompts: Actual Wi-Fi/network instructions; supplied charging setup; guest-use technology instructions
Handover check: Test connection and controls; keep private account or access details out of public worksheets

Area: Safety review
Inventory prompts: Property-specific installed safety equipment, inspection records, and responsible contacts
Handover check: Confirm relevant requirements and operating instructions with qualified providers

Order, receive and maintain the complete list

A higher product price does not establish longer service life, and a cheaper product may become expensive after assembly or repeated replacement. Compare exact specifications and complete installed costs. Keep a contingency separate from optional decoration, and record any substitution before purchase. At receipt, reconcile counts, dimensions, damage, and missing parts against the approved line rather than relying on a delivery photograph alone.

- Measure retained items and exclude unusable stock before setting order quantities.

- Record actual quotes, stock confirmation, returns terms, delivery scope, and receiving responsibilities.

- Approve substitutions against room fit, care, finish, function, cost, and delivery constraints.

- Photograph received condition, match shipment identifiers, and follow the vendor’s actual discrepancy process.

- Complete the room reset trial, label spare components, and file warranty/care information at handover.

- Reconcile the inventory after loss, repair, or replacement so the next order reflects the current property.
