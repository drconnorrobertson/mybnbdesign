Procurement, Installation and Rental Launch

Coordinate vendor quotes, substitutions, deliveries, installation, photography, and a documented furnishing handover.

Updated October 6, 2026

A furnishing project needs decision owners as much as it needs products. These guides help organize purchasing, receiving, installation, testing, and handover so unresolved details are visible before guests arrive. The sample schedules and allocations are planning examples, not promised delivery times.

Work through the decisions in order

  1. Agree on scope, approval owners, dependencies, and substitution rules.
  2. Track orders and prepare the receiving and installation team before dispatch.
  3. Close functional issues, test the guest journey, and hand over an accurate inventory.

Start with the earliest unresolved decision. Use the examples as worksheets, then replace their assumptions with measurements, written product specifications, and local operating information.
