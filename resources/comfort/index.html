Sleep and Everyday Guest Comfort

Plan bedding fit, pillow storage, acoustic comfort, charging access, climate controls, and clear guest instructions.

Updated October 6, 2026

Comfort comes from details that remain useful after the listing photo is taken. These guides help coordinate beds, textiles, storage, controls, and instructions with the turnover team. Treat trial stays and direct observations as feedback; individual guests will have different preferences.

Work through the decisions in order

  1. Build the bed and test it with the linens, protector, and base together.
  2. Try the room at night with the lights off and luggage in place.
  3. Document the reset so comfort survives cleaning, laundry, and replacement cycles.

Start with the earliest unresolved decision. Use the examples as worksheets, then replace their assumptions with measurements, written product specifications, and local operating information.
