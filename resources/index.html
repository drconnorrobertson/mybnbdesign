Project execution playbooks

Use these worksheets to organize receiving, approvals, installation and handover.

Furniture damage claims: a rental receiving workflow

Receiving furniture is an inspection task, not simply a signature at the door. Build a record that connects the order, carton, product and damage while the seller’s reporting window is still open.

How to approve furniture substitutions without losing the plan

An out-of-stock item should trigger a short fit and scope review. A visually similar replacement may have different dimensions, care requirements or delivery timing. Use an approval record before the substitute is ordered.

The measurement handoff for a remote rental designer

Remote design starts with trustworthy site information. A room’s width and length are only part of the brief: openings, obstructions and the delivery route determine whether the finished plan can be installed.

A room-by-room punch list for rental furnishing installation

Installation is complete when the property is ready for its intended use, not when the truck leaves. Keep a punch list that records unresolved fit, condition, assembly and inventory issues with a responsible person and a close date.

Build a useful spare-parts inventory for rental furniture

Spare inventory should respond to real replacement needs and storage capacity. Start with items that are vulnerable, frequently used or difficult to replace quickly. A large box of unidentified hardware is not a maintenance system.

Compare rental furnishing quotes using the same scope

Two proposals with the same headline label can assign very different responsibilities. Normalize the scope before comparing totals, and separate confirmed inclusions from assumptions that still require an answer.

A photo-readiness checklist after furnishing a rental

A room is ready for photography when the visible setup matches what guests will find. Use a readiness check to coordinate the installer, cleaner, owner and photographer before everyone arrives on site.

What belongs in a rental furnishing project handover

The handover connects design decisions to everyday operation. A host needs to know what was installed, how to care for it and who resolves unfinished work. Collect those records before the project team disperses.
