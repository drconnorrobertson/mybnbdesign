An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

Key Differences Explained

Pinterest is an incredible source of design inspiration, and it is easy to understand why so many property owners start their STR design journey there. The platform is filled with beautifully photographed rooms, curated style boards, and aspirational interiors that make design seem accessible and achievable.

The challenge is the gap between Pinterest inspiration and real-world execution. The spaces featured on Pinterest are typically designed by professionals, photographed by professionals, and styled with products that may cost significantly more than the budget-friendly alternatives a DIY approach requires. Attempting to recreate a $50,000 professional interior with $5,000 in retail purchases inevitably produces a result that falls short of the inspiration.

DIY design also consumes enormous amounts of time. Hours spent browsing Pinterest, comparing furniture options online, visiting retail stores, coordinating deliveries, assembling flat-pack furniture, and styling rooms add up to weeks or months of effort. For property owners with day jobs, families, or multiple properties, this time investment represents a significant opportunity cost, especially when every day without guests is lost revenue.

MyBnBDesign delivers the Pinterest-worthy result without the DIY effort. Our designers stay current on design trends, source from professional trade channels at designer pricing, and execute projects efficiently because design is what we do every day. The property owner invests once, receives a professionally designed and installed interior, and starts earning revenue immediately rather than spending weekends at furniture stores and assembling IKEA bookshelves.

Pinterest DIY offers self-directed diy design using pinterest as inspiration. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
