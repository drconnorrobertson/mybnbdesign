MyBnBDesign vs Frontdesk Design

An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Frontdesk Design across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Frontdesk Design

Frontdesk is a short-term rental management company operating 1,000+ units across 30+ US markets. They have internally developed their design, furnishing, and cleaning processes with W2 employees. Their Frontdesk Flex platform offers solutions ranging from SaaS tools to full-service turnkey management for multifamily properties.

About MyBnBDesign

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. We provide end-to-end design solutions, from initial concept and space planning through furniture procurement at designer pricing, professional installation, styling, and listing photography direction. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Frontdesk has built an impressive vertically-integrated operation, managing everything from design and furnishing through guest services and cleaning with W2 employees. This level of operational control ensures consistency across their portfolio of 1,000+ units and provides property owners with a genuinely hands-off management experience.

The primary limitation for property owners considering Frontdesk is the all-or-nothing nature of their service. Design and furnishing capabilities are embedded within their property management offering and cannot be accessed independently. For owners who want professional design without surrendering operational control, Frontdesk's bundled model requires more commitment than necessary.

Frontdesk's focus on multifamily and urban properties also means their design aesthetic is optimized for apartment-style units. Properties that do not fit this profile, including standalone homes, unique architectural properties, or rural and resort destinations, may not benefit from their standardized design approach.

MyBnBDesign offers the same professional design and furnishing expertise as Frontdesk's internal team, but as a standalone service accessible to any property owner in any market. Whether you self-manage, use a local co-host, or work with any property manager of your choosing, our design service stands on its own merit. You get professional, revenue-optimized design without the commitment of a full management contract.

The Bottom Line

Frontdesk Design offers in-house design and furnishing as part of property management. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
