MyBnBDesign vs Local Interior Designer

Why STR-specialized design outperforms traditional interior design for vacation rentals.

Not All Interior Design Is the Same

Hiring a local interior designer might seem like a natural choice when furnishing a vacation rental. After all, they are professionals with design training and experience. However, there is a critical difference between designing a home someone lives in and designing a property that needs to generate revenue as a short-term rental. Traditional interior designers optimize for the homeowner taste. STR designers optimize for guest experience and booking performance.

Where Traditional Designers Fall Short for STRs

Most local interior designers have built their careers around residential or commercial projects. They excel at creating beautiful spaces that reflect a client personal style. But short-term rentals have entirely different requirements. A vacation rental needs to photograph exceptionally well for online listings. It needs to appeal to a broad range of guests rather than one homeowner preferences. It needs durable materials that withstand high turnover. It needs functional layouts optimized for guest flow. And it needs to be designed with operational efficiency in mind.

Traditional designers often specify custom pieces with long lead times, fragile materials that cannot handle vacation rental wear, and highly personalized aesthetics that may not appeal to a broad guest demographic. They also typically charge by the hour with open-ended timelines, making budgets unpredictable.

The MyBnBDesign Advantage
