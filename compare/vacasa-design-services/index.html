An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Vacasa Design Services across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Vacasa Design Services

Vacasa Interior Design launched in partnership with Wayfair Professional, offering three service tiers: Guided ($199), Curated ($499-$899), and Full-Furnish ($599-$1,119). Their pilot program showed an average 12% occupancy increase, with some owners seeing 20%+ revenue boosts. Vacasa is one of the largest vacation rental management companies in North America.

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Vacasa's Interior Design program, developed in partnership with Wayfair Professional, brings the scale and data of one of the largest vacation rental management companies to the design process. Their tiered pricing model makes design accessible at various price points, from $199 for guided recommendations up to $1,119 for full-furnish service.

However, the tiered model creates significant gaps in service delivery. The entry-level Guided tier provides design ideas and two weeks of designer collaboration, essentially a mood board and shopping list. The Curated tier adds room-specific plans and shipping coordination. Even the Full-Furnish tier, while more comprehensive, is limited in scope compared to a fully custom design service that accounts for your specific property's architecture, natural light, and market positioning.

The Wayfair Professional partnership means furnishing selections are primarily sourced from Wayfair's catalog. While Wayfair offers extensive options, this single-source approach limits the ability to create truly unique, differentiated interiors that stand out in competitive markets. Top-performing listings often feature a curated mix from multiple sources, including local artisans, vintage finds, and specialty vendors.

MyBnBDesign's approach is fundamentally different. Rather than offering tiered compromises, every project receives full-service attention: custom design concepts, multi-source procurement at designer pricing, professional installation, and styling. The result is a 40% average revenue increase, substantially outperforming Vacasa's reported 12% occupancy boost.

The Bottom Line

Vacasa Design Services offers design program tied to vacasa property management platform. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
