MyBnBDesign vs Wayfair Furniture Package

Why curated design packages outperform catalog shopping for your Airbnb furnishing needs.

The Wayfair Approach to Furnishing

Wayfair offers an enormous selection of furniture and home goods at competitive prices, making it a popular starting point for Airbnb hosts looking to furnish their properties. Some hosts attempt to create their own furniture packages by shopping Wayfair room-by-room, selecting pieces that seem to match and hoping the final result comes together. While Wayfair offers great products at accessible prices, the platform was not designed for short-term rental furnishing, and the DIY package approach often falls short.

Common Pitfalls of Self-Built Wayfair Packages

The biggest challenge with building your own furniture package from Wayfair is design cohesion. Without professional guidance, hosts frequently end up with pieces that looked great individually online but clash when placed together in a room. Scale issues are common since product photos can be misleading without seeing items in person. Color matching across different brands and product lines rarely works as expected on screen. And coordinating delivery timelines across dozens of individual orders from different vendors creates logistical headaches that eat into your timeline and patience.

Product quality is another concern. Wayfair hosts products from thousands of vendors at wildly varying quality levels. Without experience sourcing for high-turnover environments, hosts often select pieces that look attractive but cannot withstand the demands of vacation rental use. Replacing worn-out furniture every year eliminates any savings from the initial purchase price.
