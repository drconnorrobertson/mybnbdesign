MyBnBDesign vs Minoan

Comparing full-service STR design with experience-based furnishing for your vacation rental.

Understanding the Two Models

Minoan and MyBnBDesign both serve short-term rental hosts, but their business models differ significantly. Minoan operates as a product experience platform, placing branded products in vacation rentals so guests can try them and purchase directly. MyBnBDesign is a dedicated interior design service that creates cohesive, revenue-optimized spaces for short-term rental properties.

How Minoan Works

Minoan partners with consumer brands to place their products in vacation rentals. Hosts receive free or discounted products in exchange for displaying them and facilitating guest purchases. The model is interesting for hosts looking to offset furnishing costs, but it introduces a retail element into the guest experience that may not align with every hosting strategy. Product selection is driven by brand partnerships rather than design cohesion.

How MyBnBDesign Works

MyBnBDesign starts with your property and your revenue goals. We create a custom design concept, select every piece of furniture and decor to create a cohesive aesthetic, handle all procurement and logistics, and install everything so your property is guest-ready. Every design decision is driven by what will generate the best return for your specific property and market.

Which Is Right for You?

Minoan can be an interesting way to reduce furnishing costs if you are comfortable with a retail-driven model. However, if your priority is maximizing rental revenue through professional design, MyBnBDesign delivers a purpose-built solution. Our designs are created to drive higher nightly rates, better reviews, and stronger occupancy. The properties we design are optimized for hospitality performance, not product sales.
