Furniture package vs custom furnishing plan

Compare a predetermined bundle with a room-by-room plan built around your property. Evaluate the exact included inventory and measurements rather than the number of pieces advertised.

What changes between the options?

Compare the practical tradeoffs
DecisionFurniture packageCustom furnishing plan
FitRequires checking every major item against actual room and delivery dimensions.Can select sizes and quantities around circulation and guest capacity.
OrderingMay simplify selection but still requires a written delivery and substitution policy.Can involve more vendors and delivery coordination.
CompletenessMay exclude lighting, kitchen inventory, linens or outdoor pieces.Can cover those categories when they are included in the scope.

A property scenario

A package contains a dining table for four while the listing is planned for six guests. Adding two chairs does not solve inadequate table space. Compare complete seating, sleeping and storage needs before calculating a package saving.

Questions to resolve before committing

  • Can items be removed or resized, and how does that affect price?
  • Which categories are excluded from the package?
  • Who approves substitutions and verifies dimensions?

Compare the complete project cost

Put both options against the same rooms, guest capacity and completion date. List product costs, design fees, shipping, receiving, assembly, installation and any repeat visits separately. Assign a person to each task and document exclusions. A lower quoted fee is only comparable when the responsibilities and deliverables match.

Apply this to your MyBnBDesign proposal

Review our service overview, then ask for a written scope for your actual property. Confirm which tasks are included, who handles site access and how approvals work before ordering. The distinctions above describe project approaches; they do not promise that every provider offers every task.
