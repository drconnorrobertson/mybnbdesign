MyBnBDesign vs Target Budget Design

Can Target home furnishings compete with professional design for your Airbnb?

Target as an Airbnb Furnishing Source

Target has made significant strides in home decor with collaborations from respected designers and an expanding selection of stylish, affordable furnishings. Their in-house brands like Threshold, Hearth and Hand, and Project 62 offer on-trend aesthetics at accessible price points. For budget-conscious hosts, Target seems like an attractive middle ground between bare-bones furnishing and professional design. But there are important limitations to consider before building your entire rental around Target home goods.

What Target Does Well

Target excels at trend-forward accessories and decor at low price points. Their throw pillows, candles, small decorative objects, and bathroom accessories can add personality to a space without a major investment. For hosts looking to refresh seasonal decor or add finishing touches to an already well-furnished property, Target can be a useful supplementary source.

Where Target Falls Short for STRs

The core furniture offerings at Target are designed for budget-conscious residential consumers, not high-turnover commercial environments. Sofas, beds, dining tables, and dressers from Target typically feature lightweight construction, thin upholstery, and materials that show wear quickly under vacation rental use. A sofa that feels fine in a guest bedroom at home will sag and pill after hosting dozens of different guests in a rental property.

Design cohesion is another challenge. While Target individual pieces can be attractive, creating a cohesive, professionally finished look by shopping across their product lines requires significant design skill. The mix-and-match approach often produces rooms that feel like a college apartment rather than a premium vacation rental.

The Smart Approach

Target can play a supporting role in your vacation rental with accessories and consumables, but it should not be your primary furniture source if you want to compete for premium bookings. MyBnBDesign creates spaces that position your property at the top of search results and justify higher nightly rates, delivering returns that budget furniture simply cannot match.
