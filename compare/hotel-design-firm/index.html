An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Hotel Design Firm across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Hotel Design Firm

Hotel design firms specialize in creating commercial hospitality environments for branded and independent hotels. They bring expertise in FF&E (Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment) procurement, ADA compliance, and brand standards implementation. Their work typically involves large-scale projects with substantial budgets and extended timelines.

Key Differences Explained

Hotel design firms represent the top tier of hospitality interior design expertise. They understand guest flow, operational efficiency, brand standards, and the commercial requirements of hospitality environments. Their work in branded and boutique hotels demonstrates a deep understanding of what creates memorable guest experiences at scale.

However, hotel design is a fundamentally different discipline than STR design. Hotel projects involve commercial construction standards, ADA compliance requirements, fire code specifications, and institutional procurement processes that add enormous cost and complexity. A hotel design firm applying their standard methodology to a three-bedroom vacation rental would produce a result that is wildly over-engineered and over-budget.

Budget expectations are also misaligned. Hotel design budgets typically start at $50,000 per room and can exceed $150,000 per room for luxury properties. These budgets are justified by the commercial revenue potential of a hotel with hundreds of rooms and decades of operation, but they make no economic sense for individual STR properties.

MyBnBDesign brings hospitality-grade design thinking to the short-term rental market at price points that deliver strong ROI for individual property owners. We understand the guest experience principles that make hotels successful, such as thoughtful amenities, consistent quality, and attention to detail, but we apply them in a residential context with residential-scale budgets. The result is a property that feels like a boutique hotel but is priced like a smart rental investment.

Hotel Design Firm offers commercial hotel and hospitality design projects. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
