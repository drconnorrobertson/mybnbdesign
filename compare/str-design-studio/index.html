An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and STR Design Studio across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About STR Design Studio

STR Design Studio is a specialized short-term rental design firm offering custom interior design packages for Airbnb and VRBO hosts. They focus on creating Instagram-worthy spaces that attract bookings and provide design plans with curated shopping lists for property owners.

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

STR Design Studio understands the short-term rental market and creates design plans specifically optimized for listing platforms. Their focus on Instagram-worthy aesthetics and social media appeal is well-aligned with what drives bookings in today's visually-driven marketplace.

Where STR Design Studio falls short is in execution. Their deliverables typically consist of design plans, mood boards, and curated shopping lists, essentially a blueprint that the property owner must execute themselves. The gap between a beautiful design plan and a beautifully designed property is enormous: it involves coordinating dozens of vendors, managing delivery schedules, assembling furniture, hanging art, styling every surface, and ensuring the final result matches the original vision.

Many property owners who purchase design-plan-only services report that the final result falls 50-70% short of the original concept. Substitutions for out-of-stock items, incorrect assembly, poor hanging and placement, and missing accessories all compound to create a result that is merely acceptable rather than exceptional.

MyBnBDesign provides the same STR-specific design expertise, including social media-ready aesthetics, listing-optimized staging, and platform-specific photography direction. But we go further: every item is sourced by our procurement team at designer pricing, delivered on a coordinated schedule, and professionally installed by our crew. The result matches the concept perfectly, every time, because the same team that designed it also executes it.

The Bottom Line

STR Design Studio offers str-specific interior design plans and shopping lists. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
