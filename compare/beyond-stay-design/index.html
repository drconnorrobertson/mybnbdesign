MyBnBDesign vs Beyond Stay Design

An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Beyond Stay Design across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Beyond Stay Design

Beyond Stay Design focuses on creating memorable guest experiences through thoughtful interior design for vacation rental properties. They operate in select markets and offer design consultation and furnishing recommendations for property owners looking to improve their rental listings.

About MyBnBDesign

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. We provide end-to-end design solutions, from initial concept and space planning through furniture procurement at designer pricing, professional installation, styling, and listing photography direction. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Beyond Stay Design offers a design-focused approach to vacation rentals, but their service model stops short of full implementation. Property owners receive design concepts and furniture recommendations, but the critical execution phase, including procurement, logistics, delivery coordination, and professional installation, falls entirely on the owner.

This gap between design vision and execution is where most rental property projects fail. Sourcing furniture from multiple vendors, coordinating delivery windows, managing returns for damaged items, and physically installing everything from beds to wall art is a full-time project that can stretch weeks beyond the original timeline. Each day the property sits unfurnished is lost revenue.

MyBnBDesign eliminates this gap entirely with turnkey packages that take a property from empty walls to guest-ready in a single, coordinated effort. Our procurement team sources every item at designer pricing, manages all logistics, and our installation crew handles setup, styling, and final photography preparation.

The scope of market research also differs significantly. While Beyond Stay Design may consider basic aesthetic trends, MyBnBDesign conducts detailed competitive analysis of your specific market: what top-performing listings look like, what amenities they offer, what price points they command, and what gaps exist that your property can fill. This intelligence drives every design decision.

The Bottom Line

Beyond Stay Design offers vacation rental design consultation. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
