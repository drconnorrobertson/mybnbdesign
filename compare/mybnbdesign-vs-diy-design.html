MyBnBDesign vs DIY Design

Should you design your Airbnb yourself or hire professionals? Here is what the numbers say. Read our client reviews to see real results.

The DIY Temptation

Many first-time Airbnb hosts consider furnishing and designing their rental property themselves. The appeal is obvious: it seems cheaper, you have full control, and there are countless Pinterest boards and YouTube videos offering inspiration. But the reality of DIY vacation rental design is far more complex than most hosts anticipate, and the hidden costs can significantly impact your bottom line.

The True Cost of DIY Design

When you design your own short-term rental, the sticker price on furniture is only the beginning. Factor in the hours spent researching products, driving to stores, comparing options online, coordinating deliveries across multiple vendors, assembling furniture, styling rooms, and inevitably returning items that do not work in the space. Most hosts report spending 80 to 150 hours furnishing a single property themselves. At any reasonable hourly rate for your time, that labor cost alone often exceeds the cost of hiring a professional.

Then there are the design mistakes. See our 20 design mistakes that kill bookings for common pitfalls. Without professional training, hosts commonly choose furniture that is the wrong scale for the space, select color palettes that do not photograph well, miss critical functional elements that guests expect, and create layouts that feel awkward in person. Each of these mistakes costs money through lower nightly rates, weaker reviews, and reduced occupancy.

The Verdict

DIY design can work for hosts with strong design skills and abundant free time. But for most hosts, professional design from MyBnBDesign delivers a better property, faster timeline, and stronger financial returns. When you factor in the true cost of your time, the retail markup on furniture, and the revenue lost to suboptimal design, professional design is almost always the smarter investment.
