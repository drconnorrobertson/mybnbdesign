MyBnBDesign vs Rental Design Lab

An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Rental Design Lab across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Rental Design Lab

Rental Design Lab takes an experimental, data-driven approach to short-term rental design. They A/B test different design elements across properties to identify what drives the highest booking rates and guest satisfaction scores. Their research-backed methodology appeals to analytically-minded property investors.

About MyBnBDesign

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. We provide end-to-end design solutions, from initial concept and space planning through furniture procurement at designer pricing, professional installation, styling, and listing photography direction. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Rental Design Lab's data-driven approach represents an interesting evolution in STR design methodology. By A/B testing design elements across properties and measuring their impact on booking rates and guest satisfaction, they build an evidence base that informs future design decisions. For property investors who make decisions based on data, this analytical framework is appealing.

The limitation of a purely data-driven approach is that it can lead to formulaic designs that optimize for measurable metrics at the expense of the intangible qualities that make a space memorable. The most successful listings do not just check data-driven boxes; they tell a story, create an atmosphere, and deliver an emotional experience that guests remember and recommend.

Additionally, Rental Design Lab's service is primarily analytical and advisory. Their data insights inform design recommendations, but the actual design execution, including furniture selection, procurement, and installation, typically falls to the property owner or a separate design team. This creates a disconnect between insight and implementation.

MyBnBDesign combines data intelligence with design artistry. We analyze competitive listings, market trends, and guest behavior data to inform our design strategy, but our experienced designers translate that data into spaces that feel curated, intentional, and genuinely inviting, not algorithmically assembled. And we execute every aspect of the project from design through installation, ensuring that data-driven insights are faithfully realized in the finished space.

The Bottom Line

Rental Design Lab offers data-driven, experimental design approach for rentals. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
