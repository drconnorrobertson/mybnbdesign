MyBnBDesign vs Sonder Interior Design

An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Sonder Interior Design across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Sonder Interior Design

Sonder is a hospitality company operating tech-enabled apartments and hotels in 40+ markets globally. Their in-house design team creates hotel-quality interiors for their managed portfolio, blending residential comfort with hotel-grade consistency. Sonder went public via SPAC in 2022 and operates thousands of units worldwide.

About MyBnBDesign

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. We provide end-to-end design solutions, from initial concept and space planning through furniture procurement at designer pricing, professional installation, styling, and listing photography direction. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Sonder has redefined the boundary between hotels and short-term rentals, creating a hybrid hospitality product with hotel-grade consistency and apartment-style living spaces. Their design team produces clean, modern interiors that are instantly recognizable across their portfolio, a valuable brand consistency that drives repeat bookings.

Like other property management companies on this list, Sonder's design capabilities are exclusively available for properties within their managed portfolio. Property owners cannot access Sonder's design team or aesthetic as a standalone service. Partnering with Sonder means entering a management agreement where Sonder takes operational control and lists the property under their brand.

Sonder's hotel-inspired aesthetic also creates a specific design limitation: uniformity. While brand consistency has value, it means every Sonder property looks essentially the same, regardless of location, architecture, or local character. Guests booking a Sonder in Nashville get the same design language as a Sonder in Miami. For many STR properties, local character and unique design identity are competitive advantages that drive higher rates and better reviews.

MyBnBDesign creates designs that are unique to each property and market. A mountain retreat in Aspen receives a completely different design treatment than a beach house in Charleston or an urban loft in Nashville. This bespoke approach, tailored to local aesthetics, guest expectations, and competitive positioning, creates listings that stand out rather than blend in, driving premium rates and memorable guest experiences.

Sonder Interior Design offers hotel-grade interior design for sonder-operated properties only. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
