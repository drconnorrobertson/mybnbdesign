MyBnBDesign vs Kasa Living Design

An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Kasa Living Design across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Kasa Living Design

Kasa Living is a tech-enabled hospitality company managing furnished apartments and hotel accommodations. Backed by $70M+ in funding including Silver Lake, Kasa handles interior design as part of their property management package, designing units with custom furniture for modern travelers. They acquired Mint House in January 2026.

About MyBnBDesign

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. We provide end-to-end design solutions, from initial concept and space planning through furniture procurement at designer pricing, professional installation, styling, and listing photography direction. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Kasa Living represents a different model entirely: they are a property management company, not a design service. Their interior design capabilities are impressive, featuring custom furniture collections and professional staging, but they are only available as part of a comprehensive property management agreement where Kasa operates your property and takes a significant percentage of rental revenue.

For property owners who want to maintain control over their operations, pricing strategy, and guest relationships, Kasa's bundled approach means giving up operational autonomy in exchange for design services. The management fee, typically 20-30% of gross revenue, represents a significant ongoing cost that far exceeds the one-time investment in professional design.

MyBnBDesign provides the same caliber of professional design, custom furnishing, and staging without requiring you to hand over your property operations. You get the design expertise, the turnkey installation, and the listing optimization, then you manage your property however you choose: self-managed, with a local co-host, or with the property manager of your choosing.

This independence is particularly valuable for experienced hosts who have refined their operations and guest communication. Why pay 20-30% of every booking in perpetuity when a one-time design investment can achieve the same aesthetic result while preserving your operational control and revenue?

The Bottom Line

Kasa Living Design offers property management with integrated design for their managed portfolio. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
