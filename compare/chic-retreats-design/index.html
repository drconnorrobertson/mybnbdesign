MyBnBDesign vs Chic Retreats Design

An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Chic Retreats Design across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Chic Retreats Design

Chic Retreats Design specializes in luxury vacation rental interiors, creating high-end, editorial-quality spaces for premium properties. Their portfolio features stunning properties in resort destinations, and they work primarily with luxury homeowners seeking to command top-tier nightly rates in exclusive markets.

About MyBnBDesign

MyBnBDesign is a premium interior design firm dedicated exclusively to short-term rental properties. We provide end-to-end design solutions, from initial concept and space planning through furniture procurement at designer pricing, professional installation, styling, and listing photography direction. With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Chic Retreats Design delivers exceptional work in the luxury vacation rental segment. Their editorial-quality interiors feature bespoke furnishings, artisan pieces, and meticulous attention to detail that command premium nightly rates in resort destinations. For property owners with luxury homes in exclusive markets, Chic Retreats creates spaces that compete with five-star resorts.

The challenge with a luxury-only design firm is accessibility and ROI. Their minimum project budgets of $15,000+ exclude the majority of STR property owners, and their extended timelines of 12-24 weeks mean properties sit vacant for months during the design process, representing significant lost revenue. The ROI calculation on a $50,000+ design investment requires either an exceptionally high-value property or a very long investment horizon.

Not every property needs or benefits from a luxury design treatment. A well-designed two-bedroom condo in a competitive urban market may generate higher ROI from a $5,000-$10,000 design investment that increases nightly rates by 40% than a luxury property would see from a $50,000 investment that increases rates by 25%.

MyBnBDesign serves the full spectrum of STR properties, from studio apartments to luxury villas. Our design team brings the same level of professional expertise and attention to detail to every project, but we right-size the investment to match the property's revenue potential. The result is consistently strong ROI regardless of property tier, with our 40% average revenue increase demonstrating that great design does not require a luxury budget.

The Bottom Line

Chic Retreats Design offers luxury vacation rental interior design. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
