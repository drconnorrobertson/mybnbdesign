An honest, side-by-side look at two approaches to short-term rental design

How Do They Compare?

Choosing the right design partner for your short-term rental property is one of the most impactful decisions you will make as a host. The design of your space directly influences your nightly rate, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction scores, and ultimately your return on investment. Below, we compare MyBnBDesign and Airbnb Design Co across the factors that matter most to STR property owners.

About Airbnb Design Co

Airbnb Design Co operates as an independent design service catering exclusively to Airbnb hosts. They offer remote design consultations and furnishing packages aimed at helping new and experienced hosts create competitive listings. Their platform-specific knowledge of Airbnb's search algorithm and ranking factors informs their design recommendations.

With 200+ properties designed across 12 markets, our clients see an average 40% increase in rental revenue and maintain a 4.9 average guest rating. As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the intersection of real estate investment and design excellence.

Key Differences Explained

Airbnb Design Co brings valuable platform-specific knowledge to their design consultations, including understanding of Airbnb's search algorithm, ranking factors, and guest preference data. For hosts who list exclusively on Airbnb, this focused expertise can provide useful insights into what drives bookings on that specific platform.

However, limiting design strategy to a single platform introduces unnecessary risk and missed revenue opportunities. Most successful STR operators list across multiple platforms, including Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and direct booking websites. Design decisions optimized solely for Airbnb may not perform as well on other platforms where search algorithms, guest demographics, and booking behaviors differ.

The remote-only consultation model also presents limitations. Without visiting the property in person, designers cannot fully assess natural light conditions, architectural details, spatial flow, and neighborhood context, all of which significantly impact design decisions. A room that appears spacious in photos may feel cramped in person, and a color palette that works in coastal light may fall flat in a mountain setting.

MyBnBDesign designs for multi-platform success. Our team understands the nuances of listing optimization across Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and direct booking channels. We visit every property in person, assessing the space, light, and surroundings to create designs that look exceptional both in person and in listing photos across every platform where your property appears.

The Bottom Line

Airbnb Design Co offers airbnb-exclusive design consultation and furnishing guidance. MyBnBDesign provides a complete, turnkey design solution built exclusively for short-term rental success. If you are serious about maximizing your property's revenue potential with professional design that pays for itself, MyBnBDesign delivers the expertise, execution, and proven results to make it happen.
