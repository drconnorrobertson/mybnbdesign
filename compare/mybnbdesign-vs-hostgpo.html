MyBnBDesign vs HostGPO

Which short-term rental furnishing solution delivers better results for your Airbnb investment?

Overview: Two Different Approaches to STR Furnishing

When outfitting a short-term rental, hosts often compare MyBnBDesign and HostGPO. While both serve the vacation rental industry, they take fundamentally different approaches. HostGPO is a group purchasing organization that gives hosts access to discounted furniture and supplies through bulk buying power. MyBnBDesign is a full-service interior design firm that handles every aspect of designing, furnishing, and installing your rental property.

What HostGPO Offers

HostGPO provides members with access to discounted pricing from furniture retailers and hospitality suppliers. The platform works by aggregating purchasing power across its member base to negotiate lower prices. Hosts browse available products, place orders, and handle delivery and assembly themselves. HostGPO is essentially a discount membership program rather than a design service.

What MyBnBDesign Offers

MyBnBDesign provides end-to-end interior design and furnishing services built specifically for short-term rentals. Our process includes professional design consultation, custom space planning, furniture and decor procurement, delivery coordination, and full installation. You receive a turnkey property designed to maximize bookings and revenue without lifting a finger.

The Bottom Line

HostGPO can save you money on individual furniture purchases, but it leaves the entire design process, logistics, and installation in your hands. MyBnBDesign delivers a complete, professionally designed property without requiring your time or design expertise. For hosts who want maximum ROI with minimum effort, MyBnBDesign is the clear choice. The revenue gains from professional design typically far outweigh the price difference between discounted furniture and a full-service design package.
