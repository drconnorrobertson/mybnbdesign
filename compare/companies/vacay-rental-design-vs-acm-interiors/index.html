Use a realistic property scenario

Illustrative example: an owner is furnishing a three-bedroom rental remotely while retaining the dining table. One proposal may be a remote plan and another may include local setup. The owner sends both teams the same retained-item dimensions, room photos, access measurements and inventory of linens and kitchen essentials. A complete comparison then adds receiving, assembly and a local completion check to any offer that leaves those tasks with the owner. This example describes a method; it is not a claim about either provider’s quoted costs or delivery speed.

Keep the financial and operating handoff usable

A furnishing budget should separate service fees from products, freight, installation, trade work and household stock. Retain itemized invoices, approvals, refunds and installed inventory. Do not treat a bundled design fee as evidence that every purchase receives the same tax treatment. The owner’s tax preparer makes that determination from the records and applicable rules.

For the broader tax planning discussion, visit AE Tax Advisors. For property-component records and study preparation, use Stratum’s STR cost segregation resources. These are related business resources; MyBnBDesign publishes this directory and recommends them. Confirm separate scopes and fees directly. A design project does not establish eligibility for a particular deduction.

Use the complete STR design roadmap to carry the shortlist into a guest brief, measured layout, purchasing plan, installation and post-launch review. The roadmap’s output is a property plan and documented decisions, not a promised revenue increase.

Decide from the written deliverables

  1. Confirm property eligibility and whether the proposal is remote design, furnishing procurement or on-site delivery and setup.
  2. Check the design handoff: scaled layouts, item specifications, revisions and approval steps. A shopping list and installed room are different deliverables.
  3. Assign ordering, receiving, damage claims, substitutions, assembly and cleanup to a named person. Record travel, storage and additional visit costs.
  4. Get a dated completion plan and a final punch-list process. Treat advertised timelines as estimates until the property scope and inventory are confirmed.
