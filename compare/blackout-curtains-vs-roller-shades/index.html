Blackout curtains vs roller shades for rental bedrooms

Light control depends on the window, mounting method and edge gaps. Compare the installed solution in the room rather than relying on fabric names or a product image.

Compare tradeoffs · Make the decision · Use the worksheet

Blackout curtains or Blackout roller shades?

Compare the practical differences for your property
ConsiderationBlackout curtainsBlackout roller shades
CoverageWidth, overlap and mounting height affect light around the panels.Inside mounts may leave edge gaps; outside mounts need suitable fixing space.
CleaningCheck panel weight, care instructions and removal effort.Check approved surface cleaning and whether the fabric can be replaced.
OperationGuests need accessible pulls and panels that move without snagging.Choose appropriate controls and have the installed assembly checked against safety instructions.

A worked planning scenario

A hypothetical east-facing bedroom has a window close to a wall corner. Wide curtain stacking space is limited, while an inside-mounted shade leaves visible light at the edges. The host tests samples and considers an outside-mounted shade with a properly fitted curtain layer. The solution comes from the opening geometry, not a universal winner.

A practical decision process

  1. Measure the opening, trim, wall space and obstructions. Photograph the window from inside and outside the room.
  2. Confirm mounting requirements and select a suitable cordless design where available. Follow manufacturer instructions and current window-covering safety guidance.
  3. Test the finished room in daylight with the treatment closed. Check side, top and bottom light gaps from the bed.
  4. Document cleaning and replacement parts. Give the turnover team a simple inspection for loose fittings and damaged controls.

Questions before you commit

  • Will the treatment obstruct an opening or required exit?
  • What mounting surface is available?
  • How are damaged brackets, panels or shade assemblies replaced?
