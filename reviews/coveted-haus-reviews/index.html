Coveted Haus Reviews 2026: Pricing, Services, Pros and Cons

An honest look at Coveted Haus and their eDesign approach for short-term rentals

By MyBnBDesign Team | October 2026

Coveted Haus has built a reputation as an award-winning interior design firm with a focus on eDesign services for short-term rental properties. Their approach centers on delivering high-end design concepts virtually, creating beautiful spaces without requiring an in-person presence. For hosts looking to elevate the aesthetic quality of their listings, Coveted Haus offers an appealing option. But how does their virtual model hold up when it comes to the practical demands of STR design? In this review, we examine their services, pricing, strengths, and limitations so you can decide whether they are the right fit for your property.

What Is Coveted Haus?

Coveted Haus is an interior design firm that specializes in eDesign, sometimes called virtual interior design, for short-term rental properties. Rather than working on-site, the Coveted Haus team creates design concepts remotely. Clients receive detailed mood boards, room layouts, furniture and decor sourcing lists, finish selections, and design direction that they can then execute themselves or with the help of local contractors and delivery teams.

The firm positions itself at the higher end of the STR design market, emphasizing aesthetics, visual storytelling, and creating spaces that photograph well for listing platforms. Their portfolio showcases properties with curated color palettes, thoughtful material selections, and a cohesive design narrative that runs through each room. The focus is on making properties look and feel like a luxury experience for guests.

In addition to the core eDesign offering, Coveted Haus provides support with sourcing finishes and furniture, managing contractor communications where applicable, and coordinating deliveries to the extent that a virtual model allows. They work with clients across the country, which is one of the advantages of the remote format. Geographic constraints that would limit a traditional on-site design firm do not apply in the same way.

The company has received recognition in the design community and has been featured across various media outlets. Their visual brand leans heavily into aspirational aesthetics, targeting hosts who view their STR as a reflection of personal taste and who want a property that stands out visually in a crowded market.

Coveted Haus Pricing

Coveted Haus design fees typically range from $3,000 to $25,000 or more, depending on the size and complexity of the project. This pricing covers the design concept itself: mood boards, room-by-room layouts, furniture sourcing lists, material and finish recommendations, and the overall design direction. It is important to understand that furniture and decor costs are separate from the design fee.

This separation between design fees and furnishing costs is standard in the eDesign world, but it can lead to a higher total investment than hosts initially expect. A $5,000 design fee might sound reasonable, but once you add $20,000 to $40,000 in furniture, decor, linens, kitchenware, and accessories, the total project cost climbs significantly. Hosts should plan their budgets around the complete picture, not just the design consultation fee.

Budget tip: When evaluating eDesign services, always ask for an estimated total project cost that includes both design fees and projected furnishing expenses. This gives you a realistic view of the full investment before committing.

Coveted Haus may also charge separately for additional services such as contractor management support, revision rounds beyond the standard scope, or expedited timelines. Hosts with tight launch deadlines should clarify turnaround times and any rush fees during the initial consultation.

Coveted Haus Pros

High-End Aesthetic Quality

Coveted Haus excels at creating visually stunning interiors. Their portfolio demonstrates a strong eye for color, texture, and spatial composition. For hosts who want their property to look like it belongs in a design magazine, the firm delivers on that front consistently. The attention to visual detail is evident in their finished projects, with cohesive palettes and thoughtful layering of materials that photograph beautifully.

Remote Accessibility

The eDesign model means Coveted Haus can work with clients anywhere in the country. You do not need to be in the same city, or even the same state, as the design team. This is a meaningful advantage for investors who own properties in markets far from their primary residence. A host in New York can get a design concept for a cabin in Tennessee or a beach house in Florida without anyone needing to travel.

Award-Winning Reputation

Coveted Haus has earned recognition in the interior design space, which provides some assurance of quality. Awards and media features signal that the firm is producing work that meets professional standards and that peers in the design industry view positively. For hosts who value credentials and portfolio depth, this matters.

Design-Forward Approach

If your priority is creating a unique, Instagram-worthy property that stands out from cookie-cutter STR listings, Coveted Haus delivers a design-forward perspective. Their concepts tend to be more creative and less formulaic than what you might get from a budget-oriented furnishing service. Each project is treated as a distinct creative challenge rather than a template to be replicated.

Coveted Haus Cons

Virtual-Only Model Limits Execution Support

The most significant limitation of Coveted Haus is the virtual-only service model. While the design concepts are strong, the firm does not handle hands-on installation, furniture assembly, or on-site styling. That responsibility falls on the host, who must either do it themselves or hire a local team. For busy hosts or out-of-state investors, this gap between design plan and finished property can be a major pain point. Coordinating dozens of furniture deliveries, managing assembly crews, and styling rooms to match the designer's vision is time-consuming and often more difficult than it sounds.

Design Fees Separate from Furnishing Costs

The separation between design fees and furniture costs means the upfront price can be misleading. A host might budget $10,000 for their Coveted Haus engagement, only to realize the design fee covers the concept alone. The actual furniture, decor, and accessories required to execute the design could easily double or triple the total spend. This pricing structure is not unusual in eDesign, but it requires careful budgeting to avoid sticker shock.

Less Emphasis on STR Revenue Metrics

Coveted Haus approaches design primarily from an aesthetic perspective. While beautiful interiors certainly help with guest appeal, the firm places less emphasis on data-driven STR performance metrics like occupancy rate optimization, average daily rate strategy, and competitive positioning within specific markets. A dedicated STR design firm evaluates every design decision against its potential impact on booking revenue, not just its visual appeal. Hosts who prioritize return on investment over pure aesthetics may find this gap significant.

Limited Availability for Large Projects

As a boutique design firm, Coveted Haus may have limited capacity for large-scale projects or tight timelines. Hosts with multiple properties to furnish simultaneously, or those operating on aggressive launch schedules, may experience longer wait times. The personalized, high-touch nature of the service means that scaling to handle volume is inherently challenging.

Who Should Use Coveted Haus?

Coveted Haus is a strong fit for hosts who prioritize aesthetics above all else and who have the time, local resources, and willingness to manage the execution side of the design process themselves. If you are a hands-on host who enjoys the creative process of setting up a property, having a professional design concept to guide your purchasing and installation decisions can be extremely valuable. The virtual model also works well for hosts who have reliable local teams already in place, such as a handyman, cleaning crew, and delivery coordinator, who can execute the design plan on the ground.

Hosts with a strong personal design sensibility who want professional refinement of their existing vision will also appreciate Coveted Haus. Rather than handing over full creative control, these hosts can collaborate with the design team to refine and elevate ideas they already have in mind.

Who Should Look Elsewhere?

If you need a turnkey, move-in-ready property and do not have time to manage furniture deliveries, assembly, and styling yourself, the eDesign model will leave a significant execution gap. Full-service STR design firms like MyBnBDesign handle every step from concept through installation, so your property is guest-ready without requiring your involvement in logistics.

Hosts who are primarily focused on maximizing booking revenue and want design decisions grounded in market data, competitive analysis, and occupancy optimization should also consider firms that build their design process around financial performance rather than aesthetics alone. A beautiful property that does not perform well on the booking platforms is an expensive missed opportunity.

Out-of-state investors who do not have a local team to manage deliveries, assembly, and installation will find the eDesign model particularly challenging. Without someone on the ground to receive furniture, assemble beds and shelving, hang art, and style each room, the gap between the design plan and the finished product can be frustrating and costly to bridge.

Our Verdict on Coveted Haus

Coveted Haus produces beautiful, thoughtfully curated design concepts for short-term rental properties. Their eDesign approach makes professional interior design accessible to hosts nationwide, and their portfolio speaks to a genuine talent for creating visually compelling spaces. However, the virtual-only model means hosts are responsible for all execution logistics, the separation of design fees from furnishing costs can lead to higher-than-expected total investments, and the design philosophy prioritizes aesthetics over STR-specific revenue metrics. For hosts who want a complete, data-driven, move-in-ready design solution, a full-service STR design firm will typically deliver a stronger return on investment with far less effort required from the property owner.
