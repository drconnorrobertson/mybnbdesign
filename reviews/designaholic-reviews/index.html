Designaholic Reviews 2026: Pricing, Pros, Cons and Alternatives

An independent look at Designaholic's virtual STR design service and how it compares to full-service options

By MyBnBDesign Team | October 2026

Designaholic positions itself as a virtual interior design service built specifically for the short-term rental market. Rather than offering full-service furnishing and installation, Designaholic delivers eDesign packages that give hosts professional design guidance, mood boards, and curated shopping lists they can execute on their own. In this review, we break down what Designaholic offers, what it costs, where it excels, and where it falls short for STR hosts looking to maximize their rental revenue.

Transparency note: MyBnBDesign is a full-service STR design company. We are a competitor to Designaholic. We have done our best to present accurate, fair information in this review. We encourage readers to research multiple providers before making a decision.

What Is Designaholic?

Designaholic is a virtual interior design service that focuses exclusively on short-term rental properties. The company understands that designing for a vacation rental is fundamentally different from designing a personal home. STR spaces need to be durable enough to handle constant guest turnover, functional enough to meet the needs of travelers, and visually appealing enough to generate strong listing photos and positive reviews. Designaholic aims to address all three of these considerations through its eDesign packages.

The service operates entirely online, which means there are no in-person consultations, on-site measurements, or hands-on installation support. Hosts submit photos, floor plans, and details about their property, and Designaholic returns a comprehensive design plan tailored to their space. The company claims to have worked with many properties and focuses on creating spaces that appeal to specific guest demographics, whether that means families, business travelers, couples, or adventure seekers.

Designaholic's approach is rooted in the idea that hosts can save money by handling the execution themselves. Instead of paying for a turnkey service that covers everything from concept to installation, hosts pay for the design expertise alone. This can be appealing for budget-conscious owners who are comfortable with the purchasing and setup process, but it does create a significant coordination burden that some hosts underestimate.

Designaholic Pricing

Designaholic's eDesign packages generally range from $2,500 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of the property, the number of rooms included, and the level of detail in the design deliverables. Basic packages typically cover mood boards, furniture layouts, and shopping lists for key living spaces. More comprehensive packages may include full-property design plans with detailed specifications for every room, styling guidance, and listing photo direction.

It is important to note that these prices cover the design work only. The cost of furniture, decor, linens, kitchenware, and other supplies is separate and paid directly by the property owner. For a typical two-bedroom vacation rental, the total outfitting cost (design fee plus furnishings) can range from $15,000 to $40,000 or more depending on the quality of items selected and the local market.

Designaholic Pros

Lower upfront design cost

Because Designaholic only charges for the design work itself, the initial fee is lower than what you would pay for a full-service company that handles purchasing and installation. For hosts who are handy, well-organized, and comfortable sourcing furniture on their own, this can translate to meaningful savings on the design portion of the project.

STR-specific expertise

Unlike general interior designers who occasionally take on rental projects, Designaholic is built around the vacation rental niche. The team understands considerations like durability, guest flow, luggage storage, and the importance of strong listing photography. This specialization means the design recommendations should be more practical for a rental setting than what you might get from a residential designer.

Demographic-focused design approach

Designaholic focuses on creating spaces that appeal to specific guest demographics. If your property targets families, the design will emphasize child-friendly furniture arrangements, durable materials, and practical amenities. If your market is couples seeking romantic getaways, the design will lean toward softer color palettes, intimate seating areas, and upscale touches. This targeted approach can help properties stand out in crowded markets.

Nationwide virtual availability

Because the entire service operates online, Designaholic can work with properties in any location. Hosts in remote areas, small towns, or markets without local STR designers can still access professional design guidance without needing someone on-site.

Designaholic Cons

No installation or setup support

The most significant limitation of Designaholic's model is the gap between receiving a design plan and having a fully furnished, guest-ready property. Once you receive your mood boards and shopping lists, the responsibility shifts entirely to you. You need to order all of the furniture, coordinate deliveries, assemble items, arrange everything according to the design plan, and handle all the small details like hanging artwork, setting up the kitchen, and styling shelves. For hosts managing properties remotely, this can be especially challenging and time-consuming.

Coordination burden falls on the owner

Separating design from purchasing creates a natural coordination challenge. Shopping lists are helpful, but sourcing specific items across multiple retailers, tracking orders, managing returns for damaged goods, and dealing with backorders all take significant time and energy. Many hosts underestimate this workload and end up spending weeks or even months getting their property ready, which means lost rental income during the setup period.

Limited post-design support

After you receive your design deliverables, ongoing support from Designaholic tends to be limited. If items on your shopping list go out of stock, if you need substitution recommendations, or if you want to refresh the design after a year of guest wear, you may need to start a new engagement or handle those decisions on your own. Full-service companies often include ongoing support as part of their relationship with clients.

Less data-driven revenue optimization

While Designaholic designs with target demographics in mind, the approach appears to be less data-driven than what some full-service competitors offer. Companies like MyBnBDesign analyze market-specific booking data, competitive set performance, and revenue trends to make design decisions that directly impact nightly rates and occupancy. A purely aesthetic approach to design, even one that considers guest demographics, may miss opportunities to optimize for measurable financial outcomes.

Who Is Designaholic Best For?

Designaholic works best for budget-conscious hosts who want professional design direction but are comfortable handling the execution themselves. If you enjoy the process of shopping for furniture, are well-organized enough to manage multiple vendor orders, have time to dedicate to the setup process, and are located close enough to the property to oversee installation, the eDesign model can be a cost-effective way to get a professionally designed space without paying for full-service support.

Hosts who are new to the STR business, managing properties remotely, or looking for a true turnkey solution will likely find the virtual-only model frustrating. The gap between receiving a design plan and having a guest-ready property is wider than many first-time owners expect, and the lost revenue during an extended setup period can quickly offset any savings on the design fee itself.

Designaholic vs. MyBnBDesign

The fundamental difference between Designaholic and MyBnBDesign comes down to scope of service. Designaholic provides design plans that owners execute independently. MyBnBDesign handles the entire process from initial design concept through furniture procurement, delivery, installation, and final styling. For hosts who value their time, manage properties remotely, or simply want to focus on running their business rather than assembling furniture, the full-service model eliminates the coordination burden entirely.

MyBnBDesign also takes a data-driven approach to design decisions. Rather than designing solely based on aesthetic preferences or broad demographic categories, the team analyzes local market data, competitive listings, booking patterns, and pricing trends to make design choices that directly support higher nightly rates and stronger occupancy. This analytical layer adds a revenue optimization component that pure design services typically do not include.

Our Verdict

Designaholic fills a real gap in the STR design market by offering affordable, rental-specific design guidance through a virtual format. For self-sufficient hosts with the time, proximity, and organizational skills to execute a design plan independently, it can be a solid option. However, the virtual-only model creates meaningful limitations around installation support, purchasing coordination, and ongoing property management. Hosts who want a truly hands-off experience, a faster time to market, or a design approach grounded in revenue data will likely be better served by a full-service provider like MyBnBDesign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Designaholic handle furniture purchasing and installation?

No. Designaholic is a virtual design service that provides design plans, mood boards, and shopping lists. Purchasing furniture and coordinating installation is the responsibility of the property owner. This is one of the key differences between Designaholic and full-service providers like MyBnBDesign.

Is Designaholic good for first-time Airbnb hosts?

However, the virtual-only model requires owners to handle all purchasing and setup on their own, which can be overwhelming for newcomers who are still learning the STR business. First-time hosts may benefit from a more hands-on, full-service option that reduces the learning curve.
