Laurie Mattson Interiors Reviews: STR Design Services Analyzed [2026]

An independent look at Laurie Mattson Interiors for short-term rental design, including pricing, services, strengths, and limitations.

By MyBnBDesign Team | October 2026

Laurie Mattson Interiors offers full-service interior design for short-term rentals, with a focus on creating properties that attract guests and perform well on booking platforms. In this review, we break down what the company offers, what it costs, who it works best for, and where it may fall short for certain hosts.

Transparency note: MyBnBDesign is an STR design company. We review competitors and peers to help hosts make informed decisions. We aim for accuracy but encourage readers to do their own research and request quotes directly from any company they are considering.

What Is Laurie Mattson Interiors?

Laurie Mattson Interiors is a full-service interior design firm that specializes in short-term rental properties. The company provides hands-on design services for hosts who want their vacation rentals to look professionally designed, feel welcoming to guests, and ultimately generate higher booking rates and revenue.

The firm takes a personalized approach to each project. Rather than offering standardized design packages or templated solutions, Laurie Mattson Interiors works closely with individual property owners to understand their goals, their target guest demographic, and the unique characteristics of each property. This hands-on philosophy is central to the company's value proposition and sets it apart from larger, more systematized design firms.

Based regionally, Laurie Mattson Interiors primarily serves hosts within her local service area. This geographic focus allows her to provide in-person consultations, on-site visits, and direct oversight of installations. For hosts within that footprint, the personal touch can be a significant advantage. For hosts outside the area, however, the regional limitation is an important factor to consider.

Services Offered

Laurie Mattson Interiors provides a range of interior design services tailored to the short-term rental market. While specific service offerings may evolve, the core capabilities typically include the following:

Full-Service Interior Design

The flagship offering is comprehensive interior design for STR properties. This encompasses everything from initial concept development through final installation and styling. The process generally involves an in-depth consultation to understand the property owner's goals, a detailed assessment of the space, development of a design concept, furniture and decor selection, procurement, and final installation with professional styling. The goal is to deliver a move-in-ready rental that photographs well and appeals to the target guest market.

Space Planning and Layout Optimization

Effective space planning is critical for short-term rentals, where guests expect both comfort and functionality. Laurie Mattson Interiors works on optimizing floor plans to maximize usable space, ensure comfortable traffic flow, and create distinct living zones that photograph well for listing images. This includes decisions about furniture scale, room arrangement, and how to make smaller spaces feel open and inviting.

Furniture and Decor Sourcing

Selecting the right furniture and decor for a vacation rental requires balancing aesthetics with durability, guest comfort with maintenance practicality, and style with budget constraints. The firm handles sourcing of furnishings, artwork, textiles, lighting, and accessories. The selections are made with the specific demands of STR properties in mind, including higher turnover, varied guest demographics, and the need for pieces that withstand regular use.

Color Palette and Material Selection

The visual identity of a rental property starts with its color palette and material choices. Laurie Mattson Interiors develops cohesive color schemes, selects paint colors, specifies flooring and countertop materials, and chooses textiles that work together to create a unified, inviting atmosphere. These choices also factor in photography considerations, since listing images are often the first impression potential guests have of a property.

Pricing and Cost Structure

Pricing for Laurie Mattson Interiors typically falls in the range of $5,000 to $30,000 or more. The wide range reflects the significant variation in project scope, from a single-room refresh to a complete multi-bedroom property transformation. Several factors influence where a particular project lands within that range.

Pros of Working with Laurie Mattson Interiors

Personalized, Hands-On Service

One of the strongest selling points is the personal attention each project receives. Working with a smaller, regionally focused firm means you are likely working directly with the principal designer rather than being handed off to junior staff. This level of personal involvement can result in a design that truly reflects the vision of the property owner and the character of the local market.

STR-Specific Design Knowledge

Laurie Mattson Interiors focuses specifically on short-term rentals, which means the design decisions are informed by an understanding of what drives bookings, positive reviews, and guest satisfaction. This is different from a general interior designer who may create beautiful spaces that are not optimized for the demands of high-turnover vacation rentals.

Local Market Understanding

A regional focus brings deep familiarity with the local rental market, guest expectations in that area, and the competitive landscape. Designs can be tailored to appeal to the specific types of travelers who visit the region, whether that means families, couples, outdoor enthusiasts, or business travelers.

Direct Oversight of Installation

Being local means the designer can personally oversee furniture delivery, placement, and final styling. This hands-on involvement during installation often results in better attention to detail and fewer issues compared to remote design projects where the property owner is responsible for executing the design plan.

Cons and Limitations

Regional Availability

The most significant limitation is geographic. Laurie Mattson Interiors serves a specific regional area, which means hosts in other parts of the country cannot access her services. For investors with properties in multiple markets, this limitation makes it difficult to use a single design partner across their entire portfolio.

Smaller Team and Capacity Constraints

As a smaller operation, there may be limitations on how many projects can be taken on simultaneously. This can lead to longer wait times, especially during peak seasons when many hosts are preparing properties for high-demand booking periods. Hosts working on tight timelines for a property launch should discuss availability early in the process.

Scalability Challenges

For investors who are growing their STR portfolios, scalability is an important consideration. A boutique, regionally focused firm may not have the infrastructure to handle rapid portfolio expansion across multiple markets. Hosts with three, five, or ten properties to design may find that a larger operation with established logistics networks is better suited to their needs.

Limited Online Presence and Reviews

Compared to larger STR design companies, Laurie Mattson Interiors has a more limited online presence and fewer publicly available reviews and case studies. This can make it harder for prospective clients to evaluate the quality of work, read about other hosts' experiences, and compare results before committing to a project. Hosts should ask for references and portfolio examples directly.

Who Is Laurie Mattson Interiors Best For?

Laurie Mattson Interiors is best suited for hosts who meet several criteria. First, they should be located within or near her service area, since the hands-on, in-person approach is central to the value proposition. Second, they should be looking for a personalized design experience where they work closely with a dedicated designer rather than going through a larger, more systematized process. Third, they should have a single property or a small number of properties to design, since the boutique model works best at smaller scale.

Hosts who value personal relationships with their service providers, who want a designer who truly understands their local market, and who are comfortable with a potentially longer timeline in exchange for more individualized attention will find the most value in working with Laurie Mattson Interiors.

Who Should Consider Alternatives?

There are several types of hosts for whom Laurie Mattson Interiors may not be the best fit. Hosts located outside the firm's service area obviously need to look elsewhere. Portfolio investors with multiple properties across different markets need a design partner with broader geographic reach and the operational capacity to handle concurrent projects. Hosts working on very tight launch timelines may find that the availability constraints of a smaller team create scheduling challenges.

Additionally, hosts who prefer a more data-driven, systematized approach to STR design, with detailed revenue projections, platform-optimized photography guidance, and listing optimization as part of the design package, may want to explore companies that build those elements directly into their service offering.

Our Verdict

Laurie Mattson Interiors delivers personalized, hands-on interior design for short-term rentals within her service area. The boutique approach and local market knowledge are genuine strengths for hosts who are located nearby and looking for individualized attention. However, the regional limitations, smaller team capacity, and limited online presence are factors that hosts should weigh carefully. For hosts outside the service area, those with multiple properties, or those who want a more scalable solution with broader support, exploring other STR design companies may be the right move.
