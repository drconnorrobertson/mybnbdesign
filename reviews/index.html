Evaluate the evidence behind a review

A customer review should identify the relevant project and service tier. Distinguish the owner’s account of design work from a guest’s review of the completed stay. Check the original source and date, and ask whether the work resembles your property, budget and implementation model. A testimonial can describe an experience without establishing typical outcomes.

The prior numerical score, review total and unattributed-to-verifiable-source testimonial collection on this hub have been removed. No replacement star score or aggregate performance result is asserted. For MyBnBDesign, start with the published portfolio and request project-specific evidence and a written scope.

Compare scope before price

Bring the same measured plan and retained-item inventory to each candidate. Ask who orders, receives, installs and accepts the finished rooms, and compare the complete project cost after assigning excluded work. A provider’s lowest advertised package is not a quote for your property.
