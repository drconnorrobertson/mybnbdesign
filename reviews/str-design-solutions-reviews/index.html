STR Design Solutions Reviews 2026: Pricing, Pros, Cons and Alternatives

An independent look at STR Design Solutions' flexible design and setup services for vacation rental hosts

By MyBnBDesign Team | October 2026

STR Design Solutions positions itself as a versatile design and setup company for short-term rental properties. The company offers a range of services from digital design plans all the way to complete property transformation, giving hosts the flexibility to choose how much support they need. In this review, we examine what STR Design Solutions offers, how much it costs, where it delivers value, and where hosts should be aware of potential limitations.

Transparency note: MyBnBDesign is a full-service STR design company. We are a competitor to STR Design Solutions. We have done our best to present accurate, fair information in this review. We encourage readers to research multiple providers before making a decision.

What Is STR Design Solutions?

STR Design Solutions is a design and setup company that creates unique short-term rental spaces through a full suite of services. The company distinguishes itself by offering multiple service tiers that allow hosts to engage at whatever level makes sense for their situation. Whether you need a digital design plan for a property you want to furnish yourself, a partial redesign of specific rooms, or a complete ground-up design and setup, STR Design Solutions aims to provide tailored solutions for each client.

The company's service offerings include digital design (remote design plans similar to eDesign), redesign of existing properties that need an update or refresh, partial design for hosts who want professional help with specific areas while handling others independently, complete design covering an entire property from concept to final styling, and full setup services that include procurement, delivery, and installation. This flexibility is one of the company's key selling points, as it allows hosts to scale the engagement to match their budget and comfort level.

STR Design Solutions takes a client-centered approach, working to understand each property's target market, location, and the owner's goals before creating a design plan. The company focuses on creating spaces that are not just visually appealing but also functional and durable enough to withstand the demands of regular guest turnover. This practical mindset is essential in the STR space, where design decisions need to balance aesthetics with real-world durability and guest experience.

STR Design Solutions Pricing

STR Design Solutions pricing typically ranges from $5,000 to $35,000 or more, with the final cost depending heavily on which service tier you choose and the size of your property. Digital design packages sit at the lower end of the range, while complete design and setup services for larger or more complex properties can reach the upper end or beyond. The broad range reflects the company's flexible service model, where costs scale with the scope of work.

For a standard two-bedroom vacation rental, hosts can expect to pay somewhere in the $8,000 to $20,000 range for design and setup services, with furniture and decor costs adding another $10,000 to $30,000 depending on quality and market positioning. Properties in luxury markets or with more bedrooms will naturally cost more. It is worth noting that pricing may vary based on project timing, as the company's capacity can affect availability and potentially turnaround times during peak seasons.

STR Design Solutions Pros

Flexible service tiers

The ability to choose from digital design, partial design, complete design, or full setup services is a genuine advantage. Not every host needs or can afford a complete turnkey solution, and STR Design Solutions gives property owners the ability to engage at a level that matches their budget, skills, and available time. A host who is handy with assembly and setup might choose a digital design package, while someone managing a property remotely might opt for the full setup service. This flexibility sets STR Design Solutions apart from companies that only offer one service model.

Tailored approach to each property

STR Design Solutions takes time to understand each client's specific situation, including the property's location, target guest demographic, competitive landscape, and the owner's financial goals. This personalized approach means that a beachfront condo will receive a very different design treatment than a mountain cabin, which is exactly how it should be. Cookie-cutter designs rarely perform well in the STR market, so the emphasis on customization is a meaningful benefit for hosts.

Complete transformation capability

For hosts who choose the full-service option, STR Design Solutions can handle a complete property transformation from an empty or outdated space to a guest-ready rental. This includes design concept development, furniture selection, procurement, delivery coordination, installation, and final styling. Having a single company manage the entire process reduces the coordination headaches that come with hiring separate designers, furniture buyers, and installers.

Redesign services for existing properties

Many STR design companies focus exclusively on new property setups, leaving hosts with established rentals to figure out refreshes and updates on their own. STR Design Solutions offers redesign services specifically for existing properties that need an update, whether that means a full overhaul or targeted improvements to underperforming spaces. This is valuable for hosts whose listings have become dated or whose booking performance has declined due to increased competition in their market.

STR Design Solutions Cons

Smaller operation may have capacity limits

As a smaller company in the STR design space, STR Design Solutions may face capacity constraints that larger firms do not. During busy seasons when many hosts are preparing properties for peak rental periods, project timelines could extend beyond initial estimates. Hosts with tight deadlines or properties that need to be guest-ready by a specific date should discuss capacity and scheduling upfront to avoid potential delays that could result in lost rental income.

Turnaround times may be longer during peak seasons

Related to capacity limits, turnaround times for design deliverables, furniture procurement, and installation may stretch during high-demand periods. The STR design industry tends to see surges in activity during spring and early summer as hosts prepare for peak vacation season. Hosts who plan ahead and engage early in the process will generally have a smoother experience than those who need rapid turnaround during these busy windows.

Less brand recognition and fewer public reviews

Compared to more established competitors, STR Design Solutions has less brand recognition in the market and fewer publicly available reviews and testimonials. This makes it harder for potential clients to evaluate the quality of work, consistency of results, and overall client satisfaction before committing. While a smaller portfolio does not necessarily indicate lower quality, it does mean that hosts have less third-party validation to draw on when making their decision. Prospective clients should ask for references and examples of completed projects similar to their own property.

Geographic reach may be limited for full-service work

While digital design packages can be delivered anywhere, the full setup and installation services that make STR Design Solutions most valuable may be limited to certain geographic areas. Hosts with properties in remote locations or outside the company's primary service areas may only be able to access the digital design tier, which removes much of the convenience advantage that the full-service model provides. It is important to confirm service availability for your specific property location before beginning the engagement process.

Who Is STR Design Solutions Best For?

STR Design Solutions is a strong fit for hosts who want flexibility in how they engage with a design company. If you have a clear sense of how much help you need and appreciate the ability to scale services up or down, the tiered model is genuinely useful. The company is particularly well-suited for hosts who have an existing property that needs a refresh or redesign, as many competitors focus primarily on new setups.

Hosts who prioritize working with a well-established company that has extensive public reviews, a large portfolio, and proven data-driven design methodology may want to explore alternatives with longer track records. Similarly, hosts with properties in locations outside STR Design Solutions' primary service area should verify that the service tier they need is available before committing time to the consultation process.

STR Design Solutions vs. MyBnBDesign

Both STR Design Solutions and MyBnBDesign offer full-service STR design capabilities, but they differ in scale, approach, and market positioning. MyBnBDesign operates as a larger, more established full-service provider with an extensive portfolio of completed projects across multiple markets. The company uses a data-driven design methodology that incorporates local market analytics, competitive set analysis, and booking performance data into every design decision, ensuring that aesthetic choices support measurable revenue outcomes.

STR Design Solutions offers more flexibility in its service tiers, which can be an advantage for hosts who want to control how much of the process they delegate. However, MyBnBDesign's integrated approach, where design, procurement, installation, and ongoing support are all managed by a single team, tends to produce faster turnaround times, more consistent results, and a smoother overall experience for the property owner. The choice between the two often comes down to whether you value service flexibility or a proven, comprehensive approach backed by market data.

Our Verdict

STR Design Solutions offers a genuinely flexible approach to STR design that can work well for hosts who want to choose their level of engagement. The range of services from digital design to complete setup provides options that many competitors do not match. However, the company's smaller scale, limited public reviews, and potential geographic constraints are worth considering. Hosts who want a proven, data-driven, full-service experience with extensive portfolio validation will likely find a stronger fit with an established provider like MyBnBDesign, while those who value flexibility and are comfortable with a newer brand may find STR Design Solutions to be a capable partner.
