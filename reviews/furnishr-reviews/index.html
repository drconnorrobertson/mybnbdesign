Furnishr Reviews 2026: Pricing, Pros, Cons and What Hosts Should Know

An honest breakdown of Furnishr's furniture package service for vacation rental owners

By MyBnBDesign Team | October 2026

Furnishr has built a name in the vacation rental space by offering "room-in-a-box" furniture packages that cover design, procurement, delivery, and installation. Founded by Karen Lau, the company promises a hands-off way to furnish short-term rental properties without the hassle of sourcing individual pieces. But is a furniture package service the right fit for every host? In this review we break down what Furnishr offers, what it costs, where it excels, and where it falls short so you can make an informed decision for your STR business.

What Is Furnishr?

Furnishr is a furniture package and delivery service designed primarily for vacation rental hosts, property managers, and real estate investors who need to furnish properties quickly and efficiently. Rather than hiring a traditional interior designer and shopping for each item individually, Furnishr bundles everything into pre-designed packages that arrive ready for white-glove installation.

The company handles the full pipeline from initial design consultation through procurement, warehousing, delivery, assembly, and final placement. Some packages also include professional photography once the space is styled. Furnishr offers a variety of popular aesthetic styles including Bohemian, Industrial, Coastal, Mid-Century Modern, Contemporary, Scandinavian, Glam, and Transitional, giving hosts a range of looks to choose from depending on their target market and property location.

The core value proposition is convenience. Hosts who do not want to spend weeks comparison shopping, coordinating deliveries from multiple vendors, and assembling furniture themselves can hand the entire process to Furnishr and receive a finished property. This model appeals strongly to remote investors who own properties in markets they do not live near, as well as first-time hosts who lack experience furnishing a rental.

What Furnishr Does Well

Streamlined Procurement and Delivery

The strongest aspect of Furnishr's service is the end-to-end logistics. Sourcing furniture for a vacation rental is one of the most time-consuming parts of launching a new listing. You have to research vendors, compare prices, coordinate shipping timelines, arrange delivery windows, and hope everything arrives intact and on schedule. Furnishr eliminates that complexity by bundling procurement and delivery into a single service. For hosts who value their time or who live far from their properties, this convenience is substantial.

Curated Style Options

Furnishr offers eight distinct style packages, which is more variety than many competitors provide. The Bohemian, Coastal, and Mid-Century Modern options tend to perform well in popular vacation rental markets. Having a curated selection helps hosts who feel overwhelmed by the sheer volume of furniture options available online. Each package is designed to be cohesive, so the finished space looks intentionally styled rather than pieced together.

White-Glove Installation

The installation service is a genuine differentiator compared to buying furniture from retailers and handling assembly yourself. Furnishr's team unpacks, assembles, and places every piece. They also handle disposal of packaging materials. For a large property with dozens of items, this saves hours of labor and eliminates the frustration of dealing with damaged items or missing hardware during self-assembly.

Photography Add-On

Some Furnishr packages include professional photography after installation, which is a valuable bonus. Quality listing photos are one of the highest-ROI investments a host can make, and having them captured immediately after a professional installation ensures the property is photographed at its best.

Where Furnishr Falls Short

Limited Deep Customization

The package-based model is both a strength and a limitation. While the curated styles look polished, hosts who want a truly unique or property-specific design may find the options restrictive. You are choosing from predefined bundles rather than selecting individual pieces that perfectly fit your space, your target demographic, and your competitive landscape. In a saturated market where differentiation matters, a cookie-cutter package may not give you the competitive edge you need to stand out from neighboring listings.

Pricing Opacity

Several hosts have noted that Furnishr's pricing is not always transparent upfront. While the company provides general ranges, the final quote often depends on a consultation call and can include add-ons, delivery surcharges, and style upgrades that push the total higher than initial expectations. For budget-conscious hosts, this lack of clarity can be frustrating during the planning phase.

Limited Revenue Optimization Focus

Furnishr is fundamentally a furniture logistics company, not a revenue optimization consultancy. The service does not typically include competitive market analysis, listing strategy, pricing recommendations, or data-driven design decisions aimed at maximizing nightly rates and occupancy. For hosts who view design as a revenue lever rather than simply an aesthetic choice, this gap is significant. A well-designed property can command 20 to 40 percent higher nightly rates in competitive markets, and that premium requires strategic design thinking that goes beyond selecting a style package.

Post-Installation Support and Refresh Options

Once Furnishr delivers and installs your furniture, the ongoing relationship is relatively limited. Vacation rental furniture takes a beating from constant guest turnover, and hosts need refresh strategies, replacement plans, and seasonal updates to maintain listing quality over time. Full-service STR design firms often provide ongoing support, seasonal styling updates, and damage replacement coordination that keep the property performing at a high level year after year. Furnishr's model is more transactional, focused on the initial setup rather than long-term performance maintenance.

Who Is Furnishr Best For?

Furnishr is a strong fit for:

  • First-time hosts who want a simple, all-in-one furnishing solution without managing multiple vendors
  • Remote investors who need a property furnished quickly without being on-site
  • Hosts on a moderate budget who prefer a known package price over open-ended design fees
  • Owners who prioritize speed and convenience over deep customization

Furnishr may not be the best choice for:

  • Hosts in competitive markets who need a unique design to stand out from dozens of similar listings
  • Investors focused on maximizing revenue per property through strategic, data-driven design
  • Owners who want ongoing design support, seasonal refreshes, and long-term property management guidance
  • Luxury property owners who require fully bespoke interiors tailored to a high-end guest demographic

What Real Hosts Say About Furnishr

Host feedback on Furnishr tends to cluster around a few consistent themes. On the positive side, many hosts praise the convenience factor. Comments like "it saved me weeks of shopping" and "everything showed up and was set up in one day" appear frequently. The white-glove delivery and installation process receives strong marks, particularly from hosts who have previously dealt with the frustration of coordinating deliveries from multiple retailers.

On the critical side, some hosts mention that the final cost was higher than they initially expected after add-ons and delivery fees were included. Others note that while the packages look attractive, they sometimes feel generic, especially in markets where multiple nearby listings use similar Furnishr styles. A few hosts have also mentioned that post-installation support was limited, with replacement items being harder to source than expected when individual pieces became damaged from guest use.

The photography service, when included, receives positive reviews. Hosts appreciate having professional photos taken immediately after installation while the space looks its best. However, some note that the photography does not come with listing optimization guidance, leaving them to write their own descriptions and set their own pricing without professional input.

Final Verdict

Furnishr fills a legitimate need in the vacation rental market. For hosts who want a straightforward, package-based furnishing solution with white-glove delivery and installation, it offers real convenience and time savings. The variety of style options is solid, the logistics are well-executed, and the ability to rent furniture provides flexibility for hosts who are testing new markets.

However, hosts who are serious about maximizing revenue, standing out in competitive markets, and maintaining their properties at a high level over time should consider whether a package-based approach provides enough strategic depth. The lack of revenue optimization, limited customization, and minimal post-installation support mean that Furnishr works best as a furnishing solution rather than a comprehensive STR design partner.

If your primary goal is to get a property furnished quickly and affordably, Furnishr deserves consideration. If your goal is to build a high-performing STR business where design drives bookings, occupancy, and premium nightly rates, a full-service design firm may deliver significantly stronger returns on your investment.
