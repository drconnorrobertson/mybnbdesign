Compare a complete proposal

  • Separate design fees, furnishing purchases, freight, receiving, installation and travel.
  • Ask who approves substitutions and what happens when an item is damaged or delayed.
  • Clarify how design revisions, site visits, photo staging and handoff are billed.
  • Confirm whether the offered scope includes your property type and market.

Published starting fees are useful for an initial conversation but are not a complete property budget. Compare written deliverables rather than expecting all providers to price the same scope. Use the quote scope worksheet to keep responsibilities and exclusions visible.
