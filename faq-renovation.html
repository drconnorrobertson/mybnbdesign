Renovation vs. Furnishing FAQ

Learn when a property needs renovation, when furnishing is enough, and how to plan both.

Home / FAQ / Renovation vs. Furnishing FAQ
How do I know if my property needs renovation or just furnishing?

If the home is clean, structurally sound, and has working systems, furnishing and styling alone may be enough. Renovation is worth considering when dated finishes, damage, or layout problems would hurt photos and guest reviews. We assess the property and recommend the most cost-effective path to a strong listing.

What is the difference between renovation and furnishing?

Furnishing covers movable items such as furniture, decor, linens, and kitchen supplies. Renovation changes the physical property itself, including floors, cabinets, fixtures, walls, and systems. Many successful STRs combine light renovation with strong furnishing for the best result.

What counts as a cosmetic update?

Cosmetic updates change the look without altering structure or major systems, such as paint, hardware, lighting fixtures, backsplashes, and flooring. They are usually faster and cheaper than structural work and often deliver the biggest visual impact. For many rentals, cosmetic updates are the best return on investment.

How much can paint change an STR?

Fresh paint is one of the most cost-effective upgrades available. Neutral, warm tones photograph well and make rooms feel larger and cleaner, while a bold accent wall can add personality. Using scrubbable, durable paint finishes also reduces maintenance between guests.

Is wallpaper a good idea in a short-term rental?

Yes, when used selectively. Peel-and-stick or high-quality wallpaper in an entry, bedroom headboard wall, or powder room creates a memorable accent that photographs well. Choose durable, wipeable materials, and avoid covering entire large spaces with busy patterns.

What flooring works best for short-term rentals?

Luxury vinyl plank is a popular choice because it is durable, water resistant, and looks like wood. Engineered hardwood and tile also perform well in the right spaces. Avoid thick carpet in high-traffic areas because it stains and is harder to keep fresh between guests.

Should I replace flooring before listing?

If floors are damaged, heavily worn, or visibly dated, replacement can significantly improve photos and guest perception. If they are in decent shape, refinishing or deep cleaning may be enough. We evaluate the condition and cost-benefit before recommending replacement.

What kitchen updates matter most for guests?

Guests value a clean, functional kitchen with working appliances, adequate cookware, and ample counter space. Inexpensive upgrades like new cabinet hardware, painted cabinets, updated lighting, and a fresh backsplash can transform the room. A full kitchen remodel is rarely necessary unless layout or condition is poor.

Is a full kitchen remodel worth it for an STR?

It depends on the property and market. For luxury or large group homes, a high-end kitchen can support higher rates, but for most properties targeted cosmetic updates deliver a better return. We compare the remodel cost against the projected revenue lift before suggesting it.

What bathroom updates have the best return?

Updating the vanity, mirror, lighting, faucets, and showerhead creates a fresh look for a modest cost. Re-caulking, regrouting, and replacing worn fixtures also make a big difference. Guests notice cleanliness and water pressure, so quality in those basics matters.

Should I convert a tub to a walk-in shower?

It can help in some properties, particularly if the bathroom is small or the audience skews toward older travelers. For family-oriented rentals, a tub may be a selling point. Consider your target guest before making a structural change.

How important is lighting in a short-term rental?

Lighting strongly affects atmosphere, photography, and guest comfort. Layered lighting with overhead, task, and accent sources makes spaces feel warm and inviting. Replacing dated or harsh fixtures and using consistent warm bulbs is a high-impact, relatively low-cost upgrade.

What lighting upgrades do you recommend?

We often recommend replacing builder-grade fixtures, adding bedside lamps, installing dimmers, and incorporating statement fixtures over dining areas or entries. Outdoor lighting also improves safety and curb appeal. All bulbs should have a consistent color temperature for a cohesive look.

Is smart home technology worth installing?

Smart locks, thermostats, and noise or occupancy monitors can streamline operations and reduce costs. Keyless entry makes check-in easy, and smart thermostats help control energy use between stays. Be transparent about any monitoring devices and comply with platform rules.

What smart home features do guests appreciate?

Guests appreciate self check-in with a keypad lock, reliable fast Wi-Fi, streaming-ready TVs, and simple controls. Features should be easy to use without a manual. Complicated systems can frustrate guests and increase support messages.

Do I need permits for renovation work?

Permits are typically required for structural changes, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work, and sometimes for window or deck changes. Cosmetic updates like paint and furniture usually do not need them. Requirements vary by city, so check with your local building department or have a licensed contractor confirm.

Do short-term rental regulations affect renovations?

Local STR ordinances may impose safety requirements such as smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, egress rules, and occupancy limits. Some areas also require inspections or licensing. Confirm local rules before finalizing a design, especially if you plan to add bedrooms or sleeping capacity.

Can you coordinate contractors for me?

We can coordinate with trusted contractors and trades so renovation work and furnishing are sequenced properly. Having one point of contact keeps schedules aligned and reduces delays. For licensed work, we recommend hiring qualified, insured professionals and verifying permits.

How should I vet a contractor?

Check licensing, insurance, references, and past work, and get detailed written estimates from more than one contractor. Ask about timelines, payment schedules, and warranty terms. Avoid contractors who demand large upfront payments or cannot show proof of insurance.

How do timelines differ between renovating and just furnishing?

Furnishing a property typically takes a few weeks from design approval to install, depending on product lead times. Renovation adds time for demolition, construction, inspections, and finishing, which can stretch to several weeks or months. Starting furniture orders while construction progresses can help compress the schedule.

How do budgets differ between renovation and furnishing?

Furnishing is usually a fraction of the cost of significant renovation, and renovation costs are more variable because of hidden issues behind walls and floors. We recommend a contingency of 10 to 20 percent for renovation work. A clear scope up front helps keep costs predictable.

What is the difference between structural and cosmetic work?

Structural work involves load-bearing walls, foundations, roofing, and framing, and it requires engineering and permits. Cosmetic work covers surface finishes that do not affect the building's integrity. Cosmetic projects are faster, cheaper, and lower risk, so they are often the first choice for rentals.

When should I renovate before listing instead of after?

Renovate first if there are safety issues, functional problems, or finishes so dated that they would undermine your photos and reviews. It is much easier to work in an empty property than around guests. Doing it before launch also avoids lost revenue from closing the listing later.

Can I renovate while the property is already listed?

You can, but it may require blocking calendar dates and managing guest expectations. Noise and mess affect reviews, so schedule work during low season when possible. For larger projects, pausing bookings is usually the cleaner option.

What renovations add the most value for guests?

Upgrades that improve first impressions and daily comfort tend to matter most, such as fresh paint, better lighting, updated bathrooms, comfortable bedrooms, and curb appeal. Outdoor living spaces like patios, decks, and fire pits are also popular. We focus on changes tied to guest experience and booking performance.
