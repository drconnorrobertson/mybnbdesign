# Category guides and utility workbooks

`curate-indexing.py dist` is a final-output editorial step. The 14 category guides
receive original category-specific planning guidance, component selection
checklists, clearer metadata and links to the roadmap, services and portfolio.

The 128-component x 19-stage workbook library remains usable with its worksheet
downloads and navigation. Those 2,432 shared-protocol utilities receive
`noindex, follow` and are excluded from all deployed sitemaps. Category owners and
the reference index stay indexable. Existing product instructions and source
disclosures remain in place; these pages are not presented as field-tested
product reviews or documented client outcomes.

Build checks require the expected component/stage inventory, preserved downloads,
no utility URLs in sitemaps, and every category owner in the sitemap. The standard
indexability, structured-data and brand gates run after this step.

Judge indexing changes against eligible guide URLs, non-brand impressions and
qualified design inquiries. Removal of utility URLs from search can reduce raw
index counts without indicating that valuable service pages lost coverage.
