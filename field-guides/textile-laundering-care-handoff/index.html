Hand off textile care information to the cleaning team

Textile choices affect the daily cleaning workflow. Preserve care labels and product identifiers, then ask the operator to confirm that the intended laundering process is compatible. Record approved replacement colors and sizes so replenishment does not gradually alter the room or introduce mismatched sets.

Work through the decision

  1. Save the original care instructions.
  2. Confirm the operator can follow them.
  3. Identify spare sets and storage.
  4. Record substitutions separately from the standard specification.

For each action, name the person responsible for preparing the response and the person who approves it. Record what evidence would close the item. If new information changes cost, timing or scope, update the affected plan and identify who needs the revised version.

Common failure to avoid

Do not invent a universal laundering temperature or treatment for every fabric. The product instructions and operator capability should drive the handoff.

Close the handoff

A finished worksheet should identify the result, its evidence and any remaining owner. If the result is uncertain, record the next action instead of marking the whole task complete. Keep the worksheet with the relevant source file and tell the receiving person which version applies.
