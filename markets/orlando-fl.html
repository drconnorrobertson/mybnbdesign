Local Design Trends

Design Trends in Orlando, Florida

Orlando is the most visited destination in the United States, welcoming over 74 million visitors annually. The short-term rental market here is dominated by properties that cater to families visiting Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, and SeaWorld. Design trends lean heavily toward bright, open spaces with a resort feel that transports guests from the moment they walk through the door.

The most successful Orlando rentals feature themed rooms that delight younger guests without alienating adults. Think subtle nods to adventure and fantasy through wall murals, custom headboards, and creative lighting rather than garish character posters. The living areas should feel like a boutique hotel lobby with comfortable sectional sofas, statement lighting, and an open floor plan that connects the kitchen, dining, and living spaces.

Color palettes in top-performing Orlando properties draw from the surrounding subtropical landscape. Warm whites, soft teals, coral accents, and natural wood tones create a cohesive aesthetic that photographs beautifully for listings. Palm leaf prints, rattan furniture, and tropical greenery reinforce the vacation atmosphere without feeling kitschy.

Outdoor living is essential in Orlando. Properties with well-designed pool decks, covered lanais with comfortable seating, and outdoor dining areas consistently outperform those without. String lights, outdoor ceiling fans, and weather-resistant cushions in coordinating colors extend the living space and create Instagram-worthy moments that drive organic marketing.

Smart home technology is increasingly expected in this market. Keyless entry, smart thermostats, and voice-controlled lighting systems appeal to tech-savvy families and reduce operational headaches for remote hosts. The best designs integrate these systems seamlessly into the aesthetic rather than treating them as afterthoughts.

Expert Recommendations

Design Recommendations for Orlando

01

Install a game room or media room with a large screen, comfortable theater seating, and a selection of board games. This single room can justify a $30 to $50 premium on nightly rates.

02

Choose furniture with performance fabrics that resist stains from sunscreen and pool water. Indoor-outdoor rugs in living areas handle heavy foot traffic while maintaining visual appeal.

03

Create a dedicated coffee bar area with a quality espresso machine, stylish mugs, and open shelving. This small investment delivers outsized guest satisfaction and review mentions.

04

Use blackout curtains in all bedrooms to accommodate guests adjusting to different time zones or returning from late-night park visits. Layer with sheer curtains for daytime light control.

05

Design the master suite as an adult retreat with a king bed, quality linens in neutral tones, and spa-inspired bathroom finishes. Parents need a sanctuary after long theme park days.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average daily rate for short-term rentals in Orlando, Florida?

The average daily rate for well-designed short-term rentals in Orlando, Florida is approximately $185. Properties with professional interior design and strategic staging typically command 30 to 50 percent above this average, making design investment one of the highest-ROI improvements available to rental property owners.

What design style works best for vacation rentals in Orlando?

The most successful vacation rentals in Orlando feature a tropical contemporary with playful accents aesthetic. This style resonates with the local market and guest expectations while creating interiors that photograph beautifully for listing platforms like Airbnb and VRBO.

What types of rental properties are most popular in Orlando, Florida?

Popular short-term rental property types in Orlando, Florida include theme park condos, resort-style homes, luxury villas with private pools, townhomes near Disney. Each property type benefits from design tailored to its specific architecture, guest demographic, and competitive positioning within the local market.
