Local Design Trends in Savannah

Savannah interiors celebrate the city's layered history through a lens of Southern Gothic romance and genteel elegance. Dark, dramatic color palettes set against bright white trim and high ceilings create striking contrast. Original hardwood floors, ornate plaster moldings, and tall windows with working shutters anchor the historic aesthetic. Velvet upholstery, antique mirrors, and crystal chandeliers add richness without excess. The city's famous squares and moss-draped oaks inspire organic, garden-connected design elements. Properties that balance historic grandeur with modern comfort and convenience achieve the strongest booking performance.

Understanding these local design trends is essential for positioning your property competitively in the Savannah GA market. Design Recommendations for Savannah Properties

Embrace deeper wall colors like navy, emerald, or charcoal in at least one main room for dramatic Southern style. Preserve and restore original architectural elements including transoms, pocket doors, and plaster medallions. Choose velvet or heavy linen upholstery in rich jewel tones for primary seating. Install a statement chandelier or pendant in the dining room and entry. Add antique or vintage mirrors to amplify light in darker historic rooms. Use fresh or high-quality faux greenery including magnolia, fern, and Spanish moss references. Create an inviting courtyard or garden seating area, as outdoor space is premium in the historic district. Stock quality barware and cocktail essentials, as Savannah's open-container laws make cocktail culture central to visits. Frame vintage Savannah maps or botanical prints as wall art.

These design strategies have been proven across dozens of successful Savannah vacation rental properties. The key is consistency of quality and authenticity of place. Every element should feel intentional, from the welcome experience at the front door to the final details in the smallest bedroom. Guests notice the effort, reward it in reviews, and pay premium rates for properties that deliver a complete, well-designed experience.
