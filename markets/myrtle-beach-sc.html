Local Design Trends

Design Trends in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

Myrtle Beach is one of the most visited beach destinations on the East Coast, attracting over 20 million visitors annually. The short-term rental market spans a wide range from budget-friendly condos to upscale oceanfront homes, and design is the key differentiator that allows properties to command rates above the local average.

Southern hospitality meets coastal living in Myrtle Beach's most successful rentals. The design aesthetic here blends the warmth and charm of Southern interiors with the light, relaxed feel of beach living. Soft color palettes, comfortable furnishings, and welcoming touches like fresh flowers and quality linens create the inviting atmosphere that guests remember and review positively.

The Grand Strand's architectural heritage leans toward traditional and transitional styles, which influences interior design preferences. Raised panel cabinetry, turned wood details, and classic furniture silhouettes appeal to the market's primary demographic of families and retirees from the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic states.

Golf is a major draw in Myrtle Beach, with over 80 courses in the area. Properties near golf courses should acknowledge this lifestyle in their design without becoming a themed golf lodge. Subtle references through color choices, artwork depicting local landscapes, and comfortable den-style rooms for relaxing after rounds are more effective than golf memorabilia.

Myrtle Beach properties must withstand significant wear from high occupancy rates, particularly during the summer peak season. Design choices should prioritize materials and finishes that maintain their appearance through hundreds of guest stays per year. This means commercial-grade hardware, solid surface countertops, and furniture built for hospitality rather than residential use.

Expert Recommendations

Design Recommendations for Myrtle Beach

01

Layer soft blues, seafoam greens, and warm whites with accents of coral and natural wood. This palette reflects both the Southern charm and coastal setting that define the Myrtle Beach experience.

02

Invest in a statement headboard for the master bedroom, whether upholstered in a durable fabric, crafted from reclaimed wood, or featuring a woven seagrass design. This single piece anchors the most photographed room.

03

Install wainscoting or beadboard paneling in bathrooms and dining areas. These classic Southern architectural details add character and visual interest while protecting walls from moisture and scuffing.

04

Choose dining tables that seat the full guest capacity of the property. Myrtle Beach travelers are primarily families and groups who want to share meals together, making the dining area a critical gathering space.

05

Provide a well-organized game closet or entertainment area with beach toys, puzzles, cards, and family games. This amenity drives repeat bookings and generates positive review content consistently.
