Why Bend Is a Top Short-Term Rental Market

Bend has emerged as one of the Pacific Northwest's hottest vacation rental markets, fueled by world-class skiing at Mt. Bachelor, hundreds of miles of mountain biking trails, a thriving craft brewery scene, and stunning high-desert scenery. The city attracts a young, affluent demographic that values design-forward accommodations. Bend's four-season appeal creates consistent year-round demand, and properties with modern, Instagram-worthy interiors command significant premiums over basic furnished units.

Bend consistently ranks among the most profitable vacation rental markets in Oregon. Travelers come for the unique experiences and natural beauty, and hosts who invest in professional interior design see measurably higher nightly rates, better reviews, and stronger occupancy throughout the year. The difference between a property that earns average revenue and one that commands premium pricing often comes down to one factor: design quality.
