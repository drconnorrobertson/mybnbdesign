The Key West Short-Term Rental Market

Key West is a small island with outsized appeal. Its historic Old Town, sunsets, fishing, and festive atmosphere attract couples, friend groups, and celebrations year-round. Strong demand and extremely limited supply create some of the highest nightly rates in Florida.

The city strictly regulates vacation rentals with licensing and a cap on transient rental units, so licensed properties carry a major premium. Conch-style homes, cottages, and small guest houses are the dominant property types and are prized for their character.

At MyBnBDesign, we treat every market as its own design problem. A listing in Key West competes against a specific set of local alternatives, serves a specific guest, and has to survive specific wear and weather. That is why our short-term rental design services start with local research before any furniture is chosen.

Common Property Types in Key West

Understanding which property types dominate a market tells us how guests search and what they expect. In Key West, the most common short-term rental properties include:

Old Town conch houses

Historic wood-frame homes with porches and gardens.

Cottages and bungalows

Small, charming homes with pools or courtyards.

Condos

Units in resort-style complexes with amenities.

Waterfront homes

Typical property prices. Typical prices range from about $800,000 for a small condo to $2.5 million or more for an Old Town home. Because purchase prices and renovation budgets vary this much, we scope each design plan to the property, the guest, and the target rate rather than applying a one-size-fits-all package.

Who Books Airbnbs in Key West

Guests include couples, friend groups, wedding parties, LGBTQ+ travelers, fishing enthusiasts, and festival attendees. Designing for the real guest, not a generic traveler, is the first step to higher conversion. A couple on a weekend trip judges a listing differently than a family of eight, and the photos, bed configurations, and amenities should answer the questions that guest is asking.

Peak seasons. Winter from December through April is the peak season, with Fantasy Fest in October another major draw. Summer is slower but includes lobster season and fishing events. We plan layouts, bedding, and styling so a property is ready for its highest-demand weeks, and we recommend pricing strategies that match those windows.

Local Competition and Why Design Matters in Key West

Key West rates are high, so guests expect charm, quality, and a clear sense of place. Because supply is limited, existing licensed homes compete mostly on design and experience. Courtyards, pools, porches, and walkability to Duval Street are top selling points, and thoughtful styling elevates both photos and reviews.

In every market, the guest decision happens in seconds. The first photo, the cover image, and the first five images in the gallery drive click-through rate, and the overall feel of the home drives review language. Better design improves both. Operating Considerations for Key West Hosts

Design works best when it supports operations. In Key West, we recommend choosing finishes that clean quickly between turnovers, stocking duplicate linen and towel sets so housekeeping never limits your calendar, and using layered, dimmable lighting so the home photographs well by day and feels welcoming at night. Smart locks, reliable Wi-Fi, and clear wayfinding in the home reduce guest questions and protect your review average. Always confirm local permits, tax registration, insurance, and any HOA rules before you finalize a design budget, since these affect which layouts and capacities you can legally market.

Key West Airbnb Design FAQ

Are vacation rentals regulated in Key West?

Yes. Licenses are limited and rules are strict, so confirm the transient rental license status of any property.

When is peak season?

December through April, with Fantasy Fest in October also very strong.

What design fits Old Town homes?

Key West conch and tropical designs that respect the historic architecture.

What features command premiums?

Pools, courtyards, porches, walkability, and parking.
