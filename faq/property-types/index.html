Property Type FAQ

Designing for Every Type of Short-Term Rental

Every property type has its own guests, challenges, and opportunities. Explore how MyBnBDesign tailors design for apartments, cabins, beach houses, luxury estates, and everything in between.

25 questions answered by the MyBnBDesign team

How do you design an urban apartment for short-term rental success?

Urban apartments live or die by space efficiency and guest convenience. We focus on multifunctional furniture such as sofa beds with real mattresses, fold-down dining tables, and vertical storage so the unit feels larger than its square footage. We also layer in sound-dampening rugs and curtains, blackout shades, and a dedicated workspace, since city guests are often a mix of business and leisure travelers.

What makes beach house design different from other STR properties?

Beach houses face salt air, sand, humidity, and heavy sun, so material choice matters as much as style. We specify performance fabrics, powder-coated or marine-grade hardware, washable slipcovers, and durable indoor-outdoor rugs that handle sandy feet. We also build in practical touches guests love, like an outdoor rinse station, a mudroom-style entry with towel hooks, and beach gear storage, while keeping a light, coastal palette that photographs beautifully.

How do you design a mountain cabin that feels cozy but not cluttered?

The goal is rustic warmth with modern comfort. We combine natural textures like wood, stone, wool, and leather with a restrained color palette so the space feels intentional instead of themed. Layered lighting, a statement fireplace or stove area, oversized seating, and plenty of throw blankets create the cozy factor, while clean surfaces and smart storage keep it easy for cleaners to turn over between stays.

What should I know about designing a lake house for guests?

Lake houses perform best when the design connects the indoors to the water. We maximize views with simple window treatments, arrange seating to face the lake, and create a transition zone for wet swimsuits, life jackets, and towels. Durable, quick-drying fabrics, plenty of sleeping capacity for groups, and a strong outdoor entertaining area with a fire pit and dining space are the features that tend to drive repeat bookings.

Can you design a desert retreat that stands out on Airbnb?

Absolutely. Desert properties reward bold, atmospheric design, so we lean into earthy tones, natural materials, and statement outdoor spaces for stargazing. We plan for extreme sun and temperature swings with UV-resistant fabrics, efficient shading, and comfortable indoor-outdoor zones such as a fire feature, cooling misters, or a plunge pool. Distinctive photography-ready moments, like a sunset lounge or outdoor shower, often become the hero images that set the listing apart.

How do you approach ski chalet design?

Ski chalets need to handle a lot of gear and a lot of people. We design a functional entry with boot trays, heated drying space, hooks, and benches, then build a comfortable great room where groups can gather after the slopes. Hot tubs, bunk rooms, durable flooring, and plenty of bathrooms are major value drivers, and we style everything with warm, layered textiles so the chalet photographs well in both winter and summer listing photos.

What are the key considerations for a rural farmhouse STR?

Rural farmhouses sell on authenticity, space, and escape. We preserve original character through reclaimed wood, vintage-inspired pieces, and quality linens, while updating kitchens and bathrooms to hotel-level standards. We also design for practical rural realities such as mudrooms, outdoor gathering areas, and durable materials that hold up to dirt and pets, and we make sure the property is easy to navigate with clear signage and reliable Wi-Fi.

How do you design a suburban home for short-term rental guests?

Suburban homes typically attract families and groups, so we prioritize sleeping capacity, a functional kitchen, and flexible common areas. We often add a game room, backyard seating, or a children's nook to give the home a clear hook beyond being a place to sleep. Because neighbors are close, we also design with quiet hours in mind, using outdoor spaces that are easy to manage and décor that blends with the neighborhood.

How do you handle condo and HOA restrictions in your designs?

We start by reviewing your HOA rules, building bylaws, and any rental policies before any design decisions are made. That covers things like approved flooring underlayment, window coverings, noise limits, balcony décor, and common-area access. Designing within these constraints from the beginning avoids costly rework, and we can help you document approvals so your plan stays compliant and your relationship with the board stays positive.

Can you design multi-unit or duplex and fourplex STR properties?

Yes, and multi-unit properties are a great fit for a cohesive design system. We create a consistent brand and a shared furniture and décor palette across units, which reduces costs, simplifies restocking, and makes replacement pieces easy to source. We then add subtle variations so each unit feels distinct in the listing, and we plan shared spaces like courtyards, laundry areas, and entry points to help the whole property feel professionally managed.

Do you design tiny homes and how do you make them feel spacious?

Tiny homes are one of our favorite design challenges. We use custom or built-in storage, lofted sleeping areas, light color palettes, and full-height mirrors to expand the sense of space. Every item has to earn its footprint, so we choose compact appliances, convertible furniture, and thoughtful lighting, and we extend living space outdoors with decks, seating, and fire features that become part of the guest experience.

What goes into designing a yurt or glamping property?

Glamping design balances adventure with true comfort. We focus on high-quality bedding, weather-resistant furnishings, reliable heating and cooling solutions, and a private bathroom or a clean, well-lit shared facility. Atmosphere is the product here, so we invest in warm lighting, natural textures, and outdoor lounge areas, while using durable materials that stand up to moisture, dust, and seasonal temperature shifts.

Can you work with unique properties like treehouses, A-frames, or converted buses?

Yes. Unique properties get booked on novelty, so the design should amplify what makes them special instead of competing with it. We identify the single most photogenic feature, whether it is a window wall, a loft, or a view, and build the design around it. We also solve the practical challenges of unusual layouts, such as limited storage and unconventional bathrooms, so guests have a memorable stay that still works smoothly.

How is luxury estate design different from standard STR design?

Luxury guests expect hotel-plus service and details, so every touchpoint matters. We specify premium bedding, spa-style bathrooms, curated art, high-end kitchen equipment, and layered lighting controls, and we plan entertainment spaces like a media room, wine area, or pool pavilion. We also create a refined arrival experience with welcome details and clear service standards, which supports higher nightly rates and strong reviews.

How do you design a pet-friendly STR?

Pet-friendly design means welcoming animals while protecting the property. We choose scratch-resistant, easy-to-clean flooring, performance fabrics, and washable throws, and we avoid delicate materials in high-traffic areas. Thoughtful extras like a pet station with bowls, a designated sleeping spot, a fenced yard or waste station, and clear house rules give pet owners confidence, and these listings often enjoy higher occupancy because supply is limited.

Can you incorporate ADA accessible design into my short-term rental?

Yes. Accessible design opens your listing to a larger market and can be built in without making the space feel clinical. We consider wider doorways, step-free entries, roll-in showers, grab bars that look like designer towel bars, lever hardware, and reachable storage and switches. We also recommend consulting a local accessibility professional for code requirements, and we coordinate our design plan with their guidance.

What is the difference between family-friendly and adults-only design?

Family-friendly design prioritizes safety, durability, and flexibility. That means rounded edges, stain-resistant fabrics, bunk rooms, kids' dining areas, and plenty of closed storage for toys and gear. Adults-only design leans into atmosphere, with statement lighting, richer palettes, bar or lounge areas, and spa-inspired bathrooms. We help you choose the approach that matches your market and then commit to it so the listing speaks clearly to its ideal guest.

Should I design my STR for a specific guest type?

Yes, we almost always recommend it. Properties that try to appeal to everyone often end up memorable to no one, while a clear guest focus lets you make smarter choices about bedding configuration, amenities, and styling. We analyze your local market, competitor listings, and booking data to identify the strongest niche, then build a design concept around it that supports higher rates and better reviews.

How do you design for properties with large groups in mind?

Group properties need smart sleeping math and shared space that actually works. We maximize bed count with a mix of king rooms, bunk rooms, and quality sofa sleepers, and we make sure bathrooms, seating, and dining capacity match the headcount. We also add group-friendly features such as oversized tables, game areas, extra refrigeration, and outdoor gathering zones, since these are common reasons large parties choose one listing over another.

Can you design a property with a pool or hot tub?

Yes, and these features deserve a full design plan of their own. We create comfortable lounge and dining zones, shade options, safe nighttime lighting, and towel storage close to the water, and we select weather-proof materials that resist chlorine and sun. Clear signage and a simple guest guide for operation and safety also help protect your guests and your liability exposure.

How do you adapt design for different climates?

Climate shapes everything from fabrics to layout. In humid regions we choose breathable, mold-resistant materials, while in cold regions we focus on insulation-friendly textiles, drying space, and efficient heating. In hot, sunny climates we specify UV-resistant finishes and shading, and for seasonal markets we plan furnishings that transition well between summer and winter so the property performs year-round.

Do you design for properties in historic or older buildings?

We do, and older buildings often have the character guests love. We preserve original details like moldings, hardwood floors, and architectural features, then update the less glamorous elements such as lighting, bedding, and bathrooms. We also account for quirks like small closets, uneven floors, or limited electrical capacity, and we choose furnishings that complement the building instead of overwhelming it.

What is your approach to second homes that I also use personally?

Dual-use homes require a plan that works for both you and your guests. We create a design that feels personal and welcoming while setting aside locked owner closets or storage for your belongings. We also choose durable, easy-care furnishings so the home holds up to rental use, and we keep your favorite pieces in spaces that guests will treat with care.

Can you help with a property I have not purchased yet?

Yes, many investors bring us in during the due diligence phase. We review the floor plan and photos to estimate furnishing costs, identify layout changes that increase sleeping capacity, and highlight features that will drive bookings in that market. This helps you build a more accurate budget and revenue projection before you commit, and it often reveals value-add opportunities that other buyers overlook.

How do you decide which property features to invest in?

We prioritize upgrades by their impact on bookings, nightly rate, and reviews, not just by personal taste. We look at which amenities top-performing listings in your market share, such as hot tubs, game rooms, or outdoor fire features, and weigh them against cost and payback timeline. We then rank recommendations so you can phase your investment in the order that delivers the strongest return.
