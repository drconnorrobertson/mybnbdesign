Furniture Sourcing FAQ

How we choose durable, beautiful furniture and furnishings that hold up to real guest use.

25 Questions Answered

Where does your furniture come from?

We source from a network of national retailers, commercial and hospitality suppliers, local makers, and trade-only vendors. This mix lets us balance style, durability, price, and lead time for each property. Every vendor is vetted for quality and reliability.

What quality levels do you offer?

We offer three general levels: value, mid-range, and premium. Each is selected for guest-use durability, with the higher tiers adding better materials, finer finishes, and longer warranties. We recommend a blend, with stronger pieces in high-traffic areas.

Which brands do you work with?

We work with a wide range of retail, contract, and trade brands rather than a single supplier, so the selection can match your style and budget. Specific brand availability varies by project and region. Your design proposal lists every product by name so you know exactly what you are getting.

Can you source custom pieces?

Yes. Custom upholstery, built-ins, headboards, dining tables, and bunk beds can be made to fit unusual spaces or create a signature look. Custom items have longer lead times and are generally non-refundable once production begins. We discuss the trade-offs before recommending them.

How do you ensure furniture is durable for STR use?

We select pieces with solid frames, reinforced joints, commercial-grade fabrics, and finishes that resist scratches and stains. STR furniture sees far more wear than a typical home, so we avoid delicate materials and trendy but fragile items. Durability is a primary filter in every selection.

What materials are best for heavy guest turnover?

Performance fabrics, leather alternatives, solid wood, metal frames, and quartz or laminate surfaces tend to perform best. We avoid light, untreated linen on seating and glass-heavy pieces that chip easily. The right materials reduce replacement costs and cleaning time.

What does guest-proof furniture mean?

Guest-proof means furniture and finishes that withstand spills, luggage, kids, pets, and constant use without looking worn. Examples include stain-resistant upholstery, washable slipcovers, rounded corners, and tip-resistant designs. It keeps your property looking fresh between professional refreshes.

Are your mattresses suitable for STR guests?

Yes. We choose medium-firm mattresses that suit most sleepers, paired with waterproof protectors for hygiene and longevity. Sleep quality has a direct effect on reviews, so we treat this as a key investment. Options range from quality hybrid foam to premium hotel-style beds.

What is your replacement policy for damaged items?

Items damaged in shipping or defective on arrival are replaced through the vendor with our coordination. For normal wear and guest damage after installation, replacements are ordered at cost, and we can help you reorder identical items. Your project agreement outlines the specifics.

Will I be able to reorder matching pieces later?

Yes. We keep a record of every product, color, and size installed in your home. This makes it easy to replace a single chair or lamp without redesigning the room. We also recommend buying a few spare pieces for high-wear items.

What warranties apply to furniture?

Warranties come from each manufacturer and vary by product and brand. Many pieces carry limited warranties against defects, while commercial-grade items often have longer coverage. We provide warranty details in your closeout documents so you can file claims if needed.

Do you offer eco-friendly furniture options?

Yes. We can source pieces made from FSC-certified wood, recycled materials, low-VOC finishes, and sustainable fabrics. Eco-friendly choices can appeal to a growing segment of guests and support your listing story. We will note the sustainability features of each recommended item.

Can I get a specific style, such as modern or rustic?

Yes. We design in styles like modern coastal, mountain lodge, farmhouse, boho, mid-century, and contemporary luxury. We choose pieces that fit both the style and the setting so the property feels authentic to its location. You approve the style direction before we buy anything.

How do you choose a style for my market?

We study your location, nearby competitors, and likely guest profile, then recommend a style that makes your listing stand out without feeling out of place. A lakeside cabin and a downtown loft should feel very different. Local character is a major factor in strong STR design.

Can you mix different styles in one property?

Yes, when done with intention. We unify mixed styles through a consistent color palette, material repetition, and balanced scale so the home feels collected rather than chaotic. Mixing can add personality and depth, especially in larger properties.

What outdoor furniture do you recommend?

We recommend weather-resistant materials such as powder-coated aluminum, teak, synthetic wicker, and outdoor-rated cushions. Outdoor areas are often a main selling point, so we design them as true extensions of the living space. Fire pits, dining sets, and lounge seating are among the most requested items.

How do you protect outdoor furniture from weather and theft?

We select UV-resistant, rust-resistant materials and recommend covers or storage solutions for off-season. For theft deterrence, heavier pieces, anchoring, and discrete locking systems can help. Your care guide includes seasonal maintenance tips.

Do you supply hot tub and pool area furnishings?

Yes. We can outfit hot tub and pool areas with loungers, towel storage, side tables, shade options, and durable decor. These amenities can significantly boost appeal, so the furnishings should look inviting and withstand moisture. We also advise on safety and signage where appropriate.

What kitchen essentials do you provide?

We stock a fully equipped kitchen with cookware, bakeware, knives, utensils, dinnerware, glassware, coffee equipment, and small appliances. The quantities are scaled to your maximum guest count. A well-stocked kitchen is one of the most mentioned features in positive reviews.

How many sets of dishes and glasses do you recommend?

We usually recommend enough place settings for the maximum guest count plus a few extras to cover breakage and dishwasher cycles. Durable, stackable, dishwasher-safe items work best. We provide a complete inventory list for your records.

What linens and textiles are included?

We provide sheets, duvets, pillows, mattress protectors, bath towels, hand towels, washcloths, bath mats, and throw blankets. We choose hotel-quality materials in neutral or coordinated colors that hold up to frequent laundering. Extra sets are recommended so turnovers run smoothly.

How many linen sets should I have for each bed?

The standard recommendation is three sets per bed: one on the bed, one in the wash, and one ready to swap. High-occupancy properties may benefit from a fourth set. We include this guidance in your supply plan.

Do you offer white or colored linens?

Both are possible. White linens look crisp and hotel-like, and can be bleached for stain removal, while colored or patterned textiles hide wear and add style. We select based on your cleaning process and design concept.

Can I reuse furniture I already own?

Yes. If existing pieces are in good condition and fit the design, we can incorporate them to save budget. We will tell you honestly if a piece does not meet STR durability or style standards. The goal is a cohesive result, not a complete replacement for its own sake.

What items do you recommend for storage and cleaning efficiency?

We include closets with owner-lock options, labeled bins, linen storage, and easy-to-clean flooring and surfaces. Smart storage keeps guest areas tidy and simplifies turnovers for your cleaning team. Small details like hooks and luggage racks also prevent damage to walls and furniture.
