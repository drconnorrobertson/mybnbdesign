Choosing and specifying STR furniture FAQ

Use these answers to prepare a clear rental-design decision. The scope is choosing and specifying str furniture faq, with practical evidence to gather from your actual property and proposal. Prices, timelines and tax treatment are not universal across projects.

Where should furniture selection begin?

Begin with the guest brief, retained inventory and measured room plan. Define the activities the furniture must support, such as group dining, reading or unpacking. Product shopping follows those requirements. A collection of attractive individual items does not automatically create a usable room or a complete guest-ready property.

How do I compare seating capacity?

Test the complete occupied arrangement using actual product dimensions. Include nearby surfaces, door movement and routes. A nominal seat count or a staged photograph does not establish how the room works for your intended group. Check the actual seated positions and review the result when a product or layout changes.

What does a useful product schedule include?

Record the exact item and variant, supplier, quantity, dimensions, finish, destination room and care information. Add delivery and installation responsibilities, approved alternatives and the date of approval. Keep retained items in the same room schedule so purchasing decisions can be checked against the full arrangement.

How should material suitability be checked?

Read the exact product’s intended-use and care information, request a sample where appropriate and involve the operating team in a permitted cleaning trial. A broad label such as durable or performance does not answer every care, warranty or installation question. Retain the evidence that supports the selection rather than relying on the label alone.

Who verifies dimensions before ordering?

Name the person responsible for site measurements and identify how the design team resolves discrepancies. Include the delivery path and relevant packaging information with the room dimensions. Confirming that the assembled furniture fits the floor plan does not establish that it can reach the room or be installed under the actual conditions.

When is the furnishing assignment complete?

Use a written acceptance standard for the chosen scope. It may be a complete design handoff, assembled furniture or a stocked and tested rental. Record remaining work, responsibilities and dates on a punch list. A delivered box or a final rendering is not equivalent to every possible finished condition.
