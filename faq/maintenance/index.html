Maintenance FAQ

Ongoing STR Maintenance and Design Refresh

A great design is only the beginning. Learn how to protect your furnishings, keep guest reviews strong, and refresh your short-term rental so it keeps earning year after year.

25 questions answered by the MyBnBDesign team

How often should I refresh my STR design?

Most successful hosts do a light refresh every 12 to 18 months and a deeper update every three to five years. Guest reviews, photo quality, and booking trends are your best signals. If reviewers mention worn furniture, dated décor, or a tired feel, it is time to act before your ranking and rates suffer.

What are signs that my STR needs a design refresh?

Common signs include declining occupancy or nightly rates, reviews that mention wear or outdated style, and listing photos that no longer compare well with competitors. Visible issues like faded fabrics, scuffed walls, or mismatched replacements are also clear indicators. Regular self-audits help you catch these before they affect revenue.

Should I update my design seasonally?

Light seasonal updates can keep your property feeling fresh and give you new content for your listing. Swapping throw pillows, blankets, wall art, and outdoor furnishings is inexpensive and effective. We recommend a simple seasonal kit that stores easily and can be changed by your cleaner in minutes.

How do I manage normal wear and tear?

Plan for it from the start by choosing durable materials and setting aside a maintenance budget, typically a percentage of annual revenue. Conduct a quick inspection at every turnover and a deeper walkthrough each quarter. Addressing small issues like loose hardware, scuffs, and worn linens early keeps them from becoming expensive problems.

What is the protocol when a guest damages something?

Document the damage right away with timestamped photos, then contact the guest through the platform's messaging system. File a claim through the platform's resolution process or your security deposit system within the required window. Keep receipts and replacement quotes, and use our product records to replace items quickly so the property is ready for the next guest.

How do I prevent damage in the first place?

Clear house rules, quality guest screening, and durable furnishings all reduce risk. Features like protective mattress covers, furniture pads, door stops, and sturdy hardware help prevent common problems. Thoughtful design also guides guests naturally, for example by placing a landing zone near the entry so bags and shoes stay off furniture.

How do you help me manage inventory?

We provide a detailed inventory list of every item in the property, including product details, quantities, and where to reorder. A simple spreadsheet or app lets your cleaner mark missing or damaged items after each stay. This makes replacement fast and keeps the property consistent with the original design.

Which essentials need regular restocking?

Consumables like toilet paper, paper towels, soap, trash bags, laundry detergent, and coffee should be checked every turnover. Linens, towels, and kitchen items need periodic replacement as they wear out. We provide a par-level guide that tells you how many of each item to keep on hand.

How many sets of linens and towels should I keep?

A reliable rule is three full sets per bed: one on the bed, one in the wash, and one in storage. Towels follow a similar principle, with extra for larger groups. Having enough stock prevents delayed turnovers and reduces wear since items rotate more evenly.

What does a good deep clean between guests include?

A standard turnover covers surfaces, floors, bathrooms, kitchens, and fresh linens. A deep clean adds details such as inside appliances, baseboards, vents, windows, and under furniture. We recommend scheduling a deep clean monthly or quarterly depending on occupancy, since cleanliness is one of the top factors in guest reviews.

How can I protect my furniture from guest wear?

Use protective covers for mattresses and pillows, felt pads under furniture legs, and washable slipcovers where appropriate. Choose performance fabrics and finishes that resist scratches and moisture. Rotating cushions and periodically checking frames and joints helps furniture last longer.

What materials are best for stain resistance?

Performance fabrics, leather or high-quality faux leather, and tightly woven synthetic blends handle spills well. For floors, luxury vinyl plank, sealed hardwood, and porcelain tile hold up to heavy traffic. We also recommend washable rugs and darker or patterned textiles in high-use areas, which hide minor stains between cleanings.

How do I handle stains and odors quickly?

Keep a small cleaning kit on site with enzymatic cleaner, upholstery spot treatment, and odor neutralizers, and teach your cleaner how to use them. Treat stains promptly since they are far harder to remove once set. For persistent odors, a professional steam clean or air purifier can restore freshness before the next arrival.

How should I maintain outdoor spaces?

Outdoor areas should be inspected at every turnover for debris, damage, and cleanliness. Seasonal tasks include pressure washing, resealing wood, cleaning cushions, servicing hot tubs, and winterizing furniture. We specify weather-resistant materials to reduce effort, but regular upkeep is what keeps outdoor spaces photo-ready.

How do I care for a hot tub or pool?

Follow a regular schedule for water testing, filter cleaning, and chemical balancing, usually handled by a professional service. Keep the area clear of slip hazards and make sure covers, lights, and safety features are in working order. A well-maintained hot tub is a major draw, while a neglected one quickly becomes a source of bad reviews.

What technology updates should I plan for?

Smart locks, thermostats, noise monitors, Wi-Fi routers, and streaming devices all benefit from periodic updates. Replace batteries on schedule, update firmware, and test your internet speed regularly since guests notice connectivity problems fast. We also recommend reviewing tech every year to retire anything outdated or unreliable.

When should I refresh my listing photos?

Refresh photos after any significant design update, and at least every two to three years even if the property looks great. Photos should reflect the current condition, seasonal appeal, and any new amenities. Fresh professional images often lift click-through rates and bookings immediately.

How do I keep up with design trends without redecorating constantly?

Build a timeless foundation with neutral walls, quality furniture, and classic layouts, then use accessories to express trends. Pillows, art, lighting fixtures, and decorative accents are inexpensive to update. This approach keeps the property current while protecting your larger investments.

Do you offer annual refresh packages?

Yes. Annual refresh packages include a design review, wear and tear assessment, and a curated list of replacements and updates for the year ahead. We can also handle sourcing and coordinate installation. This keeps your property consistent, saves you from last-minute scrambles, and helps you plan maintenance spending.

What is included in a design review?

A design review looks at your guest reviews, listing analytics, condition of furnishings, and competitor trends. We identify what is working, what is wearing out, and where small changes can have the biggest effect. You receive a prioritized action plan with estimated costs and timing.

Can you help with emergency replacements?

Yes. If a key piece is damaged right before a booking, we can help source a fast replacement. We keep records of your original selections so we can match items or recommend a close alternative. For critical needs like mattresses or appliances, we focus on rapid delivery options to protect your calendar.

How do I budget for maintenance and replacements?

A common benchmark is to set aside a portion of gross revenue for repairs, replacements, and refreshes. Properties with heavy occupancy, pets, or large groups may need to reserve more. We help you build a realistic annual plan so you can spread costs and avoid surprises.

How should I handle repairs when I am not local?

Build a reliable local network, including a handyman, cleaner, plumber, and electrician, before you need them. Use a shared maintenance log and photos to communicate issues clearly. Our team can also coordinate repairs and replacements remotely so you stay in control without being on site.

Can maintenance improve my reviews and rankings?

Absolutely. Cleanliness, accuracy of the listing, and the condition of the property are among the most mentioned factors in guest reviews. Consistent upkeep leads to better ratings, which improve search visibility and pricing power. Maintenance is one of the best investments you can make in long-term performance.

What is the best way to get started with ongoing maintenance support?

Start with a design review of your current property so we can see how it is holding up. From there, we create a refresh and maintenance plan tailored to your property type, guest profile, and budget. You can choose a one-time update or an annual package, and we are happy to explain the options on a free consultation.
