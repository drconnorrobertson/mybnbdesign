What is MyBnBDesign?

MyBnBDesign is a design and furnishing company built specifically for short-term rentals, including Airbnb, VRBO, and direct-booking properties. We handle concept, furniture selection, procurement, delivery, installation, and styling so your property is guest-ready and photo-ready. Every decision is made with occupancy, nightly rate, and review scores in mind.

How is STR design different from regular interior design?

A short-term rental has to photograph well, withstand heavy turnover, and make guests feel something within the first few seconds of a listing. Traditional residential design focuses on personal taste and long-term comfort, while STR design focuses on guest experience, durability, and cleaning efficiency. We balance all three so the space looks like a boutique stay and still holds up to hundreds of check-ins.

Who are your designers?

Our designers are STR-focused specialists who have worked on vacation homes, urban condos, cabins, and multi-unit portfolios. Each project is led by a design lead supported by a procurement coordinator and an installation team. You will know exactly who is working on your property from day one.

Do your designers have experience with Airbnb and VRBO listings?

Yes. Our team studies what drives clicks, bookings, and five-star reviews on both platforms, including hero photo composition, bed count optimization, and amenity expectations. We design every room with the listing in mind, not just the floor plan.

What types of properties do you design?

We work with single-family homes, condos, townhomes, cabins, lake houses, beach properties, urban apartments, and small multi-unit buildings. Whether you own one unit or a growing portfolio, we scale the process to fit. Larger projects can often be phased to match your acquisition and launch schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We serve STR owners across major vacation and urban markets, and we coordinate delivery and installation through local teams in each region. If your property is outside our standard service areas, contact us and we will confirm whether we can support it. Remote design planning is available for most locations.

Can you design a property if I live in another city or state?

Absolutely. Most of our clients are remote owners, and our process is built for it. You approve concepts, mood boards, and furniture selections online, and we manage delivery, installation, and final walkthrough with photos and video so you never need to be on site.

How long does a typical project take?

Most full-property projects take between six and ten weeks from approved design to a finished, guest-ready home. Design and approvals usually take one to two weeks, procurement and shipping take three to six weeks, and installation takes a few days. Lead times can vary with custom pieces and seasonal shipping demand.

Can you work with a tight launch deadline?

We can often compress timelines by prioritizing in-stock furniture and local sourcing. If you have a firm launch date, tell us during your first consultation so we can build the plan backward from it. We will be transparent about what is realistic before you commit.

What is included in your standard design service?

Our standard service includes a design consultation, a custom concept and mood board, room-by-room furniture and decor selections, a detailed budget, procurement, delivery coordination, and white-glove installation. Many packages also include final styling and a listing-ready photo checklist. See our pricing FAQ for what each tier includes.

Do you provide the furniture, or do I buy it myself?

We source and purchase the furniture on your behalf as part of our turnkey service. This lets us use trade pricing, consolidate shipping, and make sure every piece works together. If you prefer to buy certain items yourself, we can accommodate that with a design-only arrangement.

Do you handle kitchenware, linens, and decor too?

Yes. We outfit the full property including cookware, dishes, glassware, towels, bedding, throw pillows, wall art, and small touches that raise your review scores. Nothing is left for you to figure out after install day.

Do you provide a guarantee?

We guarantee that the final installation will match the approved design plan, and we will correct or replace any item that arrives damaged or does not match what was approved. Our goal is a property that looks like the concept you signed off on. Specific guarantee terms are outlined in your project agreement.

Can you guarantee more bookings or higher nightly rates?

No designer can honestly guarantee revenue, because bookings depend on location, pricing strategy, seasonality, and management quality. What we can promise is that your design follows proven STR best practices known to improve listing appeal and guest satisfaction. Many owners see stronger photos, better reviews, and improved conversion after a professional refresh.

How do we communicate during the project?

You will have a dedicated project lead reachable by email and scheduled calls, plus a shared project board where you can review designs, approve selections, and track order status. We send regular progress updates so you are never left guessing. Response times are typically within one business day.

How often will I receive updates?

During design you will hear from us at every milestone, and during procurement you will receive weekly status updates. As delivery and installation approach, we update you more frequently. You can always message your project lead for a real-time answer.

What does the first consultation look like?

The first consultation is a conversation about your property, target guests, budget, timeline, and goals. We review photos, floor plans, and your market competitors if available. You leave with a clear understanding of the recommended scope and next steps.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes, the initial consultation is free and carries no obligation. It is a chance for both sides to confirm fit and set expectations. Contact us through the link at the bottom of this page to schedule one.

What information should I bring to the first consultation?

Helpful items include your address, floor plan or measurements, current photos, target guest profile, preferred budget range, and launch timeline. A list of competing listings you admire is also very useful. If you are missing something, we can help you gather it.

What style options do you offer?

We design in a range of styles including modern coastal, mountain lodge, mid-century modern, boho, farmhouse, urban minimal, and luxury contemporary. We select the style based on your location, guest demographic, and property architecture. Our goal is a cohesive look that feels intentional and local.

Can I choose my own style or colors?

Yes. We welcome your preferences and use them as the foundation for the concept. If a preference conflicts with durability or guest appeal, we will explain the trade-off and suggest alternatives. You always have the final say.

Do you design for specific guest types, like families or remote workers?

Yes. We tailor layouts, bedding configurations, and amenities to your target guest. A family lake house needs bunk rooms, durable fabrics, and game storage, while a city condo may need a strong workspace and fast-wifi setup. Matching the home to the guest is a core part of our process.

Do you maximize sleeping capacity?

We look for smart ways to increase sleeping capacity without making rooms feel cramped, such as bunk setups, sleeper sofas, and trundle beds. More beds can unlock higher revenue, but comfort and review quality come first. We will recommend the configuration that best fits your property and market.

What about durability for heavy guest use?

Durability is built into every selection. We choose performance fabrics, commercial-grade finishes, and sturdy construction so furniture holds up to frequent turnover. Our furniture FAQ covers materials and brands in detail.

Do you offer help with the listing photos?

Yes. Our process includes final styling so the home is camera-ready, and we can coordinate professional photography as an add-on. Photos are the biggest driver of listing clicks, so we treat the photo day as a key milestone.

Do you help with staging after installation?

Every project ends with a styling pass where we arrange decor, make beds, set tables, and add finishing touches. The result is a fully staged property that is ready to be photographed and welcome its first guests.

What happens if something arrives damaged or delayed?

We inspect shipments, file claims with vendors, and arrange replacements or alternatives at no extra management cost to you. If a delay will affect your timeline, we notify you right away with options. Our process includes buffer time to absorb typical supply issues.

Do you work with property managers and cleaners?

Yes. We coordinate with your property manager, cleaning team, or co-host to make access, delivery, and handoff smooth. We can also provide a simple care guide so your team knows how to maintain each item.

Can you refresh an existing rental instead of starting from scratch?

Definitely. Many clients come to us with a furnished property that needs updated photos, better reviews, or a stronger market position. We can recommend a targeted refresh that replaces the highest-impact items while reusing pieces that still perform.

Do you offer services for multi-property portfolios?

Yes. We create repeatable design systems for portfolios so each property feels on-brand while still fitting its location. Volume pricing and coordinated purchasing can reduce costs and speed up each launch.
