The MyBnBDesign Design Process

One of the most common themes in MyBnBDesign reviews is how smooth and organized the design process feels from the client's perspective. The team follows a structured workflow that keeps projects moving without overwhelming the property owner.

The process begins with a discovery call where the design team learns about the property, the target guest demographic, the local market, and the owner's goals. From there, MyBnBDesign creates a detailed design concept that includes mood boards, furniture selections, layout plans, and a complete budget breakdown. Clients approve the concept before any purchasing begins, which eliminates surprises down the road.

"The discovery call alone was worth it. They asked questions about my market that I had never even considered, and the mood board they created captured exactly what I wanted."
— Rachel M., Mountain Cabin

Once the concept is approved, MyBnBDesign handles procurement, delivery coordination, and installation. For clients who want a fully hands-off experience, the team manages everything so the property owner simply receives a finished, photo-ready space. For those who prefer more involvement, the consultation tier provides the design plan without the full installation service.

Service Tiers and Packages

MyBnBDesign offers several service levels to match different budgets and property types. This flexibility is something reviewers frequently highlight as a reason they chose the company over competitors.

Design Consultation

Starting around $500, this tier provides a complete design plan with furniture recommendations, layout suggestions, color palette guidance, and a shopping list. The client handles purchasing and setup on their own. This is popular with budget-conscious hosts and experienced investors who want professional direction without the full-service price tag.

Furniture Packages

Ranging from approximately $5,000 to $15,000 depending on property size, furniture packages include curated selections of furnishings, decor, linens, kitchenware, and accessories. Everything is ordered, shipped, and coordinated by the MyBnBDesign team. Clients receive a move-in ready package that covers every room.

Full-Service Design

The premium tier, typically between $10,000 and $30,000, covers everything from initial concept through final installation and professional photography. This includes custom furniture sourcing, art selection, lighting design, bathroom and kitchen styling, outdoor spaces, and listing optimization recommendations. Full-service projects are managed end to end by a dedicated project coordinator.

"I went with the full-service package for my beachfront condo and it was the best decision I made. They handled everything, including details I would have never thought of, like the type of hangers in the closet and the brand of coffee maker that guests actually love."
— Sarah K., Beachfront Condo
