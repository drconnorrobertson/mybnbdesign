STR design change-control and approval FAQ

Use these answers to prepare a clear rental-design decision. The scope is str design change-control and approval faq, with practical evidence to gather from your actual property and proposal. Prices, timelines and tax treatment are not universal across projects.

What counts as an approved design decision?

Keep a dated record of the specific plan, product or finish and who approved it. A mood board, an exploratory message and a purchase authorization serve different purposes. Identify the version that controls ordering and installation so teams do not act on different screenshots of the same room.

When is a substitution acceptable?

Compare the proposed substitute with the original functional specification, dimensions, care requirements, cost and timing. Identify any related decisions it changes. Obtain the required approval before ordering, then update the product schedule. A visually similar item can still change usable seating, access or cleaning.

How should an owner-requested change be handled?

Describe the requested result, affected work, additional cost or credit and timing implications. Check whether earlier purchases can be changed under their actual terms. Keep the decision in one written record so a casual discussion does not become an assumed instruction to spend or alter the schedule.

What happens when measurements conflict?

Pause the affected decision, identify the source of each measurement and assign a person to verify the site. Update the approved plan and communicate the revision to anyone ordering or installing related items. Do not resolve a discrepancy by selecting whichever dimension makes the preferred furniture appear to fit.

How are unresolved issues tracked?

Use a punch list with item, room, observed issue, responsible party, required action and agreed date. Attach photographs or product details where they clarify the problem. Separate essential guest-use issues from optional refinements so the acceptance decision is explicit rather than implied by a photo-ready appearance.

Which records remain after handoff?

Retain approved plans, product variants, substitutions, care instructions, installation records, claims and final acceptance information. Give the operating team the version that matches the installed property. Those records make later replacements and financial reconciliation easier than a folder of unlabelled design images.
