Spring Refresh for Your Airbnb

Transform your Airbnb with a complete spring refresh featuring lighter fabrics, fresh palettes, and deep cleaning strategies to boost bookings.

By MyBnBDesign TeamOctober 02, 2026Seasonal Design

Why a Spring Refresh Matters for Your STR

Spring is the perfect time to revitalize your short-term rental property. After months of heavy winter use, your space has likely accumulated wear, and your decor may feel tired and dark. A strategic spring refresh signals to guests that your property is well maintained, current, and cared for. Booking platforms reward properties that update their photos regularly, and a spring refresh gives you the perfect excuse to reshoot your listing with bright, natural light. Guests booking spring and summer trips are actively searching right now, and a fresh listing stands out in crowded search results. The investment of a weekend and a few hundred dollars in seasonal updates can translate to measurably higher occupancy rates and nightly rates through the peak booking months ahead.

Swapping Heavy Textiles for Light Fabrics

The single most impactful spring update is transitioning your textiles from heavy winter layers to lighter, breathable options. Replace flannel sheets with crisp cotton or linen in white or soft pastel tones. Swap chunky knit throws for lightweight cotton blankets in natural fibers. Exchange heavy velvet or wool throw pillows for linen or cotton covers in spring colors like sage green, soft blush, warm sand, or sky blue. In the bathroom, switch to lighter weight towels and add a cotton bath mat. These textile swaps change the entire mood of your space without moving a single piece of furniture. Consider adding a cotton or linen duvet cover in a fresh neutral tone to brighten the bedroom. They photograph beautifully in the longer daylight hours that spring provides.

Fresh Flowers, Plants, and Natural Elements

Nothing says spring like fresh greenery and botanicals throughout your property. Place a simple arrangement of seasonal flowers on the dining table and a small potted plant on the nightstand. Add a eucalyptus bundle to the shower for a spa-like touch that guests consistently mention in five-star reviews. If you cannot maintain fresh flowers between turnovers, invest in high-quality artificial stems that look realistic in listing photos. Dried flower arrangements in neutral tones also work beautifully and last for months. Consider adding a small herb garden on the kitchen windowsill for both visual appeal and practical guest use. These botanical touches cost very little but create a welcoming atmosphere that photographs extremely well.

Deep Cleaning and Maintenance Reset

Spring is the ideal time for a thorough deep clean that goes beyond your standard turnover routine. Steam clean all upholstered furniture and carpets to remove winter buildup. Wash all curtains, duvet covers, and pillow protectors. Clean windows inside and out to maximize natural light penetration. Check all light bulbs and replace any that have dimmed or burned out. Inspect grout lines in bathrooms and kitchens and re-seal if needed. Test all appliances, smoke detectors, and carbon monoxide alarms. Service your HVAC system before summer demand begins. This comprehensive maintenance reset prevents small issues from becoming guest complaints during your busiest booking season. Document everything with a detailed checklist so you can replicate the process every spring.

Updating Your Listing Photos

After completing your spring refresh, schedule a professional photo shoot or take new listing photos during the golden hour of a sunny day. Open all curtains and blinds to flood rooms with natural light. Style each room with your new spring textiles and fresh botanicals. Include at least one photo showing any outdoor living spaces in their spring glory. Update your listing title and description to reference seasonal amenities and spring activities in your area. Guests respond strongly to listings that feel current, and updated photos consistently outperform stale ones in Airbnb search ranking algorithms. This is your best opportunity to present your property at its absolute best during peak booking season.

Budget Friendly Spring Updates

You do not need to spend thousands to create a noticeable spring transformation. Budget approximately 40 to 60 dollars on new throw pillow covers in seasonal colors. Spend 30 dollars on fresh towels in white or a coordinating accent color. Invest 20 dollars in a few potted plants or a quality artificial arrangement. Pick up a new doormat for 15 dollars and a seasonal wreath for 25 dollars. Add a new reed diffuser in a light floral or citrus scent for 15 dollars. These targeted purchases add up to under 200 dollars and deliver a property that looks and feels completely refreshed for the spring and summer booking season. The return on this small investment comes through higher occupancy and premium nightly pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to do a spring refresh?

Start your spring refresh in late February or early March, before peak booking season. This gives you time to update textiles, deep clean, photograph, and update your listing before travelers start actively booking spring and summer trips.

What are the most impactful spring design changes?

Swapping heavy winter textiles for lighter fabrics delivers the biggest visual impact with the least effort. Replace flannel sheets with cotton or linen, swap heavy throws for lightweight blankets, and update throw pillow covers in seasonal colors.

How much should I budget for a spring STR refresh?

A meaningful spring refresh can be accomplished for 150 to 300 dollars by focusing on new pillow covers, lightweight bedding, fresh plants, updated bathroom accessories, and a new doormat or wreath.
