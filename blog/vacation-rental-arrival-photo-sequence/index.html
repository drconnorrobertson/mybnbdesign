Create a guest arrival photo sequence for a vacation rental

Show the exact decisions a guest makes from parking to the entrance without exposing credentials.

Worked example

Two doors look alike from the driveway. Photograph the view from the assigned parking space, the path to the correct door and the unit marker. Ask someone new to follow the sequence with the written directions.

What to test and document

Keep codes, keys, private neighbor details and unrelated interiors out of images. Replace a photo when signage or access changes and verify the sequence still matches what guests see after dark.
