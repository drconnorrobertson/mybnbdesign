Test guest and cleaner access on a turnover day

Use separate test credentials and confirm who can enter before, during and after turnover.

Worked example

A cleaner needs early access and the next guest should enter later. Test the cleaner credential, a prior guest credential and a new guest credential from the actual door at the agreed times.

What to test and document

Record the schedule and who can approve an exception. Confirm the backup path if a credential fails, then remove the test credentials once the handoff is complete.
