Test a vacation rental arrival route after dark

Check whether guests can find the door, read the address and operate the lock under the lighting they will actually have.

Worked example

A driver can see the front light from the road but cannot read the unit number from the parking space. Try the complete walk with a bag and photograph the exact turn that needs a marker.

What to test and document

Give the property owner a punch list prioritized by the first spot a guest gets lost. Recheck the address, steps, and keypad at the intended arrival time once changes are in place.
