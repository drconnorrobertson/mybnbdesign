Modern Design for Short Term Rentals

By MyBnBDesign Team • October 02, 2026

Why Modern Design Dominates Short Term Rentals

Modern design has become the gold standard for short term rental properties. Guests searching for Airbnb stays consistently gravitate toward clean lines, neutral palettes, and thoughtfully curated spaces. As a property owner, investing in modern design is one of the smartest moves you can make to increase bookings and justify premium nightly rates.

The appeal of modern design lies in its universal accessibility. Unlike highly specific or polarizing aesthetics, modern interiors feel welcoming to travelers from all backgrounds and age groups. The style emphasizes open floor plans, natural light, and functional furniture that photographs beautifully for listing images.

Key Elements of Modern STR Design

Creating a modern short term rental starts with a restrained color palette. Think whites, warm grays, soft beiges, and occasional accent colors drawn from nature. The walls should serve as a canvas, allowing furniture and art pieces to define each room without visual clutter. Avoid busy wallpaper patterns or overly saturated paint choices.

Furniture selection is critical. Choose pieces with clean geometric lines and minimal ornamentation. A low-profile platform bed, a streamlined sofa with slim arms, and a dining table with tapered legs all reinforce the modern aesthetic. Materials like brushed metal, natural wood, and leather bring warmth without adding visual noise.

Lighting plays an outsized role in modern spaces. Recessed lighting, pendant fixtures with simple silhouettes, and floor lamps with arched profiles create layers of illumination. Smart lighting systems allow guests to adjust the mood, which elevates the overall experience and leads to positive reviews.

Modern Design on a Realistic Budget

You do not need a massive budget to achieve a polished modern look. Start with a decluttered, freshly painted space as your foundation. Invest in a quality sofa and bed since these are the two pieces guests interact with most. Fill in remaining furniture with affordable options from retailers who specialize in modern reproductions.

Art is another area where you can achieve high impact at low cost. Large-format abstract prints in simple frames create gallery-worthy moments. A single oversized piece above the sofa or bed anchors the room more effectively than a cluster of smaller items.

Textiles bring comfort and visual interest. Layer white bedding with a textured throw in a muted tone. Add linen curtains that filter light softly. Include plush towels rolled neatly in the bathroom. These small details signal quality to guests and reinforce the cohesive modern feel.

How Modern Design Boosts Your Booking Rate

Properties with cohesive modern interiors consistently outperform their competitors in search rankings and conversion rates. Airbnb's algorithm rewards listings with high-quality photos, and modern spaces photograph exceptionally well due to their clean backgrounds and balanced compositions.

Guests who stay in well-designed modern rentals leave better reviews, specifically mentioning the aesthetic quality of the space. These reviews build social proof that compounds over time, creating a virtuous cycle of higher rankings, more bookings, and increased revenue.
