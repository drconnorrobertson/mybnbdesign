Best Knife Set for Airbnb: Two Practical Options

Compare Victorinox and IKEA knife sets for an Airbnb kitchen. Check cooking tasks, dishwasher care, storage and replacement needs before buying.

By MyBnBDesign TeamReviewed November 21, 2026Buying guides

Our starting shortlist: compare Victorinox Swiss Classic 5-piece set 6.7133.5G when a dishwasher-compatible set is important, and IKEA VARDAGEN 106.024.68 when your team can reliably hand-wash and maintain wooden handles. Buy for the cooking tasks and care routine at your property, rather than the largest piece count.

This is a comparison of published manufacturer information, checked November 21, 2026. We have not conducted a hands-on knife test or measured guest outcomes. Confirm the exact model, included knives, current price and care instructions before ordering.

Choose the cooking tasks before the set

Write down what your kitchen supports: basic breakfast, family dinners or longer stays with regular cooking. A practical starting inventory is a general preparation knife, a small preparation knife and a serrated bread knife, plus enough suitable table knives for the stated guest count. This is a planning suggestion; adapt it to your property and the products you choose.

Count the actual blades, not accessories. A block, sharpener and kitchen shears may be counted as pieces in a set but do not replace missing cooking tools. If you retain an existing bread knife, a smaller new set may complete the inventory without duplicating tools. For an empty kitchen, price the complete working setup including storage and cutting boards.

Compare a set with individually replaceable knives

A matching set makes procurement straightforward, while individual knives let you replace one missing or damaged item without rebuying everything. Before choosing, search the supplier’s current catalog for the exact individual models. Record the item numbers and links in your property inventory. Availability today is not a promise of future stock, so choose an acceptable replacement process too.

A hypothetical host who already owns two sound preparation knives may need a bread knife and organized storage rather than an entire new block. Another host opening an empty property may prefer a single set with an itemized contents list. The decision depends on the retained inventory, washing routine and replacement route, not an assumed nightly-rate increase.

Fit storage to the room and the reset routine

Choose storage that protects the blades, fits the products and can be checked during turnover. Test the actual drawer dimensions or countertop position. For a wall-mounted holder, confirm the product’s installation requirements, the wall construction and the chosen location with a suitable installer. Do not treat a styling photograph as an installation instruction.

Agree on the expected inventory photograph with your cleaner. It should be easy to see which items belong in each position. Include any damaged or missing item in the punch list before the next stay; establish who removes it from service and who authorizes replacement. Follow the manufacturer’s handling and care instructions for every product.

Make the care instructions part of the purchase decision

Do not order a hand-wash-only set and assume that a note will change an established dishwasher workflow. Ask the operator how knives are washed, dried, stored and inspected today. If the new set requires different handling, agree on that change before buying. The difference between the two shortlisted sets is meaningful because the written care requirements differ.

Record the sharpening or servicing process recommended for the selected knives, rather than inventing a fixed replacement interval. A loose handle, damaged blade or other condition concern needs assessment and appropriate action; the purchase date alone does not establish that an item is ready for guests.

Compare the complete cost

Use a written list: knives, storage, cutting boards, delivery, tax, any installation and the first set of replacement items you intend to hold. Keep optional styling accessories separate. Compare like-for-like quantities and use current supplier quotes. This guide does not assume a universal kitchen allowance or a percentage of rental revenue.

Common host questions

Is a bigger knife block better for an Airbnb?

Only if the additional pieces serve actual cooking needs and your team can check and maintain them. Count useful blades, confirm storage space and compare individual replacement options.

Should every knife go in the dishwasher?

No. Use the care instructions for the exact model. The Victorinox set above is listed as dishwasher-safe; the VARDAGEN set above is hand-wash-only. Similar appearance does not establish identical care.

How often should a rental replace its knives?

Use condition checks, the manufacturer’s care guidance and your servicing process. There is no replacement interval established by this comparison.
