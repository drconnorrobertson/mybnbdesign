ROI of Professional STR Design

By MyBnBDesign Team • October 02, 2026

Understanding the True ROI of Professional STR Design

Investing in professional design for your short term rental is not an expense. It is a capital improvement that generates measurable returns month after month. Property owners who work with professional designers consistently outperform those who furnish and decorate on their own, often by margins that make the design fee look trivial in comparison.

The return on professional design investment typically manifests in three areas: higher nightly rates, improved occupancy, and reduced operational costs. When you calculate the combined impact across all three, the total return frequently exceeds 300 percent within the first year.

Higher Nightly Rates: The Most Visible Return

Professionally designed properties command premium pricing because they create premium experiences. A designer brings expertise in spatial planning, color theory, furniture selection, and styling that transforms ordinary spaces into destinations guests actively seek out and pay more to experience.

Market analysis consistently shows that professionally designed listings justify nightly rates 25 to 50 percent higher than self-styled comparable properties. For a property that might otherwise list at $150 per night, professional design can push that rate to $200 or beyond. Over 250 booked nights per year, that $50 per night difference generates $12,500 in additional annual revenue.

Improved Occupancy Through Visual Appeal

Occupancy rates improve because professionally styled properties produce superior listing photos that generate higher click-through rates. A professional designer understands how spaces will photograph and makes design decisions with the camera in mind. This is a skill set that most property owners simply do not possess.

Beyond photography, professional design creates spaces that earn better reviews, which improves search ranking and drives future bookings. The occupancy improvement from professional design typically ranges from 5 to 15 percentage points, representing significant additional revenue.

Reduced Operational Costs

Professional designers select materials, furniture, and finishes based on durability and maintenance requirements, not just appearance. This expertise reduces replacement costs, cleaning time, and maintenance headaches over the life of the property.

A designer will specify commercial-grade upholstery fabrics that resist staining, tile that hides wear, and furniture that can withstand the heavy use a rental property receives. These choices may cost slightly more upfront but save substantially over time through reduced replacement frequency.

Professional designers also optimize layouts for efficient cleaning and turnover. Every minute saved during turnover adds up over hundreds of bookings. Strategic storage solutions, easy-to-clean surfaces, and thoughtful furniture placement make the cleaning team's job faster and more thorough.

Running the Numbers

Consider a typical professional design investment of $8,000 to $15,000 for a one or two bedroom rental property. This includes design planning, furniture selection, purchasing assistance, and styling. Against this investment, calculate your expected returns. A $40 per night rate increase over 250 booked nights yields $10,000. A 10 percent occupancy improvement on a $175 average nightly rate adds another $6,000. Reduced maintenance and replacement costs save $1,000 to $2,000 annually. Total first-year return: $17,000 to $18,000 on a $10,000 investment.
