DIY vs Professional Airbnb Design

By MyBnBDesign Team • October 02, 2026

The Real Debate: DIY vs Professional Airbnb Design

Every short term rental owner faces this decision: should you design the property yourself or hire a professional? Both approaches have genuine merit, and the right answer depends on your experience, available time, budget, and revenue goals. This guide breaks down the real costs, benefits, and outcomes of each approach to help you make an informed decision.

The DIY approach offers control and cost savings. Professional design offers expertise and optimized results. Understanding where each approach excels and where it falls short will help you choose the path that makes the most financial sense for your specific situation.

The Case for DIY Design

DIY design works well when you have a genuine eye for aesthetics, available time to research and shop, and a property that does not require complex spatial solutions. Many successful rental properties have been furnished and styled by their owners using inspiration from platforms like Pinterest, Instagram, and design blogs.

The primary advantage of DIY is cost savings on design fees, which typically range from $3,000 to $15,000 depending on property size and designer experience. Those dollars can instead go directly into furniture, decor, and amenities. If you are working with a tight budget, keeping the design fee in your pocket may allow you to afford better furnishings.

DIY also gives you complete control over the timeline and decision-making process. You can make changes instantly, shop sales and thrift stores at your own pace, and adjust as you go without coordinating with a third party.

Where DIY Falls Short

The hidden cost of DIY design is time. Researching styles, shopping for furniture, coordinating deliveries, assembling items, and styling the space typically consumes 80 to 150 hours for a full property. At any reasonable hourly rate for your time, this labor cost often exceeds the professional design fee you were trying to avoid.

DIY designs also frequently suffer from common mistakes that reduce earning potential. Scale errors, poor color coordination, inadequate lighting, and photography-unfriendly layouts are issues that professional designers solve automatically but that DIY designers may not recognize.

The Case for Professional Design

Professional designers bring three things that are difficult to replicate on your own: trained spatial awareness, vendor access, and market-specific knowledge. A designer who specializes in short term rentals understands what photographs well, what guests comment on positively, and what design elements drive the highest returns.

Designers also have access to trade-only vendors and pricing that consumers cannot access. These trade discounts often offset a significant portion of the design fee. A designer might save you 20 to 30 percent on furniture purchases through trade accounts, effectively reducing the net cost of their services.

Perhaps most importantly, professional design produces results faster. What might take you months of weekends and evenings can be accomplished in weeks by a professional team. Faster completion means your property starts earning sooner, and the revenue from those additional booked weeks contributes to covering the design cost.

Making Your Decision

Choose DIY if you have design experience, ample time, a straightforward property, and a tight budget. Choose professional design if you are competing in a premium market, lack design confidence, value your time highly, or want to maximize revenue from day one.

Many owners find a hybrid approach works best: hire a designer for the planning and specification phase, then execute the purchasing and installation yourself. This captures the designer's expertise at a reduced fee while keeping your hands on the execution.
