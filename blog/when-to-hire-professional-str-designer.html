When to Hire a Professional STR Designer

By MyBnBDesign Team • October 02, 2026

Knowing When to Hire a Professional STR Designer

Not every short term rental needs a professional designer. Some properties can be successfully styled by their owners with research, patience, and a good eye. But certain situations clearly call for professional expertise, and recognizing those situations can save you time, money, and missed revenue in the long run.

The decision to hire a designer is ultimately a business calculation. If the expected return from professional design exceeds the cost by a meaningful margin, hiring makes financial sense regardless of your personal design ability. Understanding when that equation tips in favor of professional help is the key.

Signs You Should Hire a Professional

You should strongly consider hiring a designer if your property has been listed for more than three months and consistently underperforms comparable listings in your area. If competitors with similar locations, sizes, and amenities are achieving higher nightly rates and occupancy, design is likely the differentiating factor.

Complex spaces benefit enormously from professional expertise. Open floor plans that need functional zone definition, awkwardly shaped rooms, spaces with challenging architectural features, and properties requiring a complete renovation or furnishing from scratch all present challenges that designers solve daily but that most owners struggle with.

If your listing photos consistently underperform despite using a professional photographer, the problem is likely the design rather than the photography. Photographers can only work with what exists in the space. A designer creates spaces that are inherently photogenic.

Signs You Can Probably DIY

If you have previous design experience, a strong visual sense, and a straightforward space, DIY design can work beautifully. Simple rectangular rooms with standard proportions, properties in casual or budget markets, and owners with significant time to invest in research and shopping are all good candidates for self-directed design.

Properties in markets where guests prioritize location and price over design quality may not justify professional design fees. Beach houses in high-demand locations, urban studios near major attractions, and budget-friendly listings where guests expect basic accommodations can succeed with competent DIY design.

What Professional Designers Actually Provide

Beyond aesthetic expertise, professional designers bring efficiency, vendor relationships, and market knowledge that are difficult to replicate independently. A designer who specializes in short term rentals understands guest psychology, platform algorithms, and the specific design elements that drive revenue in your market.

Designers save time by making decisions quickly and correctly. What might take you weeks of deliberation and multiple returns, a designer resolves in hours. This time savings accelerates your launch timeline and gets your property earning sooner.

Trade vendor access provides financial benefits that offset design fees. Designers typically access furniture, lighting, and accessories at 20 to 40 percent below retail. On a $15,000 furnishing budget, trade pricing can save $3,000 to $6,000, substantially reducing the net cost of professional services.

How to Choose the Right Designer

If you decide to hire, choose a designer with specific short term rental experience. Interior design for owner-occupied homes requires a fundamentally different approach than design for rental properties. Ask potential designers about their experience with rental properties, their understanding of platform algorithms, and how they measure design success.

Request references from other rental property owners and ask specifically about the financial impact of the design work. A good STR designer should be able to point to measurable improvements in nightly rates, occupancy, and review scores for their previous clients.
