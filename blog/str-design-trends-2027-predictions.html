STR Design Trends 2027 Predictions

Published 2026-10-02

As 2026 draws to a close, forward-thinking STR hosts are already looking toward 2027 to understand where interior design is heading. The short-term rental market rewards those who anticipate rather than react, and the design landscape is shifting in exciting directions. Based on current momentum across hospitality, residential design, and guest behavior data, here are the predictions that will define STR design in the year ahead.

Sensory Design Takes Center Stage

The next evolution in STR design moves beyond visual aesthetics into full sensory experiences. Properties in 2027 will incorporate intentional scent profiles, curated soundscapes, and tactile diversity throughout the space. Think aromatherapy diffusers integrated into the design, acoustic panels that double as art installations, and a deliberate mix of textures that invites guests to touch and engage with their surroundings. The goal is creating a multi-sensory environment that leaves lasting impressions beyond what photographs can capture.

Hyper-Local Material Sourcing

The locally sourced trend of 2026 will deepen into true hyper-local material sourcing. Guests will gravitate toward properties built with stone quarried from nearby mountains, wood harvested from regional forests, and tiles produced by area craftspeople. This goes beyond decorative accents and into the structural and architectural elements of the space itself. Properties that can authentically claim their materials come from the surrounding landscape will command premium rates and attract design-conscious travelers.

Wellness-Integrated Spaces

Wellness tourism continues to grow, and STR design in 2027 will reflect this with spaces intentionally designed to support physical and mental health. Expect to see meditation corners with proper cushioning and ambient lighting, bathrooms designed as spa-like retreats with steam showers and soaking tubs, and kitchens equipped for healthy cooking with herb gardens on the windowsill. Even color choices will be influenced by chromotherapy principles, with calming blues and greens in bedrooms and energizing warm tones in social spaces.

Modular and Adaptable Furniture

The rigid single-purpose room is evolving into flexible, adaptable spaces. In 2027, look for modular furniture systems that allow guests to reconfigure living areas for different activities. A dining space that converts to a workspace, a living room that transforms for yoga practice, or a bedroom that accommodates different sleeping configurations. This flexibility responds to the diverse needs of modern travelers who use STR properties for work, celebration, recovery, and everything in between.

Artisan Kitchens and Dining Experiences

The kitchen will emerge as the most important design investment in 2027 STR properties. Guests increasingly want to cook and dine in, especially in vacation destinations where local ingredients are exceptional. Expect open shelving displaying beautiful cookware, oversized islands with seating, professional-grade appliances in boutique-scale kitchens, and dining areas designed for memorable meals. The kitchen that looks and feels like it belongs in a cooking show will drive bookings like never before.

Sustainable Design as Standard

Sustainability will shift from differentiator to expectation in 2027. Guests will increasingly evaluate properties based on their environmental credentials, and design choices will reflect this. Recycled and upcycled materials, energy-efficient appliances, low-VOC finishes, and water-saving fixtures will become standard rather than premium. Properties that feel genuinely sustainable without sacrificing comfort or aesthetics will earn both bookings and loyalty from environmentally conscious travelers.

Preparing Your Property Now

The smartest investment you can make heading into 2027 is establishing design flexibility. Choose foundational elements like wall colors, flooring, and major furniture that serve as neutral canvases for evolving trends. Invest in quality basics that will last, and plan to rotate accent pieces, textiles, and decorative elements seasonally. This approach allows you to stay current without major renovations. Start researching local artisans and sustainable suppliers now, as the best partnerships take time to develop and the demand for these resources will only increase.
