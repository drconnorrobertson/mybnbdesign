How Interior Design Affects Airbnb Revenue: The Data

A data-driven look at the measurable financial impact of professional interior design on short-term rental performance.

By MyBnBDesign Team October 3, 2026 Revenue & ROI 12 min read

Every short-term rental host wants more revenue, but few understand just how much money they leave on the table with an average looking interior. The gap between a professionally designed Airbnb and an undesigned one is not a matter of aesthetics alone. It is a measurable, quantifiable revenue difference that shows up in nightly rates, occupancy percentages, review scores, and search ranking position. In this article, we break down the data behind that gap, element by element, so you can see exactly where design drives dollars.

The Revenue Gap: Designed vs. Undesigned Properties

Industry analysis across major STR markets consistently reveals a significant revenue gap between properties with professional interior design and those without. On average, professionally designed short-term rentals generate between 20% and 40% more gross annual revenue than comparable undesigned properties in the same neighborhood. For a property that would otherwise earn $40,000 per year, that translates to an additional $8,000 to $16,000 annually.

This gap comes from two reinforcing factors. First, designed properties command higher nightly rates, typically 15% to 25% above the local market average for similar bedroom counts. Second, they achieve stronger occupancy, often running 10 to 20 percentage points higher than undesigned competitors. When you multiply a higher rate by more booked nights, the revenue impact compounds significantly. A property charging $175 per night at 78% occupancy earns roughly $49,800 per year. The same property without design work, priced at $140 per night with 62% occupancy, generates approximately $31,700. That $18,100 annual gap is the price of skipping design.

These figures align with broader trends in the STR interior design landscape for 2026, where hosts are increasingly treating design as a revenue strategy rather than a cosmetic upgrade.

Listing Photos and Click-Through Rates

The first place design impacts revenue is in listing photography. Airbnb's internal data has shown that listings with professional photography receive approximately 40% more bookings than those without. But photography alone is not the full story. A professional photographer capturing a poorly designed room will still produce a mediocre image. The quality of the space itself determines the quality of the photo, and the photo determines whether a traveler clicks.

Industry estimates suggest that the average Airbnb search result page generates a click-through rate of roughly 2% to 5% for most listings. Properties with standout cover photos, those featuring well-designed, visually compelling interiors, can achieve click-through rates of 8% to 12%. That means designed properties attract two to four times more potential guests into their listing page, creating a dramatically larger booking funnel from the same number of search impressions.

The most effective cover photos share certain design characteristics: strong contrast between furniture and wall colors, visible texture through layered textiles, natural light amplified by thoughtful window treatments, and a clear focal point that draws the eye. These are all outcomes of intentional interior design, not accidental arrangements. What Guests See in the First Three Seconds

Research on online booking behavior indicates that travelers spend an average of 3 to 5 seconds evaluating a listing's cover photo before deciding to click or scroll past. In that narrow window, design elements like color palette coherence, furniture scale, and lighting quality register subconsciously. Properties that pass this initial visual test earn the click. Those that do not are invisible, regardless of their location, amenities, or pricing.

Listings that rank in the top 10% for click-through rates on Airbnb tend to share three visual traits: a cohesive color story across the visible space, at least one statement piece (a distinctive light fixture, an accent wall, or a piece of artwork), and evidence of intentional styling such as arranged throw pillows, a draped blanket, or a set dining table. None of these require a massive budget, but all require design thinking.

How Design Drives Review Scores

Review scores are the lifeblood of Airbnb ranking. Listings with an overall rating below 4.7 stars face significant search penalties, and those below 4.5 stars are effectively buried. Design has a direct, measurable impact on multiple review categories, particularly "Cleanliness," "Accuracy," and the overall star rating.

Properties that undergo professional redesigns typically see their average review scores increase by 0.2 to 0.4 stars within the first 60 to 90 days after relaunch. While that may seem like a small shift, moving from 4.6 to 4.8 stars can push a listing from page three of search results to page one. Airbnb's algorithm weights recent reviews heavily, so a burst of strong post-redesign reviews creates a compounding visibility effect.

The "Cleanliness" score is particularly interesting. Properties with lighter color palettes, wipeable surfaces, minimal clutter, and streamlined furniture layouts consistently score 0.3 to 0.5 stars higher on cleanliness than properties with dark, busy interiors, even when both are cleaned to the same standard. This is because perceived cleanliness is a design outcome. A space that looks clean, with smooth surfaces, organized storage, and good lighting, reads as clean to guests. Design choices that support faster turnover and easier cleaning directly boost this score category.

The Pricing Premium for Well-Designed Properties

Across ten major U.S. short-term rental markets, properties identified as "professionally designed" or "designer" in their listings command an average nightly rate premium of 18% to 28% over the market median. In competitive urban markets like Nashville, Austin, and Scottsdale, that premium can reach 35% or higher for properties with distinctive, photogenic interiors.

To put specific numbers on this: the average nightly rate for a two-bedroom Airbnb in a mid-tier U.S. market sits around $145. A professionally designed two-bedroom in the same market can justify $170 to $185 per night, representing $25 to $40 of additional revenue per booked night. Over a year at 70% occupancy, that per-night premium alone generates $6,400 to $10,200 in additional gross revenue.

The premium is even more pronounced for properties that invest in a cohesive design concept or theme. Listings described with a specific aesthetic identity (such as "mid-century modern retreat" or "coastal farmhouse escape") outperform generic listings by an estimated 22% on nightly rate. Guests are willing to pay more for an experience that feels intentional and curated, because it signals quality across every aspect of the stay.

Occupancy Rate Improvements

Occupancy rate improvements from design work tend to follow a predictable curve. In the first 30 days after a listing relaunch with new design and photography, occupancy typically jumps by 12% to 18% compared to the prior period. By month three, once new reviews begin accumulating and the algorithm responds to improved engagement metrics, occupancy stabilizes at 10 to 20 percentage points above the pre-design baseline.

For seasonal markets, the impact is especially significant during shoulder seasons. Well-designed properties maintain stronger bookings in off-peak months because their listing photos and reviews give travelers confidence that the experience will be worth the trip, even outside peak season. Hosts in mountain and beach markets report that design improvements extend their effective booking season by 4 to 8 weeks, capturing revenue during periods when undesigned competitors sit empty.

Return on Design Investment Timelines

One of the most common questions hosts ask is how long it takes to recoup a design investment. The answer depends on market conditions, property size, and the scope of work, but industry benchmarks offer clear guidance.

For a typical one to two bedroom property, a professional STR design investment ranges from $5,000 to $15,000, including furniture, decor, textiles, and design fees. Based on the revenue increases outlined above, most hosts recover this investment within 4 to 8 months. Properties in high-demand urban or resort markets with strong year-round occupancy often hit breakeven in 3 to 5 months. Seasonal or rural properties in markets with concentrated demand may take 6 to 10 months.

After the initial payback period, the revenue uplift continues for the life of the design, typically 3 to 5 years before a refresh is needed. That means a $10,000 design investment that generates $15,000 in additional annual revenue produces a 3-year return of approximately $35,000 to $45,000 in cumulative additional income. Few investments in the STR space offer comparable returns.

Case Study Snapshots: The Numbers in Action

Urban Two-Bedroom in a Competitive Market

A two-bedroom apartment in a saturated downtown market was generating $28,500 in annual revenue at an average nightly rate of $118 and 66% occupancy. After a $9,200 design investment covering new furniture, bedding, lighting, artwork, and styling, the property relaunched with professional photography. Within 90 days, the nightly rate increased to $152, occupancy climbed to 79%, and the projected annual revenue reached $43,800. The $15,300 revenue increase represented a payback period of just over 7 months, with a first-year net gain of approximately $6,100 after accounting for the design cost.

Mountain Cabin with Seasonal Demand

A three-bedroom cabin in a mountain market earned $34,000 annually, with nearly 70% of revenue concentrated in a 16-week peak season. A $12,500 design investment focused on creating a year-round aesthetic appeal rather than a purely rustic look. Post-redesign, peak season rates increased by 20%, and off-peak bookings rose by 45%. Annual revenue climbed to $51,200, adding $17,200 per year. The design paid for itself within 9 months, and the extended shoulder season bookings reduced the property's revenue volatility significantly.

Coastal Studio Apartment

A studio apartment near a popular beach drew steady but unspectacular results: $19,000 in annual revenue, $105 average nightly rate, 49% occupancy. A focused $4,800 design refresh, including new bedding, a feature wall, updated lighting, and kitchen styling, transformed the listing photos. The average nightly rate rose to $138, occupancy jumped to 68%, and annual revenue reached $34,300. That $15,300 increase on a $4,800 investment produced a payback period of under 4 months and a first-year ROI exceeding 300%.

Which Design Elements Have the Highest ROI

Not all design investments produce equal returns. Based on industry analysis and host surveys, certain elements consistently deliver the strongest revenue impact per dollar spent.

  1. Premium bedding and linens ($300 to $800 per bedroom): Estimated ROI of 300% to 500%. High-quality bedding photographs beautifully, drives "comfort" mentions in reviews, and creates the hotel-quality feel that guests associate with premium pricing.
  2. Statement lighting fixtures ($150 to $600 per fixture): Estimated ROI of 250% to 400%. Distinctive lighting transforms a room's character in photos and creates ambiance that guests notice and comment on. A single pendant light over a dining table or a pair of sculptural bedside lamps can redefine a space.
  3. Curated artwork and wall decor ($200 to $1,000 total): Estimated ROI of 200% to 350%. Blank walls signal a generic, low-effort listing. Thoughtfully selected art creates visual interest, supports a design narrative, and gives guests something to photograph and share on social media.
  4. Kitchen styling and accessories ($150 to $500): Estimated ROI of 200% to 300%. A styled kitchen with matching dishware, quality cookware visible on open shelving, and a few decorative elements signals that the host cares about the complete guest experience.
  5. Bathroom upgrades and styling ($200 to $800): Estimated ROI of 180% to 280%. New towels, a framed mirror, quality soap dispensers, and a small plant or candle can elevate a bathroom from forgettable to noteworthy, directly impacting review scores.

The Compounding Effect of Good Design

Perhaps the most powerful aspect of design investment is how its benefits compound over time. This is not a linear relationship; it is a virtuous cycle that accelerates revenue growth the longer it runs.

The cycle works like this. Professional design creates a more photogenic property. Better photos produce higher click-through rates, pulling more guests into your listing. More traffic means more bookings. More bookings generate more reviews. More positive reviews (driven by the better guest experience that design creates) push your listing higher in search rankings. Higher rankings bring even more traffic, which produces even more bookings. Each revolution of this cycle strengthens the next.

Hosts who invest in design and then optimize their listing photography report that the full compounding effect takes approximately 4 to 6 months to mature. By that point, their search ranking has stabilized at a higher position, their review volume and score have both increased, and their pricing power has expanded. The revenue difference between month one and month six post-redesign is typically 30% to 50% greater than the initial uplift alone, because the compound effect has not yet peaked at launch.

This is also why design quality matters for the long term. A well-executed, durable design maintains this compounding cycle for years. A cheap, trend-chasing design that looks dated within 12 months breaks the cycle and forces a costly restart.

Market Trends in STR Design for 2026

The STR design market continues to mature rapidly. An estimated 35% of new Airbnb listings in major U.S. markets now launch with some form of professional design input, up from approximately 18% in 2022. This rising baseline means that undesigned properties face stiffer competition each year, and the revenue penalty for skipping design grows accordingly.

Guest expectations have shifted in parallel. Survey data from hospitality industry reports indicates that 72% of Airbnb guests now consider interior design quality "important" or "very important" when choosing a listing, compared to 54% three years ago. The bar is rising, and properties that met guest expectations in 2023 may fall short in 2026 without updates.

For hosts evaluating whether to invest in design, the data points in a single direction: design is not an optional luxury for STR properties. It is a core revenue driver that impacts every metric the platform uses to rank, price, and promote listings. The sooner a property enters the compounding cycle of good design, strong photos, positive reviews, and higher rankings, the greater the cumulative financial benefit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much more revenue can a professionally designed Airbnb generate?

Industry data suggests that professionally designed short-term rentals generate approximately 20% to 40% more gross revenue than comparable undesigned properties in the same market. This increase comes from a combination of higher nightly rates (typically 15% to 25% above market average) and improved occupancy rates (often 10 to 20 percentage points higher). For a property averaging $150 per night, this could translate to an additional $8,000 to $18,000 in annual revenue.

How long does it take to see a return on an STR design investment?

Most hosts report recovering their design investment within 4 to 8 months of relaunching their listing. Properties in competitive urban markets with strong year-round demand tend to reach ROI faster, often within 3 to 5 months. Seasonal or rural properties may take 6 to 10 months. The timeline depends on the scope of the redesign, local market conditions, and how effectively the new design is captured in listing photography.

Which design elements provide the highest ROI for Airbnb hosts?

The highest-ROI design elements for short-term rentals are typically professional-quality bedding and linens (estimated 300% to 500% ROI), statement lighting fixtures (250% to 400% ROI), and curated artwork or wall decor (200% to 350% ROI). Kitchen and bathroom updates also perform well, with modern fixtures and thoughtful styling generating strong guest satisfaction scores. Professional photography of these improvements amplifies returns further, as listing images are the single biggest driver of click-through rates.
