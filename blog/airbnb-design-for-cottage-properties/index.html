Airbnb Design for Cottage Properties: Complete Guide

How to design a cottage property for Airbnb success. Expert tips on cozy scale and nostalgic design elements.

By MyBnBDesign TeamSeptember 08, 2026Property Types

Why Cottage Properties Make Great Airbnbs

Cottage properties offer unique advantages in the vacation rental market. Their distinctive architectural character gives guests an experience they cannot find in a standard hotel.

Design Challenges Specific to Cottage Properties

Every property type comes with unique design challenges. Cottage properties present specific considerations around cozy scale and nostalgic design elements. Work with the existing architecture rather than against it.

Furniture Selection for Cottage Spaces

Choose furniture that complements the scale and character of your cottage. The architectural style suggests certain furniture families and proportions. Avoid furniture that conflicts with the architectural language.

Color and Material Palette

The color palette for a cottage should take cues from its architectural style while remaining warm, inviting, and photographable. Use natural materials like wood, stone, and metal that reinforce the design story.

Maximizing Unique Features

The architectural features that define a cottage are your biggest design assets. Highlight and frame these features rather than hiding them. Make them the focal points of your listing photos.

Photography and Listing Strategy

Photograph your cottage to showcase its unique architectural character. Lead your listing with images that communicate what type of property guests are booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I decorate a cottage for Airbnb?

Lean into the architectural character. Focus on cozy scale and nostalgic design elements. Choose furniture and decor that complement the property style.

What furniture works best in a cottage?

Furniture that matches the scale and character of the architecture. Prioritize comfort, durability, and photo appeal.

Can a cottage be profitable as an Airbnb?

Yes, cottage properties can be highly profitable because they offer unique experiences that stand out in search results.
