Airbnb couch specifications: capacity, care and replacement

Use this guide to turn the design goal into a property-specific decision. Worked examples are hypothetical; no independent product ranking or promised booking result is asserted.

Specify the actual seating requirement

Start with the advertised guest group and the way the lounge is used. Count usable seats in the complete arrangement rather than relying on a sofa’s marketing label. Check where guests place drinks, play games, watch a screen or talk. A larger couch is not automatically a better solution if it consumes circulation or leaves the rest of the room without useful surfaces.

Measure the room and delivery route

Use the actual assembled and packaging information for the product under consideration. Check doors, stairs, turns and access restrictions with the responsible delivery team. Place the sofa and other furniture on the measured plan, then test the occupied arrangement. Confirm who verifies dimensions before ordering and how a product substitution will be approved if the original is unavailable.

Evaluate upholstery with the operating team

Request the exact upholstery designation and applicable care information. Test a permitted cleaning method on an appropriate sample, and assess visible marks, drying needs and routine handling. Ask about replaceable components and compatible cushions where relevant. Do not assume a general label such as performance fabric or commercial grade establishes every care or warranty requirement for your intended use.

Worked choice: sectional or separate pieces

A hypothetical group rental can fit a sectional in the photograph but loses flexibility for conversation and cleaning access. The owner compares a smaller sofa with separately placed chairs using the same guest-capacity and reset tests. The choice follows the complete room use, product instructions and maintenance plan. This is an illustrative specification exercise, not an independent review of a particular model.

Inspect before guest use

Record delivered condition, exact variant, assembly and any defects. Follow manufacturer instructions and obtain appropriate installation help where required. Test the actual seated positions and surrounding surfaces after setup. Keep packaging and condition evidence as needed for the agreed claims process, and resolve essential problems before treating the room as accepted.

Prepare a replacement specification

Keep dimensions, upholstery, care guidance and required room functions together. Identify which aspects a substitute must preserve and who can approve it. Track observed condition rather than prescribing a universal replacement date or an arbitrary spending premium. A couch should be evaluated as part of the room’s ongoing use and maintenance, not only its purchase-day appearance.

Use the decision worksheet

Download this guide’s evidence worksheet (CSV). Complete it with the actual property information and proposals.

  • Occupied seating and room circulation
  • Exact upholstery and care trial
  • Packaging dimensions and delivery path
  • Exact products and permitted care
  • Complete landed cost and local work
  • Guest-use and turnover acceptance
  • Final record and approved substitutes

Make the decision reviewable

Keep the measured plan, actual product or deliverable specification and approval together. A useful record names the problem, the proposed change and the test that will establish whether it works. Distinguish estimated dates and costs from confirmed commitments. If the proposed item changes, review the affected room decisions rather than approving it solely because its appearance is similar.

After implementation, repeat the guest-use and operating checks and record what remains unresolved. A photograph is useful evidence of the arrangement, but it does not replace care information, installation instructions or a complete handoff. Review observed operating results before carrying the same specification to another property.
