Exterior Paint Colors for Curb Appeal

Published on October 02, 2026 by MyBnbDesign Team

First Impressions Start at the Curb

Your exterior is the first thing guests see when they arrive, and it is the thumbnail image that catches their eye while scrolling through listings. Strong curb appeal builds excitement and confidence before guests even step inside. A fresh, well-chosen exterior paint scheme signals that the property is maintained, cared for, and worth the price. It also makes your property easy to find and instantly recognizable in the neighborhood.

Exterior color choices are more permanent than interior ones. Repainting a house exterior is expensive and time-consuming, so getting it right the first time matters. The best exterior colors work with the architectural style of the home, the surrounding landscape, and the neighborhood context while still standing out in listing photos.

Winning Exterior Color Combinations

Classic White with Black Trim

Nothing beats the timeless elegance of a white exterior with crisp black shutters, doors, and trim. This combination works on virtually every architectural style from colonial to modern farmhouse. It photographs beautifully in every season and lighting condition. Use a true bright white like Sherwin-Williams Extra White SW 7006 for the body and Tricorn Black SW 6258 for trim. Add brass or matte black house numbers and a black mailbox to complete the look.

Warm Gray with White Trim and a Colored Door

A warm medium gray exterior with bright white trim creates a sophisticated canvas that lets a bold front door steal the show. Choose a statement door color like deep teal, warm red, or sunny yellow to add personality and help guests identify the property. This combination feels modern and polished. Use Benjamin Moore Chelsea Gray HC-168 for the body with Simply White OC-117 trim.

Navy Blue with White Trim

Dark navy exteriors have become increasingly popular for short-term rentals because they photograph dramatically and stand out in listings. Navy reads as confident and luxurious. Pair it with bright white trim, a natural wood or white front door, and warm brass lighting fixtures. This combination works especially well on smaller homes and cottages where the dark color adds substance and presence.

Sage Green with Cream Trim

For properties in natural settings, sage green blends with the landscape while maintaining visual interest. It reads as organic and inviting. Pair it with cream rather than bright white trim for a softer, more traditional feel. Add natural wood elements like a stained front door or cedar window boxes. This palette works beautifully on craftsman, cottage, and farmhouse-style homes.

Landscaping as Part of Your Color Scheme

Your exterior paint is only part of the curb appeal equation. Well-maintained landscaping amplifies your color choices. For white or light exteriors, add color through flowering plants and a green lawn. For dark exteriors, use white or light-colored planters and bright flowers to create contrast. Keep landscaping tidy and symmetrical for the best listing photos. Add outdoor lighting to highlight your exterior at night and create a welcoming arrival experience for guests checking in after dark.

Do not forget about the driveway, walkway, and porch. These transitional spaces should feel cohesive with your exterior color scheme. Power wash concrete surfaces, stain or paint the porch floor, and add a quality doormat. Small details like matching house numbers, a fresh welcome mat, and a potted plant by the door show guests that every inch of the property is cared for.
