Keypad vs Smart Lock for Airbnb: Which Keyless Entry System Is Right for You?

Comparing traditional keypad deadbolts to WiFi and Bluetooth smart locks for vacation rental properties.

Keyless entry is essential for modern vacation rentals, but hosts face a fundamental choice: a traditional keypad deadbolt or a WiFi-connected smart lock. Both eliminate physical key exchanges, but they differ significantly in features, cost, reliability, and management convenience. Here is a detailed comparison to help you choose the right system for your property.

Keypad Deadbolts Explained

A keypad deadbolt is a standard deadbolt lock with a numeric keypad on the exterior. You program codes directly on the lock, and those codes unlock the door when entered correctly. Keypad deadbolts do not connect to WiFi or Bluetooth. They run on batteries, typically lasting a year or more, and they operate independently of any network or app. Popular models include the Schlage BE365, Kwikset SmartCode 913, and Yale B1L. Prices range from $80 to $150 for quality models.

Smart Locks Explained

Smart locks connect to your home network via WiFi, Bluetooth, Z-Wave, or Zigbee. They offer all the features of a keypad deadbolt plus remote access, activity logging, auto-lock, temporary code scheduling, and integration with property management software. You manage everything through a phone app, and many models support voice assistants like Alexa and Google Assistant. Premium models include the Schlage Encode Plus, Yale Assure Lock 2, and August Wi-Fi Smart Lock. Prices range from $150 to $350.

Code Management Comparison

This is where the difference matters most for STR hosts. With a keypad deadbolt, you program codes by pressing a sequence of buttons on the lock itself. Adding or removing codes requires physical access to the property. Most keypad deadbolts support 10 to 30 user codes. With a smart lock, you create, schedule, and delete codes from your phone app from anywhere in the world. You can set codes to activate at check-in time and expire at checkout. This automation is the single biggest advantage of smart locks for vacation rental management.

Reliability and Failure Modes

Keypad deadbolts are remarkably reliable because they have no dependency on WiFi, cloud services, or apps. If your internet goes down, the lock keeps working. If the app servers crash, the lock keeps working. The only failure point is battery depletion, and most models give weeks of warning before batteries die. Smart locks add potential failure points. WiFi outages can prevent remote code changes. App bugs can lock you out of management features. Cloud service interruptions can disable remote access. However, smart locks still function as manual keypads when connectivity is lost, so guests can always enter their code even during an outage.

Cost Analysis

The upfront cost difference between a quality keypad deadbolt and a smart lock is $70 to $200. But the ongoing cost difference is where the calculation gets interesting. If you manage codes manually with a keypad deadbolt, factor in the time and travel cost of visiting the property to change codes between guests. If you have a cleaner who handles this, add their time cost. With a smart lock, code management takes 30 seconds per booking from your phone. For hosts managing multiple properties or high-turnover listings, the time savings from smart locks justify the higher purchase price within the first few months.

Best Use Cases for Each

Keypad deadbolts are ideal for properties where you live nearby and can easily visit to manage codes, for listings with low turnover like monthly rentals, for properties in areas with unreliable internet, and for hosts who prefer simple technology with fewer potential failure points. Smart locks are better for remotely managed properties, high-turnover listings with frequent guest changes, multi-property portfolios where efficiency matters, and hosts who use property management software that integrates with smart locks.

Our Recommendation

For serious vacation rental hosts managing properties remotely, a WiFi smart lock pays for itself quickly through time savings and operational convenience. The Schlage Encode Plus is our top pick for most installations. For hosts who manage a single nearby property with moderate turnover, a quality keypad deadbolt like the Schlage BE365 delivers reliable keyless entry without the complexity of connected technology. Whichever you choose, always maintain a physical key backup stored securely offsite for emergency access.
