Design check-in for a rental with two locked entrances

Map the street entrance and the unit door separately so guests can reach both after hours.

Worked example

A condo guest has the right keypad code for the unit but cannot get through the building entrance. Test the whole route from the street and note where a fob, entry code or staff handoff is needed.

What to test and document

Label the two doors separately in guest instructions. Include the building’s approved after-hours access procedure and a contact who can help at the first locked door.
