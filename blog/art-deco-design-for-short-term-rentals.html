Art Deco Design for Short Term Rentals

By MyBnBDesign Team • October 02, 2026

Art Deco Design: Glamour That Commands Premium Rates

Art Deco design transforms short term rentals into show-stopping destinations that command the highest rates in any market. This style, born from the glamour of the 1920s and 1930s, celebrates bold geometry, luxurious materials, and dramatic visual impact. For rental owners seeking to differentiate their property at the premium end of the market, Art Deco delivers unmatched appeal.

Guests who book Art Deco styled properties are often celebrating special occasions, planning romantic getaways, or simply seeking an elevated experience. This guest demographic tends to be less price-sensitive and more focused on aesthetic quality, making Art Deco properties particularly profitable on a per-booking basis.

The Art Deco Visual Language

Art Deco is defined by geometric patterns, symmetrical layouts, and a palette that balances drama with sophistication. Think bold chevrons, sunburst motifs, stepped forms, and fan shapes. These patterns appear in everything from tilework and wallpaper to furniture silhouettes and accessory design.

The color palette pairs rich jewel tones with metallic accents. Deep emerald green, sapphire blue, rich burgundy, and black create dramatic backgrounds for gold, brass, and chrome details. Use jewel tones on accent walls, upholstery, and accessories while keeping larger surfaces in sophisticated neutrals.

Statement Furniture and Fixtures

Furniture in an Art Deco rental should make a statement. Channel-tufted velvet sofas, geometric side tables, mirrored surfaces, and streamlined silhouettes define the style. Look for pieces with curved fronts, stepped profiles, and lacquered finishes that reference the era.

The bedroom is where Art Deco truly shines. An upholstered headboard in velvet with geometric quilting, mirrored nightstands, a vanity with a round mirror, and bedside pendants in brass and glass create a glamorous sleeping environment. Bedding should be luxurious with high-thread-count sheets and a velvet or silk throw.

Bathroom design benefits from black and white tile patterns, geometric-patterned floor tiles, gold-toned fixtures and hardware, and a vanity with Art Deco-inspired proportions. A framed mirror with a geometric or sunburst design anchors the space.

Lighting and Metallic Accents

Lighting is perhaps the most impactful element in Art Deco design. Geometric chandeliers, frosted glass sconces, tiered pendants, and table lamps with glass or marble bases create the glamorous atmosphere the style demands. Every room should have at least one standout lighting fixture.

Metallic accents appear throughout Art Deco interiors. Brass, gold, and chrome finishes on hardware, frames, trays, and accessories add the glimmer and luxury that define the era. A large sunburst mirror, a brass bar cart, or a geometric metal sculpture can serve as a room's focal point.

Making Art Deco Work for Rentals

The key to profitable Art Deco rental design is investing in statement pieces while keeping backgrounds relatively neutral. This approach creates maximum visual impact while remaining practical to maintain. Focus your budget on a few signature items per room and let them do the heavy lifting.

Art Deco properties photograph dramatically, creating listing images that stand out immediately in search results. The bold patterns and metallic accents catch the eye and communicate luxury without requiring a single word of description.
