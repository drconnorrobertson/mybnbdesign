STR Design Trends 2026

Published 2026-10-02

The short-term rental industry has evolved dramatically, and 2026 is proving to be a watershed year for interior design in STR properties. Hosts who stay ahead of design trends consistently outperform their competitors in bookings, reviews, and nightly rates. This year, the trends are all about creating spaces that feel both elevated and authentic, blending comfort with visual storytelling that photographs beautifully and lives even better.

Earthy Warmth and Natural Materials

The dominant palette of 2026 centers on warm earth tones layered with natural textures. Think terracotta, warm clay, and sun-bleached linen paired with raw wood, stone, and woven elements. Guests are drawn to spaces that feel grounded and connected to nature. This means replacing cool grays and stark whites with warmer alternatives like creamy ivories, soft taupes, and muted ochres. Natural materials such as travertine, rattan, and reclaimed wood add depth without overwhelming the space.

Curved and Organic Shapes

Sharp angles and rigid lines are giving way to softer, more organic forms throughout STR interiors. Arched doorways, rounded sofas, oval dining tables, and curved headboards create a sense of flow and comfort that guests find irresistible. These organic shapes photograph exceptionally well, creating visual interest without clutter. The psychological effect is notable: curved forms reduce stress and create a feeling of safety and relaxation that aligns perfectly with the vacation mindset.

Intentional Lighting Design

Lighting has become one of the most powerful design tools in the STR toolkit for 2026. Hosts are moving beyond the standard overhead fixture and embracing layered lighting strategies. This includes sculptural pendant lights as focal points, warm-toned LED strips for ambient glow, and statement table lamps that double as art objects. The key is creating multiple lighting zones that allow guests to shift the mood from bright and functional to soft and intimate with ease.

Textured Walls and Dimensional Surfaces

Flat painted walls are becoming a thing of the past in high-performing STR properties. In 2026, hosts are embracing limewash finishes, textured plaster, board and batten paneling, and even subtle wallpaper in strategic locations. These dimensional surfaces add visual richness and photograph with beautiful depth. Limewash in particular has surged in popularity because it creates an Old World elegance that feels simultaneously modern and timeless.

Locally Sourced Art and Decor

Generic mass-produced art is being replaced by locally sourced pieces that tell a story about the property's location. This includes work from regional artists, handcrafted ceramics from local studios, and vintage finds that reflect the area's heritage. Guests increasingly value authenticity and are more likely to leave positive reviews when the design feels connected to place rather than appearing pulled from a catalog. This approach also differentiates your listing from competitors who rely on identical Amazon decor packages.

Outdoor Living Spaces as Design Priority

The outdoor areas of an STR property are no longer afterthoughts. In 2026, the most successful hosts are treating patios, decks, and gardens as fully designed rooms with comfortable seating, proper lighting, dining areas, and decorative elements. Weather-resistant furniture has become increasingly stylish, with options that rival indoor pieces in both comfort and aesthetics. String lights, outdoor rugs, fire features, and container gardens create spaces where guests naturally want to spend their time and capture shareable moments.

Making These Trends Work for Your Property

The beauty of 2026 design trends is their accessibility. You do not need a complete renovation to incorporate these elements. Start with the highest-impact changes: swap out lighting fixtures, add textured throw pillows in warm tones, introduce a statement piece of local art, and invest in quality outdoor seating. Each of these changes can be implemented in a weekend and will immediately elevate your listing photos and guest experience. The properties that perform best are those that feel intentional, warm, and uniquely connected to their setting.
