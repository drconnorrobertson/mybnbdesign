How to Get 5-Star Reviews Through Design

By MyBnBDesign Team • October 02, 2026

Designing Your Way to Consistent 5-Star Reviews

Five-star reviews are the currency of short term rental success. They improve your search ranking, build guest trust, and allow you to charge premium rates with confidence. While many factors contribute to review scores, interior design is the most controllable and impactful element within your power to optimize.

Guests form their overall impression of a stay within the first five minutes of arrival. That impression is overwhelmingly shaped by the visual and sensory experience of entering the space. A well-designed interior creates an immediate positive reaction that carries through the entire stay and ultimately influences the review.

The Arrival Experience

Design the entry experience with the same care a hotel puts into its lobby. The first thing guests see sets the tone for everything that follows. Ensure the entryway is clean, well-lit, and welcoming. A console table with a mirror, a place to set bags, and a small welcome display creates an organized first impression.

The initial view from the entry into the main living space should reveal the best of your property. Arrange furniture so the most attractive angle is what guests see first. Clear sightlines to statement pieces, large windows, or architectural features draw guests deeper into the space with anticipation.

Photography Spots That Generate Social Proof

Design intentional moments throughout the property that guests will want to photograph and share. A perfectly styled bed, a reading nook with natural light, a dining table set for a meal, or a balcony view framed by plants all become organic marketing when guests share them on social media.

These photographable moments also translate directly into review content. Guests who take photos of your space are more engaged with the experience and more likely to leave detailed, positive reviews. They often include their own photos in reviews, providing authentic visual proof that reinforces your listing images.

Comfort Details That Reviews Mention by Name

Certain design elements appear in five-star reviews with remarkable consistency. Premium bedding is mentioned more than any other single feature. Guests notice and comment on the quality of sheets, the comfort of pillows, and the weight and warmth of duvets. This is your single highest-impact investment.

Bathroom amenities rank second. Quality towels, a well-lit vanity area, a rainfall showerhead, and premium toiletries generate specific positive mentions. Stock full-size products rather than hotel miniatures. Include extras like bathrobes and hair dryers prominently displayed rather than hidden in closets.

Kitchen design and equipment appear frequently in positive reviews, especially for longer stays. A well-organized kitchen with quality cookware, sharp knives, and essential pantry staples shows guests you anticipated their needs. Clear labeling of cabinets and drawers reduces frustration and confusion.

Consistency Is the Secret Ingredient

The properties that maintain consistently high review scores share one common trait: design consistency throughout every room and every detail. When quality is uniform from the living room to the bathroom to the kitchen to the closets, guests perceive the entire experience as premium.

Inconsistency is a review killer. A gorgeous living room paired with a poorly equipped kitchen, or a luxurious bed in a room with stained carpet, creates a jarring disconnect that guests always mention negatively. Audit every room and every detail through the eyes of a paying guest.
