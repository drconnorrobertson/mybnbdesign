Trending STR Colors 2026

Published on October 02, 2026 by MyBnbDesign Team

The Color Trends Shaping Short-Term Rentals in 2026

The short-term rental industry moves fast, and design trends directly impact booking performance. Properties that look current attract more clicks, more bookings, and higher nightly rates. The color trends for 2026 reflect a broader shift toward warmth, nature, and authenticity. Guests are tired of sterile, all-gray interiors and are gravitating toward spaces that feel alive, personal, and connected to the natural world.

Staying current with color trends does not mean repainting every year. It means making strategic updates that signal freshness. Sometimes that means a new wall color, but often it means updating accent pieces, textiles, and accessories to reflect current palettes. Here are the colors defining STR design in 2026.

Top Trending Colors for 2026

Butter Yellow

Soft butter yellow is the breakout color of 2026. After years of cool grays and stark whites, guests are craving warmth, and butter yellow delivers it in spades. Use it on a feature wall in the kitchen or living room, or introduce it through upholstery and textiles. Benjamin Moore Hawthorne Yellow HC-4 is a sophisticated option that avoids looking childish. Pair it with warm wood, brass, and creamy whites for a sunny, optimistic feel.

Warm Olive

Olive green continues its dominance from 2025, evolving into warmer, more yellow-green variations in 2026. This earthy tone creates a sophisticated backdrop that works with both modern and traditional design. It pairs beautifully with natural materials and warm metallics. Use it in living rooms and bedrooms for a grounding effect. Sherwin-Williams Messenger Bag SW 7740 captures this trend perfectly.

Rust and Terracotta

Warm earth tones continue to trend strongly, with rust and terracotta leading the way. These colors bring warmth and personality to any space. Use them in accent pieces, tile work, or a bold accent wall. They pair naturally with the warm neutrals that dominate rental design. These colors are particularly effective in desert, southwestern, and Mediterranean-style properties but translate well to any market.

Mushroom and Taupe

The new neutrals are moving away from gray toward warmer, more organic tones. Mushroom, taupe, and warm putty colors are replacing cool grays as the go-to neutral. These colors have brown undertones that create warmth without adding obvious color. They work as a whole-house palette that feels current and sophisticated. Benjamin Moore Revere Pewter HC-172 and Sherwin-Williams Shiitake SW 9173 are excellent options.

Moody Plum

For hosts willing to push boundaries, moody plum and deep berry tones are emerging as the new accent wall color. These rich, complex colors create a luxurious atmosphere that photographs dramatically. Use them sparingly in bedrooms or dining areas. Farrow and Ball Brinjal No.222 is a stunning example. Pair with warm metallics, velvet textures, and warm lighting to bring out the depth.

Colors Falling Out of Trend

Cool gray is officially over as a dominant rental color. While it served the industry well for years, it now reads as dated and impersonal. Stark white without warm accents is similarly falling out of favor. Millennial pink, once ubiquitous, has faded from relevance. And farmhouse blue-gray, the Joanna Gaines signature, is transitioning out as the farmhouse trend evolves into something more refined.

The overall direction is clear. Guests in 2026 want warmth, personality, and connection to nature. Whether you adopt these specific trending colors or simply shift your palette warmer, aligning with this direction will keep your property feeling fresh and desirable. Focus on timeless warm tones and update your accents with trendier pieces that are easy to swap as styles evolve.
