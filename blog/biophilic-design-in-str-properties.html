Biophilic Design in STR Properties

Published 2026-10-02

Biophilic design, the practice of incorporating natural elements and nature-inspired patterns into interior spaces, has become one of the most impactful design strategies for short-term rental properties. Research consistently shows that humans have an innate need to connect with nature, and spaces that satisfy this need reduce stress, improve mood, and create memorable experiences. For STR hosts, biophilic design translates directly into higher guest satisfaction scores, better reviews, and increased booking demand.

The Science Behind the Strategy

The effectiveness of biophilic design is not just anecdotal. Studies from environmental psychology demonstrate that exposure to natural elements reduces cortisol levels, lowers blood pressure, and improves cognitive function. When guests enter a space filled with natural light, living plants, natural materials, and views of greenery, their bodies physically relax. This biological response creates a powerful positive association with your property that shows up in reviews using words like peaceful, relaxing, rejuvenating, and exactly what we needed. These are the review keywords that drive future bookings.

Natural Light as Foundation

The most impactful biophilic element costs nothing: natural light. Maximizing daylight in your STR should be the first priority. Remove heavy curtains and replace them with sheer linen panels that filter light softly. Clean windows thoroughly and regularly. Trim exterior vegetation that blocks light from entering rooms. Position furniture to take advantage of natural light patterns, and use mirrors strategically to bounce light deeper into the space. Light-colored walls and ceilings amplify the effect, creating rooms that feel open, airy, and naturally energizing during the day.

Living Elements and Indoor Greenery

Indoor plants are the most recognizable element of biophilic design, and they deliver outsized returns in STR properties. The key is selecting species that thrive with minimal care and survive the variable attention of different guests. Snake plants, ZZ plants, pothos, and rubber plants are all excellent choices that tolerate inconsistent watering. Place larger plants in corners to soften hard angles, medium plants on shelves and counters to add life, and small succulents in bathrooms and bedrooms. The visual warmth and air-purifying benefits make plants one of the best investments per dollar in STR design.

Natural Materials Throughout

Biophilic design extends well beyond plants to encompass the fundamental materials used throughout the space. Wood, stone, clay, linen, cotton, wool, and jute all carry the visual and tactile qualities of the natural world. Replace synthetic materials wherever possible with natural alternatives. A stone soap dish in the bathroom, wooden cutting boards displayed in the kitchen, linen bedding in the bedroom, and a jute rug in the living area. Each natural material adds warmth and authenticity while reinforcing the connection to nature that guests instinctively seek.

Water Features and Sound

The sound of water is one of the most calming natural elements, and incorporating it into an STR can be surprisingly simple. A small tabletop fountain in the living area or bedroom creates gentle ambient sound that masks street noise and promotes relaxation. If your property has outdoor space, a simple recirculating water feature adds both visual and auditory appeal. Even without physical water features, the biophilic principle of natural sound can be addressed through window placement that captures birdsong or the sound of rain, wind chimes on a covered porch, or a curated playlist of nature sounds available through a smart speaker.

Nature-Inspired Patterns and Forms

Biophilic design also encompasses patterns and shapes found in nature. Organic curves, branching forms, fractal patterns, and natural color variations can be introduced through artwork, textiles, and architectural details. A botanical print on the wall, a throw pillow with a leaf motif, or a pendant light with an organic shape all reference the natural world without requiring living elements. These pattern references work at a subconscious level, creating visual complexity and interest that keeps the eye engaged without creating clutter or overwhelm.

Bringing It All Together

The beauty of biophilic design for STR properties is its scalability. You can start small with a few well-placed plants and natural material swaps, or go all-in with a comprehensive biophilic renovation. The impact is cumulative: each natural element you add reinforces the overall effect. Begin with the highest-impact changes, including maximizing natural light, adding three to five low-maintenance plants, and replacing one synthetic element per room with a natural alternative. Document the changes with fresh listing photos that showcase the natural elements prominently. The guest response will be immediate and measurable in both reviews and booking velocity.
