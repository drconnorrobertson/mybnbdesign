Mediterranean Design for Short Term Rentals

By MyBnBDesign Team • October 02, 2026

Mediterranean Design: Sun-Soaked Luxury for Your STR

Mediterranean design brings the warmth, texture, and relaxed elegance of Southern European and North African coastal living into your short term rental. This style creates an immediate sense of escape, transporting guests to sun-drenched villas and seaside retreats regardless of your property's actual location. For rental owners, Mediterranean design offers a distinctive aesthetic that commands premium pricing.

The style draws from centuries of architectural and decorative tradition, resulting in spaces that feel rich with history and cultural depth. Guests respond to this sense of timelessness, often describing Mediterranean-styled rentals as romantic, luxurious, and unforgettable in their reviews.

The Mediterranean Palette

Warm, sun-baked tones define the Mediterranean palette. Start with walls in warm white, soft cream, or pale terracotta. Layer in accent colors drawn from the landscape: deep olive green, azure blue, burnt sienna, and golden yellow. These are colors that exist naturally in Mediterranean climates and feel authentic and harmonious together.

Avoid the temptation to over-saturate. The most elegant Mediterranean interiors use rich colors strategically, perhaps in a tiled backsplash, an accent wall, or a collection of ceramic pieces, while keeping larger surfaces in warm, light neutrals.

Essential Materials and Surfaces

Natural materials are fundamental to Mediterranean design. Terra cotta tile flooring, stone countertops, plaster walls with subtle texture, and wrought iron details create the structural framework. Wooden ceiling beams, whether structural or decorative, add architectural character and warmth.

If your property lacks these built-in features, focus on achievable updates. Terra cotta-look porcelain tiles, textured wall finishes, and wrought iron light fixtures and hardware can transform a standard space. A decorative tile accent in the kitchen or bathroom creates an authentic focal point.

Furniture and Textiles

Mediterranean furniture tends toward substantial, solid pieces with carved details and weathered finishes. A heavy wooden dining table, an upholstered armchair with nailhead trim, and a carved wooden bed frame anchor the major rooms. Balance heavier pieces with lighter elements like rush-seated chairs and rattan accessories.

Textiles should feel luxurious but relaxed. Linen is the signature fabric of Mediterranean living. Use it for curtains, bedding, and table settings. Layer with cotton throws in warm colors and include cushions in rich fabrics like velvet or embroidered cotton.

Outdoor living is central to the Mediterranean lifestyle. If your property has outdoor space, invest in quality patio furniture, outdoor dining, and lush container plantings. String lights and lanterns extend the usable hours and create magical evening atmospheres.

Decorative Details That Complete the Look

Ceramic and pottery are signature Mediterranean accessories. Hand-painted tiles, artisan bowls, olive jars, and ceramic vases add color and cultural authenticity. Display fresh lemons, olive branches, or herbs as natural decorative elements that also serve a practical purpose.

Wrought iron details throughout the space tie everything together. Curtain rods, towel bars, candle holders, and mirror frames in iron or bronze create a consistent material thread that reinforces the Mediterranean vocabulary.
