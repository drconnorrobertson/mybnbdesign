Outdoor Room Design Trend for STR

Published 2026-10-02

The outdoor room has evolved from a nice-to-have amenity into one of the most important booking drivers for short-term rental properties. Guests increasingly prioritize outdoor living space when selecting vacation rentals, and properties with thoughtfully designed outdoor rooms consistently outperform those with neglected or afterthought patios. The outdoor room trend treats exterior spaces with the same design intention applied to interiors, creating fully realized living environments that extend the usable square footage of your property and provide the experiences guests are willing to pay premium rates to enjoy.

Defining the Outdoor Room Concept

An outdoor room is more than a patio with furniture. It is a defined space with clear boundaries, comfortable furnishings, lighting, decorative elements, and a sense of purpose. Just as an interior room has walls, a ceiling, and a floor, an outdoor room uses elements like pergolas, landscape hedging, outdoor curtains, overhead structures, and defined flooring to create enclosure without blocking fresh air and views. The space should feel like a room that happens to be outside rather than outdoor furniture placed on a deck. This distinction is what separates properties that guests describe as having amazing outdoor space from those where the patio goes unmentioned in reviews.

Comfortable Seating as the Foundation

The single most important investment in an outdoor room is comfortable seating. Weather-resistant outdoor furniture has improved dramatically in recent years, with options that rival indoor pieces in both comfort and aesthetics. Deep-seated outdoor sofas with plush all-weather cushions, lounge chairs with proper support, and dining chairs that guests can sit in comfortably for hours are the foundation of a successful outdoor room. Avoid the hard, minimally cushioned metal furniture that many hosts default to. Guests will not use outdoor seating that is not genuinely comfortable, regardless of how beautiful the view may be.

Outdoor Dining Experiences

An outdoor dining area is one of the most photographed and praised features in STR reviews. Create a proper dining setup with a table sized for your guest capacity, comfortable chairs, and enough clearance for easy movement. Add overhead lighting, whether string lights, a pendant fixture on a covered patio, or lanterns, so the dining experience extends into evening. Provide outdoor-safe tableware and a serving surface for food preparation. The goal is creating a space where guests naturally choose to eat outside because the environment is more appealing than the indoor dining area. In warm-weather markets, outdoor dining can be the defining feature of your entire listing.

Shade and Weather Protection

Usable outdoor rooms require protection from sun, rain, and wind. Pergolas with climbing plants or shade cloth provide filtered light and architectural definition. Market umbrellas offer flexible shade that can be repositioned throughout the day. Retractable awnings provide on-demand coverage. Outdoor curtains on a covered patio create privacy, wind protection, and a sense of enclosure that makes the space feel intimate and room-like. For maximum versatility, layer shade solutions so guests can adjust their environment. Properties in hot climates should prioritize shade aggressively, as outdoor spaces without it go unused during peak daytime hours.

Outdoor Lighting Design

Lighting transforms outdoor spaces from daytime amenities into evening destinations. The most effective approach layers multiple lighting types. String lights or festoon bulbs provide ambient glow and photograph beautifully. Path lights ensure safe movement after dark. Uplighting on trees or architectural features creates drama. Lanterns and candles add warmth and intimacy. Solar-powered options reduce operating costs and eliminate the need for electrical installation. The goal is creating an outdoor environment that is as inviting after sunset as it is during the day, effectively doubling the usable hours of your outdoor space and providing magical evening experiences that guests consistently rave about in reviews.

Fire Features and Gathering Points

A fire feature serves as a natural gathering point that extends outdoor seasons and creates memorable experiences. Gas fire pits offer easy operation with instant on and off, while wood-burning options provide the crackle and aroma that many guests prefer. Tabletop fire bowls offer a smaller-scale option for compact spaces. Position fire features as the focal point of a seating arrangement, creating a circular or semicircular layout that encourages conversation and connection. Provide blankets or throws for cooler evenings. The presence of a fire feature in listing photos signals comfort, atmosphere, and the promise of memorable evenings, which is exactly what drives booking decisions.

Building Your Outdoor Room

Start with the highest-impact elements: comfortable seating and lighting. These two components alone can transform a bare patio into a compelling outdoor room. Add a dining setup if space allows. Introduce plants in containers to soften edges and add greenery. Consider a shade solution that defines the space overhead. Finally, add a fire feature or water element as a focal point. Each addition increases the usability and appeal of the space. Update your listing photos to showcase the outdoor room prominently, including evening shots that show the lighting at its best. Properties with well-photographed outdoor rooms see measurable increases in booking inquiries, and guests who experience these spaces consistently highlight them as the best part of their stay.
