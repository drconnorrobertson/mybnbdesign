DIY Airbnb Design vs Hiring a Professional: The Complete Guide

Expert guide comparing DIY and professional approaches to vacation rental interior design with actionable tips.

By MyBnBDesign TeamSeptember 23, 2026DIY Guides

The DIY vs Professional Decision

Every host faces whether to design themselves or hire help. The answer depends on budget, timeline, design confidence, and market competitiveness.

Advantages of DIY Design

DIY saves money on professional fees ($2,000 to $15,000). You maintain complete control and develop skills for future properties. Online resources provide unprecedented access to professional-level inspiration.

When Professional Help Pays Off

Professionals bring trained eyes, vendor relationships, and cross-property experience. For premium properties targeting $250+ nightly rates, professional design almost always delivers positive ROI.

DIY Design Resources and Tools

Start with Pinterest and Instagram for inspiration. Use Canva or RoomSketcher for floor planning. Download the IKEA app for AR furniture placement. Join vacation rental host communities on Facebook.

Hybrid Approach: Best of Both Worlds

Hire a designer for a consultation ($200 to $500) to create a plan and shopping list, then execute it yourself. You get professional guidance while controlling purchasing.

Making Your Decision

Evaluate based on budget, design confidence, and market positioning. Budget properties succeed with thoughtful DIY. Premium properties benefit from professional guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I hire a professional to design my Airbnb?

Professional design makes sense for properties targeting $250+ nightly rates or in highly competitive markets.

How much does professional Airbnb design cost?

Ranges from $200 to $500 for a consultation, $1,500 to $5,000 for a virtual package, and $5,000 to $25,000 for full-service.

Can I design my Airbnb with no experience?

Yes. Study top-performing listings, choose a cohesive color palette, invest in quality over quantity, and follow sizing guidelines.
