Our Story

We Design Rentals That
Outperform Their Market

MyBnBDesign started with a simple observation -- the short-term rentals that earn the most are not just well-located. They are well-designed. Guests book properties that feel intentional, that photograph well, and that deliver something hotels cannot.

We bring the kind of design thinking you see in boutique hotels and editorial interiors to the short-term rental market. Every project begins with understanding the property, the local market, and the revenue goals -- then we design to hit them.

As a BnB Accelerator company, we sit at the crossroads of real estate investment and interior design -- helping property owners build spaces that work as hard as they do.
