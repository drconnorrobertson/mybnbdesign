Pricing FAQ

Clear answers about design packages, furnishing budgets, payment terms, and what to expect from your investment. Book a free consultation to discuss pricing for your property.

25 Questions Answered

How much does MyBnBDesign cost?

Pricing depends on property size, number of rooms, and the design tier you choose. Our design fees are separate from furniture and decor costs, so you can see exactly what you pay for each part. After a free consultation we provide a written proposal with fixed fees and an itemized furnishing budget.

What design packages do you offer?

We offer three tiers: Essentials for budget-conscious launches, Signature for a fully custom look, and Luxury for high-end, designer-level properties. Each tier increases the level of customization, sourcing effort, and styling detail. We will recommend the best fit during your consultation.

What is included in the Essentials package?

Essentials includes a design consultation, a curated concept, a room-by-room furniture list from our vetted catalog, procurement, delivery, and installation. It is built for owners who want a polished, guest-ready home efficiently. Custom sourcing and bespoke pieces are not included at this tier.

What is included in the Signature package?

Signature adds a fully custom concept, expanded mood boards, wider sourcing options, custom decor curation, and a styling day. You get more revision rounds and a more distinctive final look. It is our most popular tier for owners who want to stand out in a competitive market.

What is included in the Luxury package?

Luxury includes everything in Signature plus custom or made-to-order pieces, premium brands, art curation, and white-glove project management. We also provide extended styling and photo-day coordination. It is designed for high-end properties targeting premium nightly rates.

Are furniture costs included in the design fee?

No. Design fees cover our services, while furniture, decor, and supplies are billed at cost based on your approved budget. This keeps pricing transparent and ensures you only pay for what you choose. You approve the budget before any purchase is made.

How much should I budget for furniture?

Budgets vary widely by property size and finish level. As a general guide, owners commonly plan a per-bedroom budget that increases with the quality tier, plus a separate allowance for kitchen, linens, and outdoor spaces. We provide a precise estimate after reviewing your floor plan and goals.

Can you give me a cost breakdown by room?

Yes. Every proposal includes a room-by-room breakdown covering furniture, lighting, rugs, decor, linens, and supplies. You can see where your money is going and adjust individual rooms up or down. This makes it easy to prioritize high-impact spaces like the living room and primary bedroom.

Do you take a markup on furniture?

We are transparent about how we are compensated. Our proposal states whether we pass through trade pricing at cost or apply a procurement fee, so there are no surprises. Any procurement fee is disclosed in your written agreement before you sign.

What is the deposit to get started?

We typically require a design deposit to reserve your spot and begin concept work, with the amount outlined in your proposal. The deposit is applied toward your total design fee. Furnishing funds are collected separately before purchasing begins.

How does the payment schedule work?

Design fees are generally split into milestones such as deposit, concept approval, and installation. Furniture funds are collected before orders are placed so vendors can be paid on time. All amounts and dates are listed in your agreement.

Do you offer payment plans?

For larger projects we can discuss milestone-based payment schedules, and some clients choose third-party financing for furnishing costs. Availability depends on project size and terms. Ask during your consultation and we will explain the options.

What is your refund policy?

If you cancel before concept work begins, your deposit may be refunded minus any administrative costs, per your agreement. Once design work is underway, fees for completed work are non-refundable. Furniture already ordered cannot be refunded once manufacturing or shipping has started, though we help with cancellations when possible.

What if I cancel after furniture is ordered?

Many vendors allow cancellations only within a short window, and custom pieces are usually final sale. We will contact vendors on your behalf and try to cancel or reroute orders to minimize loss. Any non-refundable costs will be explained before you decide.

Are there hidden fees?

No. Our proposal lists design fees, furnishing costs, shipping, delivery, installation, and any applicable taxes. If something outside the original scope comes up, we will request your approval before incurring added cost. Transparency is a core part of how we work.

Are shipping and delivery included?

Shipping and freight are itemized in your furnishing budget because they vary by vendor and location. We consolidate deliveries wherever possible to reduce costs. Installation labor is quoted separately or included depending on your tier.

Is sales tax included in estimates?

Estimates show product costs and note taxes separately, since sales tax depends on your property location and the vendor. Your final invoice reflects the actual tax charged. We will flag this early so you can budget accurately.

Do you offer budget-friendly options?

Yes. Our Essentials tier and budget-conscious sourcing let you launch with a polished look at a lower investment. We focus spending on high-impact areas like the primary bedroom, living room, and hero photo spaces. Lower-cost pieces are still selected for durability in guest use.

What do luxury options look like?

Luxury projects can include designer furniture, custom upholstery, curated art, premium mattresses, and high-end outdoor setups such as hot tub areas and fire pits. These elements can support higher nightly rates and standout listings. We will tie each upgrade to the guest experience it creates.

Where should I spend more and where can I save?

Spend more on mattresses, seating, lighting, and the main living space, because guests notice comfort and photos drive bookings. Save on secondary bedrooms, decor accents, and items guests rarely use. We build the budget around this principle.

What return on investment should I expect?

Returns depend on your market, pricing, and management, so we avoid promising a specific number. Well-designed STRs commonly see improved occupancy, better reviews, and the ability to charge higher rates, which helps recoup furnishing costs over time. We can walk through a simple payback estimate during your consultation.

How long does it take to pay back my design investment?

Payback varies by property and market, but many owners evaluate it by comparing added nightly revenue and occupancy to their total furnishing cost. A realistic estimate requires your expected rates and occupancy. We can help you model best-case and conservative scenarios.

Do you price match?

If you receive a comparable written quote for identical products, we will review it and try to match or beat it when possible. Price matching applies to like-for-like items and does not cover clearance or used goods. Ask your project lead before ordering.

Do you offer seasonal pricing or promotions?

We sometimes offer promotions around major retail sales events when vendors discount heavily, and we pass those savings along. Scheduling your purchases during sale periods can reduce furnishing costs. Ask us about current offers when you request a consultation.

Do you offer discounts for multiple properties?

Yes. Owners with multiple properties often qualify for bulk discounts through consolidated purchasing and repeatable design packages. The more units you furnish, the more we can streamline. Contact us with your portfolio size for a custom quote.
