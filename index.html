BEFORE WE BEGIN
Can you help with an existing rental?

Yes. Start with current photos, guest feedback and an inventory of what you want to keep. Discuss whether a targeted refresh or a broader redesign makes sense for your property.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Your property location, floor plan or room dimensions, recent photos, approximate furnishing budget and target opening date.

How much will my project cost?

Costs depend on property size, furniture inventory, design scope and installation logistics. Agree on a written scope and separate design, furnishings and delivery costs before committing.

Do you work in my market?

Share your location with the team to confirm service availability, logistics and the right scope for your project.
