Entries and arrival spaces: STR design workbooks

Study 9 design elements across nineteen practical decisions, with specific evidence cards and editable property records.

Published October 13, 2026 · MyBnBDesign

Choose an element, then the unresolved decision. Each element uses the same nineteen-stage lifecycle so the task and supporting records remain clear. Component-specific evidence changes the application; the shared protocols are identified on each workbook.

9 elements

Entry bench

Provide a deliberate place to sit and handle footwear without turning the arrival route into storage.

Evidence to begin with: Record bench depth, door swing, usable seat area and the location of bags while someone sits.

Use the reference with the project roadmap

Begin with the earliest unresolved decision for the element. Keep the actual site, product and service evidence together and assign open questions to a person who can answer them. The workbooks organize an owner’s process and are not universal product specifications or professional certifications.
