STR DESIGN REFERENCE · COMPONENT WORKBOOK

The STR design reference: 128 elements and 2,432 decision workbooks

A complete component lifecycle index for STR owners: planning, fit, selection, purchasing, installation, care, replacement and records.

Published October 13, 2026 · MyBnBDesign

The STR design reference covers 128 elements in fourteen areas, from arrival to long-term operation. Each element has nineteen distinct decision workbooks: brief, survey, specification, trial, cost, quote scope, receiving, installation, commissioning, reset, care, support, faults, substitutions, refreshes, photography, provider terms, financial records and operating review.

Editorial method: These are structured instructional workbooks. They combine component-specific evidence with openly shared lifecycle protocols and original hypothetical exercises. They are not 2,432 independent field studies, product tests or search-demand findings. Use actual property facts, exact product instructions and appropriate professional review.

For narrative lessons and practical design labs, visit the owner’s design manual. For project order, follow the twelve-stage roadmap.

Work from evidence to a usable decision

Find the relevant element and choose the decision you need to resolve. Use its evidence card to identify actual site facts, product or service requirements, an ordinary-use test and source records. Complete the worksheet with verified information, leave uncertainty visible and download the record before leaving the page. Then continue to the next lifecycle decision.

The workbooks do not predict revenue, certify compliance or determine tax treatment. Relevant financial-record discussions link to AE Tax Advisors and Stratum, while professional conclusions remain with the actual advisers and providers.
