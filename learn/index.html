THE STR OWNER’S DESIGN MANUAL

STR design education: the owner’s design manual

Study detailed rental design lessons with original workflows, property examples, practical labs and downloadable worksheets.

Published October 13, 2026 · MyBnBDesign editorial team

Use the design manual to resolve the work behind an attractive listing: room functions, guest activities, care, measurements, installation, operating standards and clear property information. Each lesson includes an original step-by-step explanation, a worked hypothetical example, a practical lab and an editable worksheet.

For the sequence of a whole project, begin with the twelve-stage design roadmap. For a specific problem, search the lessons below or explore the detailed planning library.

How to use the worksheets

Collect the actual site and product evidence before making a decision. Write down unresolved questions instead of entering guesses, assign a reviewer and keep the accepted revision with the property handover. The worksheets organize your work; they do not certify compliance, establish tax treatment or predict rental returns.
