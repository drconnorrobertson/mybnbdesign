Choosing an Airbnb interior designer

If you plan to order and install furnishings yourself, ask for layouts, product selections, and the information you’ll need to manage substitutions. If you want a full-service setup, ask the designer to identify who handles purchasing, receiving, installation, styling, and any work left after handover. MyBnBDesign’s proposal spells out which tasks are included for your property.

If you’ll manage the project from another city, use the out-of-state furnishing checklist to plan site access, approvals, and installation handover.

What goes into an Airbnb design and furnishing quote?

Share the property location, room count, floor plan or photos, target opening date, and pieces you plan to keep. Ask for a scope that separates design deliverables, furniture and materials, and on-site setup so you can compare quotes with the same starting assumptions.

  • Design: Confirm which rooms get layouts and specifications, how revisions work, and who approves substitutions.
  • Furnishing: Ask who places orders, tracks lead times, handles returns, and pays freight or receiving fees.
  • Installation and styling: Confirm the site access window and whether assembly, packaging removal, and photography staging are included.

See completed project photos or use the rental furnishing cost guide to budget before you request a scope. For design fees and a sample budget, see the Airbnb interior design pricing FAQ.

What to confirm in your design proposal

A useful proposal connects your design decisions to the work at the property. Confirm each responsibility before paying a deposit or scheduling photography.

Design and budget

Agree on the rooms, retained furniture, target guest, layout deliverables and revision process. Separate the design fee from furniture, freight, tax, receiving, assembly and contingency.

Purchasing and installation

Identify who approves substitutions, tracks orders, receives shipments, documents damage and installs each item. Confirm local availability and access requirements for your property.

Handover and launch

Set a punch-list review, an installed inventory and a photography-ready checklist. Confirm which listing, photography and operating tasks are included or separately quoted.

How much does Airbnb interior design cost?

Your quote depends on the property, rooms, existing inventory and execution scope. Use our furnishing budget tools to build a property-specific allowance, then compare complete furnishing quotes. A furniture subtotal alone does not represent the installed project cost.

Can you help with a property I manage remotely?

Bring the location, room measurements, current photos and the name of your local access contact. Confirm who verifies dimensions, accepts deliveries and checks the completed work. Agree on coverage and timing during the consultation.

What happens on the first call?

Discuss your property, budget, guest profile and launch date with BNB Accelerator. The team can clarify the available design and launch support and the next step for your project.
